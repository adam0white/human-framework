No scholarly review is expected. These are engineering choices about which understanding the framework models by default, not rulings. Hosts may configure a person's understanding differently.

## Zakat al-fitr opt-in contract (GDI-162, 2026-10-25)

This section supersedes the older “not modelled yet” row for opt-in hosts only.

- **Transmitted sources:** Bukhari 1503 and 1509 describe a food amount and payment before the Eid prayer. The source record used substitute sites; it did not directly verify sunnah.com or the Arabic. No Qur'an verse is presented here as specifying this amount. See [eid-and-mourning-sources.md](eid-and-mourning-sources.md) §2 and its verification limits.
- **Interpretation:** the person may hold zakat al-fitr as obligatory. The source record distinguishes the Hanafi nisab/net-needs-and-debts test from the Shafi'i provisions test. Neither is inferred from generic Person state. Hosts supply the person's eligibility assessment. Food quantities and cash equivalents differ by interpretation; the recorded Diyanet 240 TL figure applies only to its stated 2026–2027 period. There is no universal cash amount. Diyanet says missed payment remains owed at the first opportunity.
- **Engineering (fix1):** the explicit opt-in helper requires a held norm, host-assessed eligibility, a positive amount in host resource units, an opening minute and a preferred before-prayer deadline. It returns two separate commitments: `commitment` is a norm-free appointment reminder ending at the preferred deadline; `payment` is a duty linked to the held norm, open from the supplied opening through `MAX_MINUTE`, the framework's finite timestamp horizon, not a religious deadline. Both explicitly use `completion: 'end'`: only a confirmed full-payment outcome whose completion minute is inside the window can keep it. Beginning a transfer is not a receipt. Hosts adopt both with `promise` and list both adopted ids in the full-payment affordance's `fulfills`, so an early full receipt keeps both in one composite `finish`. A late full receipt keeps only payment; it cannot rewrite a missed reminder. Partial transfers use a different action and never publish full-payment completion. Hosts retain the plan and cumulative confirmed receipts; the pure reading reports the outstanding amount and elapsed preferred time. The payment duty continues to offer ordinary agenda utility after the preferred time, including after partial payment. It has no new first-opportunity detection, immediate omission veto, or religious lateness severity. Its finite horizon is a representation limit; hosts must not treat it as a discharge date. Missing the reminder retains ordinary personal-plan memory, distress and esteem cost, with no linked-norm breach. No generic missed-cost rule is suppressed. No automatic calendar date, currency conversion, recipient ruling, dependent assessment, receipt store or new Person slice is introduced. The general completion-time option is explicit; absent it, duty/prayer overlap, underway protection and will paths remain unchanged. Native Person save/restore and replay retain it. Portable calendar-persona transport is unchanged; its strict field whitelist rejects this new option, so no cross-game transport support is claimed.
- **Scope:** this represents a person's held understanding. It computes no divine acceptance, reward, purification or ruh quantity. Before/after timing is a calendar fact, not a religious verdict. Hosts keep faith gentle; no game adoption or Eid-cost change belongs to this candidate. Existing calls and the default norm catalog remain exact.


## Community death facts and attended knowledge (2026-10-25)

For the explicit community/attended mode, widowhood is a factual bond change at the declared death minute.
The waiting interval already fixed by the marriage custom starts there, even when notice is dropped or arrives later.
This follows the held interpretation in [marriage-sources.md §8](marriage-sources.md#8-widowhood): Qur'an 2:234 and Bukhari 1280 support the interval; the cited contemporary explanation places its start at death.
The interval remains 130 days as an engineering rounding, not a new ruling.
Only attended news marks a social tie as deceased and produces grief. Knowledge does not determine whether the marriage has ended.
The scheduler knows only its discovery clock; it does not infer a biological death minute within a tick.
Omitted `tellDeath` options retain the previous receipt-time behavior for exact continuation.

### HF 3.2 repair boundaries (candidate, 2026-10-26)

`warmth-through-25` uses the existing family responsiveness expression at zero warmth: 0.3 × normalized carer mood. When all warmth weights vanish, living carers count equally for attachment only. F10/F11 support responsiveness as a modeling direction; neither establishes this boundary, its coefficients or its rate. No living carers supplies no exposure. Values, norms and voice trust still receive zero warmth influence. The omitted model retains its original arithmetic.

`widowhoodMortality` reads factual widowhood status and `endedAt`, independently of heard news or grief. F20 records aggregate widowhood associations, not the effect before a spouse learns of the death. The current status-based multiplier is an engineering assumption. Whether knowledge should gate any mortality effect remains a source/model question for the later body batch (review 2026-11-15); no source here justifies changing the gate in this repair. Factual bond timing and attended grief provenance remain separate.

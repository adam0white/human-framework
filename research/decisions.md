# Decisions on contested points

Recorded 2026-10-04 on the user's direction: aim roughly for a post-sectarian, united understanding; where schools differ, take the most common position overall; decide pragmatically for a game framework and move on. No scholarly review is expected. These are engineering choices about which understanding the framework models by default, not rulings. Hosts may configure a person's understanding differently.

| Point | Decision (framework default) | Basis | Source notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| End of Fajr | Sunrise | Agreed across schools | [prayer-times-sources.md](prayer-times-sources.md) §1 |
| Start of Asr | Shadow equal to the object's length (plus the noon shadow) | Majority (Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali, and Abu Yusuf and Muhammad among Hanafis); also Diyanet's calendar | prayer-times-sources.md §1, §4 |
| End of Maghrib / start of Isha | Disappearance of the red twilight (shafaq ahmar) | Majority (Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali, and the two Hanafi companions); Abu Hanifa's white twilight is the minority | prayer-times-sources.md §2 |
| End of Isha | Preferred until midnight; still valid until true dawn | Common ground across schools (preferred time differs: a third or half the night) | prayer-times-sources.md §1 |
| Makruh times | Sunrise until about 20 minutes after, solar zenith, the last minutes before sunset; the day's own Asr may still be prayed before sunset | Common core across schools; the length after sunrise is a labelled simplification (Diyanet gives 40–50 minutes) | prayer-times-sources.md §3 |
| Smoking while fasting | Breaks the fast; the day is made up | Diyanet and Hanafi texts; widely held. Expiation (kaffara) is not modelled: disputed | [fasting-sources.md](fasting-sources.md) §1 |
| Eid al-Fitr prayer | Offered on Eid morning from shortly after sunrise until before midday, as a strongly emphasised prayer (wajib for Hanafis, sunnah mu'akkada for most others); in congregation; no individual make-up | Schools differ on status; all hold it strongly emphasised | [eid-and-mourning-sources.md](eid-and-mourning-sources.md) A |
| Missed prayers | A missed prayer leaves a make-up debt (qada). Sleep, forgetting and unconsciousness lift blame, not the debt; after loss of reason or unconsciousness lasting beyond five prayers, the debt for that stretch drops. A mental break with reason intact is accountable | Majority positions; the five-prayer threshold is Hanafi, kept as the framework default | [capacity-and-excuse-sources.md](capacity-and-excuse-sources.md) |
| Zakat al-fitr | Not modelled yet | Liability depends on debts and nisab; deferred until a game needs it | eid-and-mourning-sources.md B |
| Grave visits, first-Eid mourning | Custom, not obligation; may appear as personal practice | Custom | eid-and-mourning-sources.md 1, 3 |
| Games | Faith is quiet background texture of characters who hold it, never the theme, goal or central mechanic | User direction, 2026-10-04 | AGENTS.md |
| Consent to marriage | Both spouses' own consent is required; a guardian cannot compel (ijbar is not modelled) | Bukhari 5136 and 5138 and every contemporary source fetched point to required consent; classical schools differ on a father compelling a virgin, so the framework takes the position that also matches the hadith and modern practice | [marriage-sources.md](marriage-sources.md) §1 |
| Guardian (wali) for the bride | Default Islamic understanding: the bride's guardian consents (three of four schools; Hanafi is the named exception). A guardian who refuses unjustly does not block | Majority by school count; 2:232 and Bukhari 5130 on unjust blocking. Held only by people a host gives `MARRIAGE_NORMS`; the generic custom has no guardian requirement | marriage-sources.md §2 |
| A man's parents | A matter of obedience and good relations (birr), obligatory in some cases and not others; it does not affect validity. In the framework: a weighed voice, not a validity condition | No source treats the groom's parents' approval as a validity condition. Direction rests on contemporary fatwas only (islamqa.info 98768, 26852, quoting Ibn Taymiyya that refusing a spouse the parents choose is not disobedience), not classical school texts; the wali rules concern the bride | marriage-sources.md §2 |
| Prohibited degrees | Blood, foster and in-law degrees of 4:22–23; cousins allowed; fosterage is not modelled (no foster relation in the framework) | Qur'an 4:22–24 | marriage-sources.md §5 |
| Courtship | No seclusion (khalwa): courting is chaperoned or in company; looking at a prospective spouse is permitted | Bukhari 5233, Muslim 1341 (consensus per Nawawi); Tirmidhi 1087 | marriage-sources.md §6 |
| Marriage age | Host-supplied; default 18 | Turkish Civil Code full capacity at 18; Diyanet press report that 18 fits Islamic principles; an engineering default, not a ruling | marriage-sources.md §10 |
| Widowhood | A widow waits four months and ten days before remarrying (130 days, an engineering rounding of the lunar count); a widower has no waiting period | Qur'an 2:234, Bukhari 1280; SeekersGuidance | marriage-sources.md §8 |
| Mahr, witnesses, announcement | Host ceremony, not modelled | Agreed elements, but they add nothing to a person's decision in the framework | marriage-sources.md §3–4 |
| Divorce | Not modelled yet (deferred) | Permitted with a procedure (2:229, 65:1); deferred until a game needs it | marriage-sources.md §9 |
| Polygyny | Not modelled; `maxSpouses` 1 by default | Engineering scope choice, not a statement about the norm | — |

# Empirical approaches for a reusable human simulation

Research date: 2026-09-06. Status: an initial evidence screen and design proposal, not a systematic review, validated human model, or claim of exhaustive faculty coverage. This memo was developed independently of the earlier Gemini categories. The project's Islamic commitments constrain interpretation; the scientific literature evaluated here does not establish metaphysical claims about the soul, divine agency, moral worth, or the ultimate nature of free choice.

## What should enter the model

Build around interacting processes with multiple timescales, then expose descriptive traits as summaries. A personality inventory describes recurring differences; a learning equation proposes a process; an intervention study identifies a bounded causal effect; a grand theory attempts unification. Treating these as interchangeable would produce an elegant-looking but untestable engine.

The decisions below mean **accept as a provisional modeling ingredient**, **modify before use**, or **reject as a default empirical claim**. None means that the whole associated theory is true or false. Evidence strength belongs to individual claims, measured outcomes, and populations. Newness alone is no warrant for replacing an older replicated finding.

### 1. Embodied regulation, allostasis, and interoception — accept with separation

Represent bodily state separately from the agent's perception of that state. Interoception includes sensing and interpreting internal signals, across conscious and unconscious levels; it is not one reliable introspective meter. The interdisciplinary roadmap by Khalsa and colleagues treats its measurement and clinical interpretation as active research problems. This supports an interface between bodily dynamics, sensation, attention, and interpretation without proving any particular universal brain algorithm. [S1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29884281/)

Allostasis contributes a useful distinction between adaptive responses and accumulated costs when responses are frequent, prolonged, or poorly regulated. McEwen's synthesis describes interacting autonomic, endocrine, and immune mechanisms; it does not license a universal scalar stress-to-disease conversion. [S2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9629234/) Direct experimental support for coupling body and cognition is especially clear in sleep restriction: a randomized laboratory study found accumulating cognitive deficits whose trajectory was not faithfully reflected in subjective sleepiness. The small adult sample and controlled setting limit parameter portability. [S3](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12683469/)

**Model implication:** keep sleep pressure/circadian state, hydration, energy availability, pain/injury, physical capacity, and stress response/recovery as distinct processes where a scenario needs them. A character can feel adequately rested yet perform worse. Short-term arousal can help a task while chronic activation imposes costs. A simple game may aggregate these, but should label the aggregation and preserve an upgrade path. Endocrine, immune, reproductive, sensory, and motor detail remains an explicit extension area, not silently covered by “health.”

### 2. Motivation: SDT and Maslow — accept concurrent tensions, reject obligatory stages

Self-determination theory offers relatively well-supported constructs: autonomy, competence, and relatedness, including distinctions between need support, satisfaction, frustration, and controlled versus autonomous motivation. A 2024 meta-analysis covering 881 samples found substantial associations with well-being and smaller associations with performance. Much of this evidence is correlational; it does not establish three exhaustive biological variables or a universal numerical weighting. Autonomy means experiencing volition, and should not be implemented as compulsory individualism or rejection of legitimate obligation. [S4](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38635183/)

Maslow's later metamotivation is richer than an extra pyramid tier. His 1967 paper discusses devotion to values such as truth, justice, and beauty, acknowledges weak data and the absence of a deliberate control group, allows that people outside his selected “self-actualizing” group may show metamotivation, and says metaneeds lack a single hierarchy of precedence. It also naturalizes spiritual life. Therefore, neither its biological metaphysics nor its evaluative ranking of persons should enter by default. Retain meaning, vocation, service, excellence, and transcendence as candidate motive descriptions requiring independent conceptual and empirical scrutiny. [S5](https://www.jenniferleighselig.com/uploads/4/6/0/9/46094275/maslow--a_theory_of_metamotivation.pdf)

Across 123 countries, Tay and Diener found that associations between particular need satisfactions and well-being were largely independent of whether other needs were fulfilled. This undermines a hard lock in which an unmet “lower” need disables every “higher” motive. It remains observational evidence about well-being, not a complete test of motivational behavior. [S6](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21688922/)

**Model implication:** maintain concurrent needs, commitments, threats, aspirations, and relationships. Permit fasting, sacrifice, artistic work in poverty, and caring for others while distressed. Let bodily constraints restrict feasible action without erasing reasons for acting. Do not infer moral superiority from happiness, productivity, or need satisfaction.

### 3. Cognition, intelligence, and plasticity — modify

Separate task performance, acquired knowledge, strategies, attentional control, working-memory performance, and longer-term ability differences. Evidence that education affects cognitive test outcomes defeats a wholly fixed-capacity model: a meta-analysis of three quasiexperimental design families, exceeding 600,000 participants, estimated positive effects of additional schooling. Its reported IQ-point range is a heterogeneous intervention estimate, not an annual level-up rule; changes in test scores do not by themselves identify the underlying cognitive mechanism or establish all downstream benefits. [S7](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29911926/)

Conversely, being plastic does not mean being universally trainable. A second-order meta-analysis found reliable near transfer but little robust far transfer from studied cognitive-training programs after methodological corrections. Its authors' strongest generalization should itself be kept within the training domains examined. A 2024 synthesis still found small working-memory improvements, which is compatible with useful local plasticity and does not establish a general intelligence multiplier. [S8](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.203), [S9](https://doi.org/10.3390/jintelligence12110114)

**Model implication:** use skills and representations with sparse, explicit transfer relationships. Language knowledge may improve learning related vocabulary; strength training does not automatically improve diplomacy. Keep “current effective performance” distinct from “learned competence”: illness, distraction, tools, instruction quality, and assistance can alter the former without instantly rewriting the latter. Reject global experience points as a literal human-learning mechanism; allow them as a declared game interface.

### 4. Personality, situation, and identity — accept summaries; avoid trait essentialism

Experience-sampling research shows substantial within-person behavioral variation alongside stable differences in average tendencies. Traits-as-distributions provides a bridge: a person can be reliably more outgoing on average while behaving quietly in many situations. It is a descriptive and explanatory research program, not proof that a Big Five label causes an action. [S10](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4673017/)

The 2022 longitudinal meta-analysis supports both continuity and change in personality. Population rank stability is different from an individual's immutability; broad traits and narrow facets do not have identical stability. [S11](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35834197/) Cross-cultural measurement also matters: Laajaj and colleagues found serious validity problems for common Big Five questions in several non-WEIRD samples, with survey method and education contributing. This challenges universal use of those questionnaires, not the existence of personality differences. [S12](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6620089/)

**Model implication:** traits should parameterize context sensitivity and distributions of states, not issue commands. Store role, relationships, goals, perceived norms, memories, and opportunities alongside dispositions. A developer can use Big Five or another validated taxonomy as a view over behavior, while avoiding culture-specific item scores as universal internal anatomy. Identity and moral character require richer histories than five sliders.

### 5. Emotion and affect — retain competing explanations

Continuous valence and arousal are useful compact descriptors, but can lose the difference between anger, fear, grief, guilt, gratitude, and awe. Cowen and Keltner identified 27 distinguishable reported emotion categories with gradients between them in a particular video/self-report design. This is evidence against insisting that two dimensions exhaust reported emotional experience; it is not proof of exactly 27 universal internal modules. [S13](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1702247114)

Constructed-emotion theory instead emphasizes the interaction of bodily regulation, prior experience, categorization, and context. Its breadth is useful for generating models, while its stronger active-inference commitments remain theoretical rather than established by mentioning neuroscience. [S14](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5390700/)

**Model implication:** distinguish body signals, appraisal of events, feeling reports, action tendencies, expression, and regulation. Allow mixtures, cultural vocabularies, concealed expression, and mistaken self-interpretation. An appraisal implementation can track perceived threat, control, responsibility, loss, and goal relevance without asserting that this exhausts emotion. Keep appraisal and construction variants swappable until their predictions discriminate them.

### 6. Learning, habits, and self-control — accept local mechanisms, reject a single willpower fuel

Human sequential-choice experiments support both forward-looking and learned-value influences. The model-based/model-free reinforcement-learning distinction is useful for contrasting planning with cached action values, but a fitted two-step task is not a complete account of everyday habits, deliberation, or moral decision. [S15](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3077926/) Real-world habit research found asymptotic automaticity development with substantial individual variation; a fitted average is not a universal number of days required to establish a habit. [S16](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ejsp.674)

The resource-depletion metaphor requires stronger restraint. A preregistered 36-laboratory study found no significant effect in its confirmatory analysis; exploratory effects were small. Such results do not show that fatigue is imaginary, but they undermine treating every act of restraint as spending a known fungible willpower resource. “Ego depletion” also supplies no empirical definition of nafs. [S17](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34520296/)

**Model implication:** distinguish cue-linked automaticity, expected consequences, attentional effort, fatigue, temptation, motivation, and commitment. Permit strategies such as changing cues, seeking assistance, precommitment, and reflective reevaluation. Model competing actions rather than converting every difficulty into a lower self-control stat. Rewards can reinforce harmful or helpful habits; the learning signal is not moral approval.

### 7. Development, heredity, and aging — accept trajectories, reject fixed inheritance percentages

Heritability is a population variance statistic under particular environments and measurements. It does not say that a specified percentage of an individual is genetic, that a trait cannot change, or that a difference between populations is genetically caused. Genetic contribution and malleability are compatible. [S18](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/understanding/inheritance/heritability/)

Age-related cognition is asynchronous: large online samples and standardized-test norms show different capabilities peaking at different times. Cross-sectional age curves still mix development with cohort and selection effects; copying them as each person's destiny would be an unjustified causal inference. [S19](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25770099/) Together with the schooling and personality findings, the defensible starting point is several developmental trajectories affected by experience, condition, and opportunity.

**Model implication:** sample correlated starting characteristics and context-dependent developmental parameters with declared uncertainty. Represent learning opportunities, caregiving, health, maturation, and aging processes over time. Include individual variation and disability through changes to specific capacities, supports, and environments. Do not encode a global decline after one birthday, a genetic ceiling inferred from group averages, or automatic virtue increases with age.

### 8. Social and cultural environment — an active subsystem

An isolated person simulation omits causes that reside in institutions and relationships. Reanalysis of the randomized Moving to Opportunity housing experiment found different long-term outcomes depending on children's age at relocation, supporting the importance of exposure duration. It establishes a context-specific intervention result, not that neighborhood wealth alone is the mechanism or that identical coefficients transport to another society. [S20](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20150572)

**Model implication:** represent access to resources, family ties, trustworthy information, sanctions, institutions, social roles, and learned norms. Distinguish actual social conditions from beliefs about them. Let an agent change the environment and let other agents respond. Treat culture as learned, heterogeneous, changing practices and expectations, not an inherited package that determines every member. Religious commitment can interact with community and practice without being reduced to social conformity.

### 9. Active inference as a unifier — research option, not foundation truth

Active inference supplies a mathematically coherent family of generative-model and policy-selection methods. Its appeal is a common language for perception, learning, action, uncertainty, and information seeking. However, the 2024 empirical review reports only modest support for predictive coding and warns that active-inference work often fits behavior without sufficient formal comparison against competing models. Good fit is weaker evidence than successful discriminating prediction. [S21](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38030100/)

**Decision:** accept an optional controller that competes against simpler planning, reinforcement-learning, and rule-based models. Reject the inference that one free-energy equation is an empirically established explanation of every faculty. No controller's mathematical usefulness identifies consciousness, ruh, ultimate purpose, or freedom with its optimization objective.

## Provisional formal structure and rejection tests

The following are **engineering proposals**, not equations obtained by pooling the cited papers:

\[
x_{t+\Delta t}=F_M(x_t,a_t,e_t,d_t;\theta_M,\Delta t)+\epsilon_t,
\qquad y_t\sim O_M(x_t,e_t;\phi_M).
\]

Here \(x\) holds process states, \(a\) action, \(e\) environment, \(d\) developmental context, and \(y\) observations available to the agent. \(M\) identifies the chosen model family. Each variable needs units, timescale, valid range, and declared interpretation. Correlated noise and history dependence should be added when justified; randomness alone is not agency.

A local skill update might combine own-domain practice and feedback with a transfer matrix \(K_{ij}\), recovery, and disuse. Unsupported transfer entries should default to absent or explicitly hypothetical, not optimistic. This is preferable to a hidden universal multiplier. A need model can prioritize several discrepancies and commitments simultaneously, with scenario-specific feasibility constraints. It need not turn all reasons into one pleasure score.

Keep parameter uncertainty, measurement error, stochastic variability, and disagreement between model families separate. A simulation run can close computationally by choosing a version and seed while remaining open epistemically. Record why an agent acted and which evidence or design assumption supplied the rule.

Initial rejection tests should include: accumulated sleep restriction without proportional awareness; a hungry agent retaining a meaningful commitment; improved practiced skill without universal transfer; different conduct by the same person across settings; a supported person succeeding where the same capabilities alone fail; and lifespan trajectories with different peaks. Compare held-out behavior and intervention effects against simpler baselines. Matching a memorable story is an illustration, not validation. Detailed physiology, attachment, language, sexuality, trauma, psychopathology, consciousness, and nonhuman comparisons require dedicated evidence reviews before claiming comprehensive coverage.

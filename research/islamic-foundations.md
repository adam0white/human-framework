# Islamic foundations for a human simulation framework

Research date: 2026-09-06. Starting commitment: Islam is true; Sunni interpretation, beginning with Hanafi–Maturidi theology. Status: a researched design proposal, not a settled theological taxonomy or a claim that software reproduces a human soul. This memo was developed independently of the earlier Gemini material.

## 1. Preserve the distinction between truth, understanding, and implementation

Use five distinguishable records: revealed text; transmission/authentication; translation and interpretation; empirical finding; implementation assumption. A Qur’anic proposition, a scholar’s interpretation of it, and a numerical rule inspired by that interpretation must never become one undifferentiated “Islamic fact.” Prophetic reports also need collection, identifier, wording, and authentication provenance. The hadiths selected below are from Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim; that traditional standing does not make an English translation or our application infallible.

The user’s source hierarchy need not imply that science is an infallible book beneath revelation. Scientific observation, measurement, and explanation all involve fallible procedures. Nor can empirical findings alone determine the purpose for which a human ought to live. Qur’an 51:56 locates human purpose in worship of Allah; 17:70 accords dignity to the children of Adam. **Design inference:** retain purpose and dignity as normative commitments, while testing descriptive mechanisms against evidence. Neither dignity nor ultimate worth should depend on an intelligence, productivity, or health score. [Qur’an 51:56](https://quran.com/adh-dhariyat/56), [17:70](https://quran.com/al-isra/70).

For each claim, store its scope, interpretive school, source passage, disagreements, translation, and what would justify revising the model. Distinguish uncertainty about which interpretation is warranted from uncertainty about a character’s state. An invented probability is not a solution to either.

## 2. The faculties are not six independent containers

| Term | Textual anchor and interpretive boundary | Proposed computational treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Qalb** | Qur’an 22:46 connects hearts with reasoning and perceptual failure. It cannot be reduced to emotion opposed to reason. | An interpretive view of the person’s understanding, commitments, attention, and moral orientation; physiological heart variables remain separately named. |
| **ʿAql** | The same verse uses a verb of reasoning in relation to hearts; it does not prescribe a modern cognitive architecture. | Model inference, deliberation, inhibition, planning, and epistemic responsibility as functions, without declaring one brain network to be ʿaql itself. |
| **Nafs** | Qur’an 12:53 describes a self urging evil; 75:2 a self reproaching itself; 89:27 a tranquil self. Qur’an 91:7–10 addresses its formation and purification/corruption. | Represent a whole self with changing tendencies. Separate appetite, self-reproach, and settled orientation; do not automatically build three agents, three substances, or an obligatory three-stage ladder. |
| **Ruh** | Qur’an 17:85 attributes ruh to the Lord’s affair/command and stresses limited human knowledge. | Preserve a theological referent without claiming to simulate its substance or measure its quantity. Consciousness measures, arousal, metabolic energy, and memory are not interchangeable with ruh. |
| **Fitrah** | Qur’an 30:30 and Muslim 2658b connect created human disposition with religion; the hadith also names parental influence. | Keep the theological commitment distinct from any proposed empirical prior. Do not identify a Big Five profile, cultural preference, or learned conformity with fitrah. |
| **Embodied and relational person** | Bukhari 1968 affirms obligations toward Allah, oneself, and family, in a narrative about fasting and sleep. | Include bodily maintenance, relationships, and competing obligations. A spiritual practice cannot be modeled solely as a resource bonus. |

Sources: [22:46](https://quran.com/al-hajj/46), [12:53](https://quran.com/yusuf/53), [75:2](https://quran.com/al-qiyamah/2), [89:27 and surrounding verses](https://quran.com/al-fajr/27), [91:7–10](https://quran.com/ash-shams/7-10), [17:85](https://quran.com/al-isra/85), [30:30](https://quran.com/ar-rum/30), [Muslim 2658b](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2658b), [Bukhari 1968](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1968). Qur’an links display Arabic and Mustafa Khattab’s *The Clear Quran*; bracketed expansions belong to the translation. In Muslim 2658b, the English parenthetical “Islam” is interpretive wording, not an additional Arabic word in the opening clause.

Al-Ghazali offers a useful competing mapping: *Ihya*, Book 21, chapter 1 distinguishes multiple meanings of each of qalb, ruh, nafs, and ʿaql, with an overlapping referent in the knowing human reality. He expressly discusses characteristics and states rather than explaining its ultimate nature. This supports overlapping interpretive views rather than isolated meters. It is his scholarly synthesis, not a revealed diagram. Its historical physiological account of a bodily spirit must not be imported as current biology. The translation’s editorial analogies are also separate from his words; its note discussing “ectoplasm” is not a scientific foundation. [Al-Ghazali, *Marvels of the Heart*, trans. W. J. Skellie, printed pp. 5–11](https://www.emaanlibrary.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Al-ghazalisMarvelsOfTheHeart.pdf).

Ghazali is an Ashʿari theologian and Shafiʿi jurist, so using his spiritual psychology does not establish that every part is specifically Maturidi. His taxonomy is valuable comparative material, while the project’s doctrinal default remains separately declared. [TDV, “Gazzâlî”](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/gazzali).

## 3. Agency and qadar: a Hanafi–Maturidi starting point

Qur’an 81:28–29 places human willing and dependence on Allah’s willing together. Removing either side to simplify a program would be an interpretive decision, not a neutral implementation. [Qur’an 81:28–29](https://quran.com/at-takwir/28-29).

The primary text of ʿUmar al-Nasafi’s creed offers a compact starting constraint. Paragraph 19 affirms Allah’s creation of human actions. Paragraph 20 affirms voluntary human actions and responsibility, while distinguishing divine approval of good from disapproval of evil. Thus, an action’s occurrence does not establish its moral approval. [*Al-ʿAqaʾid al-Nasafiyya*, Arabic text and site translation, §19](https://nasafi.org/paragraph-019/), [§20](https://nasafi.org/paragraph-020/).

For al-Maturidi specifically, Bekir Topaloğlu’s TDV account attributes action to Allah as creation and to the human as action/acquisition, citing *Kitab al-Tawhid*, pp. 357–366 in its referenced edition. It also emphasizes the limits of a person’s knowledge and control over all details of an act. This is a secondary scholarly guide to the primary work, not a direct verification of that edition. [TDV, “Mâtürîdî,” theological views](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/maturidi#2-kelama-dair-gorusleri).

Later **cüzi irade** formulations require care. Yusuf Şevki Yavuz’s TDV survey distinguishes general created capacity from particular volitional determination, treated in influential formulations as a mental relation rather than an independently existing thing. It reports internal variation and Ashʿari objections. Do not turn this into an uncreated component, a soul randomizer, or an exact claim about all Maturidis. [TDV, “Mâtürîdiyye,” human actions](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/maturidiyye). Contemporary Hanafi scholar Faraz Rabbani likewise affirms choice and divine creation while directing detailed school comparisons to Bayadi, Sadr al-Shariʿa, and later commentaries. [Rabbani, 2023](https://seekersguidance.org/answers/islamic-belief/what-is-the-maturidi-position-on-actions-of-servants-afal-al-ibad/).

**Engineering proposal:** circumstances and learned tendencies shape what an agent notices and can attempt; a decision interface allows selection, refusal, postponement, reconsideration, and commitment; the world resolves consequences, including failure and unintended effects. A player, deliberative policy, or another controller can supply a decision. Stochastic choice and deterministic replay are computational mechanisms; neither proves nor disproves metaphysical freedom. Never name the random seed “qadar” or the world scheduler “Allah.” Muslim 2664 combines seeking benefit and divine help with acceptance of what has occurred, providing an anti-fatalist practical orientation. [Muslim 2664](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2664).

## 4. Intention, accountability, and change

Intention belongs in the action record. Bukhari 1 relates deeds to intentions; Muslim 2564c distinguishes divine regard for hearts and deeds from appearances and wealth. **Design inference:** identical outward actions may arise from distinct purposes. Store intention separately from behavior, expected consequences, actual consequences, and observers’ beliefs. Observers should not automatically know a character’s inner motive. [Bukhari 1](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1), [Muslim 2564c](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2564c).

Do not collapse an intrusive thought into assent or deed. Bukhari 5269 distinguishes internal suggestions from enacted or uttered behavior. This is not a blanket doctrine that every settled inward commitment is exempt: detailed distinctions remain an interpretive task. Capacity and compulsion also matter: Qur’an 2:286 relates obligation to capacity; 16:106 explicitly distinguishes coerced disbelief from willingly embraced disbelief. **Proposal:** log awareness, available alternatives, coercion, capacity, deliberative assent, and action. These are inputs for an explicit scenario-specific ethical assessment, not a formula for Allah’s judgment. [Bukhari 5269](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5269), [Qur’an 2:286](https://quran.com/al-baqarah/286), [16:106](https://quran.com/an-nahl/106).

The framework needs meaningful change. Qur’an 39:53–54 joins hope in mercy with repentance and return; 91:7–10 addresses purification. Bukhari 6464 values sustained deeds and rejects deeds alone as a guarantee of entry into Paradise. **Proposal:** habits, commitments, relationships, and reparative behavior may change over time, with empirically tested learning rules where available. Spiritual success cannot be computed as accumulated points, and the text supplies no numerical habit-learning rate. [Qur’an 39:53–54](https://quran.com/az-zumar/53-54), [Bukhari 6464](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6464).

## 5. What the engine should and should not claim

The following boundaries are design judgments derived from the source distinctions above:

- Simulate behavior and attributed perspectives; do not claim that a functioning agent possesses ruh or human moral standing.
- Represent a character’s trust, hope, worship, doubt, and reported experience; do not identify distress with weak faith or outward prosperity with divine favor.
- Permit explicit fictional mechanics when a genre requires them, but label supernatural resources as fiction instead of empirical or revealed anatomy.
- Preserve accountability, mercy, and divine knowledge as theological commitments without implementing a salvation calculator, hidden worth score, or a causal formula for answered prayer.
- Keep measurement error, parameter uncertainty, interpretive disagreement, and unmodeled scope separate. Absence of a numeric representation does not mean ontological absence.

Operational closure is attainable: every simulated situation can have a documented fallback and a reproducible result. Complete knowledge of the human being does not follow. A good fallback preserves uncertainty and remains replaceable rather than claiming that an arbitrary coefficient has resolved an unseen reality.

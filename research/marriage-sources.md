# Marriage, courtship and widowhood: source record

Research gathered 2026-10-04 by one subagent web pass for HF 2.0 L3 (courtship and marriage); written up the same day. A second pass the same day re-checked hadith numbers against content, Qur'an verses, Turkish and Ottoman age claims and school positions; all held except the corrections noted in place. Turkish Civil Code statute text and classical school texts remain unread. Status: a sourced record of what named sources say, not a ruling and not a qualified review. It gives provenance for the marriage norms in `conscience/catalog.ts` (`MARRIAGE_NORMS`) and the decisions in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

Tiers as in [eid-and-mourning-sources.md](eid-and-mourning-sources.md): revealed (Qur'an); transmitted (hadith, collection and number); classical school positions (cited via contemporary pages, **none read directly**); contemporary references (islamqa.info, islamqa.org mirrors of Hanafi and Shafi'i fatwa sites, Dar al-Ifta Egypt, SeekersGuidance, TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi); encyclopedic (Wikipedia); press.

**Quote caveats.** sunnah.com returned HTTP 403 to every fetch; hadith text comes from hadithunlocked.com (named, with the graders it cites: Albani, Ali Za'i), Qur'an from quranx.com. The fetch tool runs pages through a summarizer, so wording below is paraphrase. Hadith gradings are the substitute site's cited graders, not independent takhrij.

## Majority, disputed, unverified

Settled or majority: both spouses' consent (hadith; consensus on annulment for a previously married woman); two witnesses; mahr; the prohibited degrees of 4:22–23; seclusion forbidden; looking at a prospective spouse permitted; not proposing over another's proposal; a widow's four months and ten days, none for a widower. By school count, three of four schools require a wali for the bride; Hanafi is the exception. Disputed: ijbar over a virgin (Maliki and Shafi'i classically allow it; Hanafi does not; every contemporary source fetched and Bukhari 5136/5138 point to required consent). Unverified: the Hanbali ijbar position (mainstream Hanbali is likely pro-ijbar; Ibn Taymiyya and Ibn al-Qayyim dissent), the Maliki announcement position. A man's parental approval rests only on contemporary fatwas (§2). The decisions taken are in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

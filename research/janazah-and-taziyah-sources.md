# Funeral prayer, burial, condolence and the deceased's debts: source record

Research gathered 2026-10-26 for the planned game _Three Days_ and the proposed group-held duty slice. Status: a sourced record of what named sources say. It is not a ruling and not a qualified review. It extends [eid-and-mourning-sources.md](eid-and-mourning-sources.md) §3–§5 and does not repeat it; it closes four open items there (§4 "three-day wording via snippets", mahmoodiyah 53729 "snippet only", Bukhari 5334 "unverified", Kurul 368's Ja'far citation). Access labels below describe the original research pass, not a new verification during this move into `research/`.

Tiers as in eid-and-mourning-sources.md: **revealed** (Qur'an); **transmitted** (hadith, collection and number); **classical** (school texts, **none read directly**: every title is "cited via" the page named); **contemporary** (Diyanet Din İşleri Yüksek Kurulu, TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyyah, Jordan's General Iftaa', Malaysia's Federal Territory Mufti, islamqa.info, islamqa.org mirrors of Hanafi and Shafi'i fatwa sites, Mathabah); **secondary handbook** (Sayyid Sabiq, _Fiqh al-Sunnah_, online copies); **custom** (what people do, not what is owed); **engineering assumption**.

**Quote caveats.** This pass differs from the earlier records. sunnah.com still returns HTTP 403, but hadithunlocked.com, quranx.com, kurul.diyanet.gov.tr, islamansiklopedisi.org.tr, islamqa.info, islamqa.org, dar-alifta.org and muftiwp.gov.my **answered raw** to curl on 2026-10-26, and their text was read stripped of HTML. Rows marked **raw** were read that way, so quoted wording is the page's own. Rows marked **summarised** come from a subagent's summarising fetch and are paraphrase. **Snippet** means a search-result summary only. Hadith numbers are hadithunlocked's (sunnah.com-style, with letter suffixes for Muslim); Diyanet and islamqa.info sometimes use a different Bukhari numbering (for example Diyanet "Kefâlet, 5 [2298]"), so a number in a Diyanet citation may not match hadithunlocked's. Gradings are those the page shows (Albani, Ali Za'i, Abd al-Baqi's "agreed upon"), not independent takhrij.

## 1. The funeral prayer (salat al-janazah)

### 1a. Standing: a group-held duty

| Claim | Tier | Source | Wording / notes |

### 1c. Who leads

| Claim | Tier | Source | Wording / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hanafi: the Muslim ruler, then the imam of the local mosque, then the guardian and relatives by closeness | Contemporary, citing classical | [Mathabah via islamqa.org 133748](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/mathabah/133748/who-has-the-greatest-right-to-lead-the-funeral-prayer/), **raw** | Cites al-Fiqh 'ala al-Madhahib al-Arba'a, Fath al-Bari, I'la al-Sunan, Bada'i'. Reports: Husayn sent the governor of Madina forward to lead Hasan's prayer, "had it not been a sunnah, I would not have come forward" (Bazzar, Tabarani, Bayhaqi, cited via). |
| Shafi'i: relatives by closeness, then the ruler | same | same, **raw** | |
| Maliki: the person the deceased named in his will (if notably pious), then the ruler, then relatives | same | same, **raw** | |
| Hanbali: the person the deceased named, then the ruler, then relatives | same | same, **raw** | |
| Conflicting report: Maliki and Hanbali put the guardian first, then the ruler; Abu Hanifa and Muhammad: ruler, judge, local imam, guardian; al-Shafi'i and Abu Yusuf: father first | Secondary handbook | [Fiqh al-Sunnah 4/54 (alim.org)](https://alim.org/hadith/fiqh-us-sunnah/4/54), summarised | **Disagrees** with Mathabah on the Maliki and Hanbali nominee. |
| Two views (authority first, or family first); favours a qualified guardian, otherwise the appointed mosque imam | Contemporary (Dar al-Ifta Egypt) | [Fatwa 23948](https://dar-alifta.org/en/fatwa/details/23948/precedence-in-leading-the-funeral-prayer), summarised | Cites al-Nawawi, al-Majmu' 5/217. |
| The one with the right may hand it to another competent person | Contemporary | Mathabah 133748, **raw** | "there is no objection, when the one who has the right … grants permission to another". |

**Reading.** Three schools (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) give the family or the deceased's own nominee priority over a ruler or appointed imam in at least one of the two reports; the Hanafi order puts authority and the mosque imam first. Every report read lets the one with the right delegate it. Who leads in Turkish practice is **unsourced** here.

## 8. Engineering mapping (assumptions, not rulings)

Each line says how a game might represent a **person's understanding** through `understandNorm` (a held reading with provenance), never a ruling, a score or an acceptance.

1. **Funeral prayer.** A held understanding "the funeral prayer is owed by the community and discharged when someone performs it". A proposed `enableGroupDuty(community, { normId, window, quorum })` reads which holders attended. **`quorum` is a host parameter with no sourced default:** the sources make one performer enough for discharge (§1a), and 40/100/three rows are reward and intercession counts (§1b). A game may show the crowd; it may not say "too few for it to count". If nobody attended, a held reading may record that the prayer is still owed at the grave (islamqa 20472), never that a person is condemned. Who is at fault when none attend stays unsourced; a reading should say "nobody went", not assign sin. Who leads is a held understanding (Hanafi: imam; others: family or nominee), delegable.
2. **Washing and shroud.** Community tasks done by others, off-screen or as background; no per-person reading.
3. **Speed of burial.** A held understanding "do not delay the burial without need", giving people urgency. No timer penalty; the source gives no hour count.
4. **Condolence.** A held understanding with a three-day window from the death, once per visitor; repeat visits disliked; late learners and travellers exempt. Phrases from §4b feed speech. Food: neighbours bring, family does not host (held by Hacer and Şükrü); the family hosting is a disputed custom a character may hold either way. Sitting for three days, recitation for the dead and the 3rd/7th/40th days are **disputed customs**, each held by a person with its source label; the game never resolves them.
5. **Mourning.** Women's mourning beyond three days is outside the held norm except a widow's 4 months 10 days (already in `MARRIAGE_NORMS`). Grief itself is HF grief, not this norm.
6. **Debts.** A held understanding "the estate pays debts first; heirs are not personally bound; a volunteer may pay and clear him". Burak's ledger is a host fact; settling it is a choice with social readback, not a meter on Kemal's soul. The hadiths about the soul being suspended are a person's belief and may appear in speech; the engine never computes any state of the dead.
7. **Make-ups of the dead.** Feeding per missed day is the default understanding for fasts, paid from a bequest or volunteered; nobody prays another's prayers; prayer-iskat and devir are customs a person may hold, Diyanet calls them baseless. `owedMakeUps` on a dead person is never surfaced as guilt; it may appear as "what he left undone" in a relative's held reading.

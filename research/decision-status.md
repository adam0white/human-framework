# What is rejected, what is deferred, and what can return

Status: 2026-09-07. The earlier roadmap compressed several different judgments into “rejected.” This register distinguishes a failed formulation from a valuable domain that the prototype has not implemented. A rejected formula does not close its surrounding scientific or theological question.

## Formulations we should not carry forward

| Formulation | Why it is rejected | What could return, and the admission test |
|---|---|---|
| Compute an individual's inherited trait points from a population heritability percentage. | A population variance estimate is not an individual's genetic share or a character-generation coefficient. | A clearly specified inheritance model for a defined trait and population, with appropriate data, uncertainty and validation. Do not repurpose heritability as individual destiny. |
| Personality or general intelligence directly grants medicine, programming or other learned expertise. | This bypasses training and conflates a disposition with acquired task knowledge. | A tested effect on a particular learning process or task, while retaining the actual practice and knowledge prerequisites. |
| Training any cognitive skill universally increases intelligence or every linked skill. | The inherited equations stipulated broad transfer that their sources did not establish. | A specific directed transfer hypothesis with active controls, untrained outcomes and an effect that survives comparison with a no-transfer model. The engine can already record authored, nonrecursive transfer; presets leave it off. |
| Fixed lifetime malleability caps or a universal forgetting constant. | Neither one curve nor one limit is justified for every skill, person and context. | A task-specific retention/development model with a defined time scale, measurement procedure and longitudinal comparison. |
| Qalb health points, ruh as altruism, or a scalar measuring divine acceptance. | These equations identify theological referents with invented measurable quantities. A sacred name does not justify that identification. | Reviewed representations of a character's understood duties, intentions, worship, repentance or service, with source and interpretation recorded separately from behavior. Do not claim that those records measure ultimate inward or divine standing. |
| Interconnected sliders guarantee human consciousness, free will, social cohesion or historical cycles. | Declaring connections does not demonstrate those phenomena. Randomness alone does not establish agency. | Bounded behavioral claims tested against alternatives and observations. Keep metaphysical conclusions separate. Macro models need institutions, interactions and independently specified outcome patterns. |

The source-by-source grounds and unresolved evidence are in the [historical audit](historical-source-audit.md), [empirical review](empirical-models.md), and [Islamic foundations](islamic-foundations.md). These decisions concern the exact claims above; they do not mean genetics, intelligence, learning, spiritual life or social history are irrelevant.

## Current engineering choices remain replaceable

The logistic work-success rule, fatigue and hunger proxies, practice curve, trust increments, utility weights and time settings are authored mechanisms. They are **implemented hypotheses**, not research findings promoted to truth. Each should be replaced when a simpler or better-supported mechanism explains the declared task more accurately. Belief and practice ablations disable both their update and corresponding expected benefit; they do not isolate updating as a single causal variable.

The first playtests identified two concrete failures: an eight-round repair could leave no slack for unsuccessful work after rest, and bounded fatigue allowed continued exertion once the meter saturated. The correction separates intended action from physically executable action, adds mandatory recovery when exertion exceeds capacity, and gives longer workloads room for repeated recovery and meals. This is a game-capacity rule, not a medical threshold. All policies face it. The [historical benchmark](../docs/history/benchmark-report-0.1.0-2026-09-07.md) remains available; its ranking of policies cannot establish the superiority of a human model.

Workload calibration should make a legible strategy feasible across seeds, permit some failed attempts, and expose the intended tradeoffs. It must not optimize coefficients until the policy called “full” beats its comparators. Human calibration would require a defined task, population and independently collected observations; whether a user wins is not such a measurement.

**Later 2026-09-07 review decision:** do not add a forced-recovery efficiency penalty solely to separate policies. [Deadline-only experiments](../docs/recovery-design-exploration.md) already distinguish competent planned recovery from greedy requests, while preserving cases where greed is useful. Separating physical capacity from a host's fallback action is accepted integration work; automatic food choice is not a universal body rule. A genuine interruption/setup cost remains an eligible authored mechanism with a zero-cost control and sensitivity test. This defers an untested formulation rather than rejecting recovery consequences as a domain.

The [fresh direction review](../docs/post-mvp-review.md) also identifies an open portability defect: equivalent progress-unit changes alter policy choices. An explicit task-to-goal value conversion and a host-owned game integration now precede broader faculties. Neither is already implemented. The default Full scorer may be replaced or narrowed if a simpler policy supplies the required benefit.

## Deferred domains with concrete return conditions

| Domain | Why it is waiting | Smallest useful return experiment |
|---|---|---|
| Detailed physiology, sleep, pain, injury and metabolism | The current body proxy cannot claim physiological realism; a complete body model would overwhelm this iteration. | Choose one task and one measurable limitation, compare a coarse capacity model with one physiological alternative, and test recovery and impairment over matching durations. |
| Memory, attention, habits, appraisal and planning | Adding every candidate together would obscure which one changes behavior. | A delayed cue that is no longer visible; a distraction; a repeated cue with reversal; or a two-step task where immediate utility fails. Admit one mechanism only if it distinguishes the alternatives. |
| Broader motives, worship and meaning | Fixed care/duty weights are not a complete account of a person's purposes. | A reviewed scenario with a traceable conflict between understood duty, personal need and immediate advantage, without an automated judgment of sincerity. |
| Relationships, consent, attachment and testimony | Current help prepares support; another actor does not independently accept or refuse. | A structured request, an independent response, and ambiguous evidence about the outcome. Preserve Solo Repair as a control with no social effects. |
| Childhood, aging and inheritance | They require a declared developmental time scale and evidence beyond a workday. | One specific change over time, separated from practice, cohort and current performance effects. |
| Institutions, economics, community cohesion and cliodynamics | Small task agents do not guarantee macro patterns. | Add one institution or network mechanism and test several independently specified patterns across conditions; compare simpler explanations. [Prior-art assessment](prior-art-mvp.md). |
| Adaptive UI, fourth-wall effects and contracting time | These are promising interaction experiments but should follow understandable action and recovery feedback. | Change presentation or playback pacing while keeping the same recorded simulation, then test whether users understand decisions and deadlines better. |
| Arbitrary language input | Runtime processing must remain structured and reproducible. | An optional compiler that produces validated commands, preserves ambiguity and can be removed without changing headless execution. |

Simulating quarks until people emerge is outside the implementation route: it offers no tractable validation or delivery path for this reusable game component. Its useful lesson is to choose and disclose the abstraction boundary, then move that boundary only when a concrete task needs it.

Before admission, every candidate needs an owner for its state, permitted observations, update rule, computational cost, simpler comparator and a result that would count against it. The [coverage ledger](../docs/coverage-ledger.md) describes the broader gaps; the [roadmap](../docs/roadmap.md) sequences the next experiments.

## Five kinds of claim

| Kind | How it enters the framework | How it can change |
|---|---|---|
| Revealed source | Qur’anic Arabic and authenticated prophetic reports, accepted within the project's Islamic commitment | A source record can be corrected; source authenticity, interpretation, and applicability are separate questions |
| Interpretation | Translation, tafsir, kalam, fiqh, ethical/spiritual reading | Attributed to a scholar, school, edition, and context; assessed rather than silently equated with revelation |
| Empirical finding | A measured result with task, sample, design, uncertainty, and replication context | Updated when better evidence changes its scope or support |
| Explanatory model | A theory linking observations to mechanisms | Compared with alternatives and tested where its predictions differ |
| Engineering convention | A formula, parameter, category, fallback, time step, or gameplay choice | Changed transparently; carries no borrowed theological or scientific authority |

These are types, not five numeric confidence levels. A Qur’anic source and a fitted coefficient are not entries in one probability leaderboard. For hadith, keep collection/number, wording, grading authority, transmission assessment where relevant, and application distinct. Arabic text, translation, and commentary must occupy separate fields.

## What “non-obsolete” means here

Publication date alone is inadequate. Older work remains relevant when its specific claim survives scrutiny; recent work can be weak or preliminary. Prefer original texts/papers, well-designed experiments, replications, transparent syntheses, and explicit model comparison. Check population, cultural setting, measurement validity, confounding, and whether evidence is correlational before assigning a causal simulation rule.

The [Open Science Collaboration replication project](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aac4716) motivates checking replication rather than trusting a famous result. [Henrich, Heine and Norenzayan](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X0999152X) motivate scrutiny of population scope. Neither source warrants blanket dismissal of psychology. These are methodological reasons for scoped claims, not universal failure rates for all human research.

For each proposed mechanism, record: exact claim; claim kind; source and locator; access depth (full text, passage, or abstract); population/task; support and counterevidence; competing explanation; proposed simulation use; parameters and units; applicability limits; validation target; review date; retain/modify/compare/defer decision. Unknown quantities remain unknown until an explicitly labeled scenario default is chosen.

## Research limits and next acquisitions

This pass prioritizes architecture-changing evidence. It does not deeply review every sensory modality, developmental stage, clinical condition, jurisprudential issue, or cultural setting. “Faculties covered” means a declared conceptual location and extension contract, not a validated module for every faculty.

Before a public claim of Islamic adequacy, obtain qualified Hanafi–Maturidi review of exact source-to-mechanism mappings. Before empirical prediction claims, obtain appropriate datasets and compare held-out predictions against simpler alternatives. Those are concrete future validation requirements; they do not prevent the present research proposal from being useful.

Unresolved apparent conflicts between text, interpretation, and empirical evidence receive a recorded conflict entry. Inspect authenticity, language/context, strength of the interpretation, measurement, and model assumptions. Do not falsify observations to protect a favored interpretation, or silently let a computational convenience decide theology.

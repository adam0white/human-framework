# What carries forward from the earlier work

Prepared 2026-09-06. Based only on the two supplied local records; the full old UHTF reports remain unread in this run. This is an assessment of recovered ideas, not a review of unseen documents.

**2026-09-07 update:** both full reports have now been supplied and audited. The earlier assessment below records the evidence available during the first synthesis. The [new audit](historical-source-audit.md) supersedes its access limitation and distinguishes promising questions from unsupported coefficients, citation mismatches and numerical defects. The [current MVP](../README.md) implements the newly accepted loop rather than adopting either historical report as a specification.

The original ambition is stronger than a personality-stat system. It combines human development, context-sensitive action, bounded generative novelty, and a player's limited knowledge of other people. The current request adds a clearer epistemic order and explicitly asks to investigate new directions before combining them.

The historical assertions that victory produces laziness, danger increases speed, or experience gain simply slows with age are **scenario hypotheses**. None should become a universal law merely because it appeared in the original game sketch. Success may support mastery or complacency; threat responses depend on appraisal, training, relationships, and available actions. Domain-specific evidence is needed before setting causal functions.

The records preserve an unresolved RNG boundary: G1-B rejects RNG, while G1-D–E permits event randomness and candidate selection. The current request emphasizes choice rather than a predetermined life. A suitable research proposal therefore supports deterministic replay and explicit stochastic models without equating either one with metaphysical freedom. Player or externally supplied choices remain causal inputs.

The old labels Human Element Engine, UHPF, and UHTF belong to one evolving lineage in the recovery. No name, ECS implementation, moral meter, equation, or “production-ready” claim becomes mandatory through that lineage. The working title **Human Framework** leaves the investigation open.

The main revision is reciprocal development: a capacity can affect performance and learning, but tools, opportunity, relationships, bodily condition, knowledge, and past practice also alter what the person can do. A one-way innate-stats-to-skills tree cannot carry all of that without exceptions accumulating everywhere.

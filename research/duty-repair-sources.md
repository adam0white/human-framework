# Bounded understood duty and repair: source record

Research checked 2026-09-15. The developing-person duty candidate models one authored entrusted-property situation. It records source attribution, an actor's received and stated understanding, a stated intention, delivered host-attested restitution, and a recipient's response separately. It does not issue a Hanafi fiqh ruling, authenticate a host receipt, infer sincerity, decide forgiveness, or compute virtue or divine acceptance.

## Revealed and transmitted anchors

- [Qur'an 4:58](https://quran.com/4/58) commands returning trusts to their rightful recipients and fairness in judgment. The linked page presents Arabic and Dr. Mustafa Khattab's *The Clear Quran* English translation. Applying the passage to a particular item, deadline, damage amount or remedy is a further interpretive step, not a number supplied by revelation.
- [Sahih al-Bukhari 1](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1) relates deeds to intentions. The Arabic report, collection/identifier and displayed English translation are distinct records. The software stores only a character's *stated* intention; it cannot verify an inward state or infer it from restitution.
- [Sahih al-Bukhari 1968](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1968) narrates Salman's reminder concerning rights of Allah, self and family during fasting and sleep, and the Prophet's approval of his statement. This supports representing competing responsibilities without reducing worship or care to a productivity bonus. It does not supply a universal scheduling priority or a specific property-damage rule.
- [Qur'an 2:286](https://quran.com/2/286) relates obligation to capacity; [39:53–54](https://quran.com/39/53-54) speaks of mercy and return. These limit simplistic claims that a failed attempt proves culpability or that an apology mathematically settles a harm. Specific application and conditions require attributed interpretation.

The existing [Islamic foundations](islamic-foundations.md) memo sets a Sunni Hanafi–Maturidi starting point and locates Bukhari 1, 1968, Qur'an 2:286 and 39:53–54 within the project's broader theological research. The [source method](source-method.md) keeps revelation, transmission, translation, interpretation, empirical finding, explanatory model and engineering convention distinct. These passages were checked through the linked primary-text pages. English translations remain attributed renderings, not the Arabic text. No claim of manuscript verification or independent hadith criticism follows from this access.

## Case interpretation and engineering boundary

The first case is an adult who received an entrusted item and missed its return. The authored case treats returning that item as a relevant duty and declares one returned item as its sole operational requirement. It does not model damage or determine additional compensation. Its `reviewStatus` is **unreviewed** until a qualified Hanafi reviewer assesses the exact source-to-case mapping. Bukhari 1968 is broad context for concurrent duties, not a fiqh ruling on this entrustment. Qur'an 39:53–54 concerns repentance and mercy; the simulation does not turn a recipient's acceptance into Allah's forgiveness.

The case's requirement IDs and `requiredAmount` values are **engineering and host-authored scenario quantities**. They are not sourced religious units. The duty module sums only delivered host-attested restitution receipts. Amount zero preserves a failed attempt; partial amounts remain partial; all authored requirements must be met for the module's operational `completed` display. That display means only that the host has delivered enough claimed outcome receipts for the declared case requirements. It does not settle the original breached commitment, erase harm outside the declared case, or establish legal/spiritual discharge. A canonical `outcomeId` counts once even when receipt IDs differ.

The host owns property, resource consumption, deadline breach, actual restitution and recipient behavior. `receiveRepairOutcome` records delivery to one actor at `at`; it does not separately store when the physical event occurred. A host requiring delayed-event reasoning must retain occurrence time in its world record or later extend the receipt schema explicitly. The module cannot authenticate source actor IDs, canonical outcome IDs or audience disclosures. Its strict schemas, owner checks, finite 16-case/8-requirement/128-event limits, and explicit export/restore are software safeguards, not empirical or theological validation.

Actor-local notices and private stated intentions stay out of an observer view unless a host explicitly declares disclosure to that viewer. A recipient response is independent of material restitution. `accepted`, `refused` and `deferred` record attributed social responses, not forgiveness or an automatic alteration of canonical property outcomes. Authored decision rules and planner priority order must consume these views explicitly; a source record cannot silently outrank a purpose or cause an action.

Each delivered duty notice also records the actor's **stated stance** toward this case's application: `accepted`, `uncertain` or `rejected`. The passage and authored scenario interpretation do not themselves imply that the actor knew, assented to or accepted applicability. A later delivered notice can revise that stance while older records remain attributed. This is a reported understanding state and an engineering vocabulary, not a scholar's applicability judgment or a hidden moral verdict. None of the three values changes property, restitution or planning priorities on its own.

Before claiming interpretive fidelity, request qualified review of the exact Qur'an/hadith passages, translation, scenario circumstances, school-specific analysis and resulting case fields. Before predicting people, use appropriate human evidence and meaningful simpler alternatives. Software tests and deterministic authored examples establish only this narrow executable representation.

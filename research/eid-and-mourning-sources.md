# Eid al-Fitr, zakat al-fitr, grave visits and condolence: source record

Research gathered 2026-10-04 by two subagent web passes; written up the same day. Status: a sourced record of what named sources say, not a ruling and not a qualified Hanafi review. It answers the HANDOFF deferred question on the Eid prayer and zakat al-fitr, and gives provenance for the town scenario's grave visit and mourning customs.

Tiers as in [prayer-times-sources.md](prayer-times-sources.md): revealed / transmitted; classical Hanafi (**none read directly**; every title is "cited via" the page named); contemporary Turkish Hanafi (Diyanet, TDV); Hanafi fatwa sites (SeekersGuidance, islamqa.org mirrors); **blog** (hanafilegalrulings.blogspot.com, an unattributed blog, the sole carrier of several Eid points below); press; **custom / folklore** (what people do, not what is owed); engineering assumption.

**Quote caveats.** sunnah.com returned HTTP 403 to every fetch, so no hadith was checked on sunnah.com. Hadith numbers come from sunnah.com search-result titles and from substitute sites (ihadis.com, tohed.com, hadithunlocked.com), named as such. The fetch tool runs pages through a summarizer, so "quotes" are mostly close paraphrases; long or Turkish quotations are the tool's extraction, not checked against the page. Ibn Abidin page numbers come from secondary pages, not from Radd al-Muhtar itself. The 2026 fitre announcement was re-fetched on 2026-10-04 (also through a summarising fetch).

**Where the window's start comes from.** TDV says the Eid prayer begins once the sunrise kerahat has passed, but gives no minutes. The Diyanet Board's mekruh-times page (re-checked 2026-10-04; [prayer-times-sources.md](prayer-times-sources.md) §3) sets the sunrise kerahat at 40-50 minutes; other fatwa sites give about 20. The researcher of the prayer-times pass found no source on "kerahat before the Eid prayer" as such; the two halves above come from two different pages, and joining them is this note's step, not a source's. The 2026 Diyanet times in the press (about 07:23-07:45 on 20 March) are consistent with sunrise plus a kerahat margin, but sunrise was not retrieved, so that is not checked.

Not found: a Diyanet İlmihal passage or Kurul page on bayram namazı that could be read directly. A Diyanet statement at diyanet.gov.tr/es-es/Institucional/Detalle/29560 failed with a parse error.

**Hadith** (via substitute sites, since sunnah.com was blocked; texts not checked against the Arabic):

- Bukhari 1503 (Ibn Umar): one sa' of dates or barley on every Muslim, slave or free, male or female, young or old; ordered paid before people go out to the prayer. [ihadis](https://ihadis.com/en/bukhari/hadith/1503)
- Bukhari 1504 (Ibn Umar): one sa' of dates or barley on slave and free Muslims. [ihadis](https://ihadis.com/en/bukhari/hadith/1504)
- Bukhari 1506 (Abu Sa'id): one sa' of meal, barley, dates, cottage cheese or raisins. [ihadis](https://ihadis.com/en/bukhari/hadith/1506)
- Bukhari 1508 (Abu Sa'id): under Mu'awiya, when wheat was plentiful, one measure of wheat was counted as two. The researcher reports this as the basis of the Hanafi half-sa' of wheat. [ihadis](https://ihadis.com/en/bukhari/hadith/1508)
- Bukhari 1509 (Ibn Umar): ordered zakat al-fitr paid before the Eid prayer. [ihadis](https://ihadis.com/en/bukhari/hadith/1509)
- Muslim 984: one sa' of dates or barley. [tohed](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/muslim/984/). tohed's added "so the poor could participate" and "accepted half sa' of wheat" look like **summarizer embellishment, not hadith text**.
- Muslim 986: paid before the people go out for prayer; Ibn Umar paid one or two days early. [tohed](https://en.tohed.com/hadith/muslim/986/)
- Abu Dawud 1609 (Ibn Abbas): purification of the fasting person from idle and obscene talk and food for the poor; accepted as zakat if paid before the prayer, ordinary charity after. Search result shows "Hasan (Al-Albani)" on [sunnah.com/abudawud:1609](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1609); page not read. One search summary put this wording under Muslim 984d, likely a conflation; treat it as Abu Dawud only.

Classical Hanafi texts on fitre (al-Hidaya, al-Fatawa al-Hindiyya, Radd al-Muhtar): **unsourced** in this pass.

## 6. Engineering mapping (assumptions, not rulings)

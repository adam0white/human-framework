# Ramadan fasting: smoking, illness, fidya, suhoor and tarawih — source record

Research gathered 2026-10-04 by a subagent web pass; written up the same day. Status: a sourced record of what named sources say, not a ruling and not a qualified Hanafi review. It answers the HANDOFF deferred question on smoking as a fast-breaker and records the sources behind the town scenario's illness exemption and make-up fasts.

Tiers as in [prayer-times-sources.md](prayer-times-sources.md): revealed / transmitted; classical Hanafi (**none read directly**; every title is "cited via" the page named); contemporary Turkish Hanafi (Diyanet, TDV); Hanafi fatwa sites (SeekersGuidance, islamqa.org mirrors); press; other schools; engineering assumption.

**Quote caveats.** The fetch tool returns summaries, not raw page text. Turkish in quotation marks is what the tool returned in quotation marks; English is a paraphrase of the page's wording. sunnah.com returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch, so no hadith page was opened. No Diyanet page specific to hypertension was found. Two Diyanet pages were re-fetched on 2026-10-04 during the write-up (marked "re-checked", also through a summarising fetch).

**Conflict: kaza only, or kaza and kefaret.** The Diyanet Kurul page says only that smoking breaks the fast. Diyanet's public wording relayed in the press says kaza. Hanafi English fatwa sites say qada plus kaffara for a deliberate act. Whether Diyanet holds kefaret due for smoking is **unconfirmed**. Do not encode "kefaret under Diyanet" without a Diyanet source.

## 2. Illness exemption

| Claim | Tier | Source | Wording / notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Someone a reliable doctor says would fall ill if fasting is treated as sick | Contemporary (Diyanet) | same page | Paraphrase only; no Turkish quote returned. |
| A specialist advises against fasting: temporary illness means kaza; permanent illness means fidye; someone unable to pay fidye is not accountable until able | Contemporary (Diyanet); revealed source cited | [Kurul: uzman doktorun oruç tutmamasını önerdiği kimse](https://kurul.diyanet.gov.tr/tr/fetva/uzman-bir-doktorun-oruc-tutmamasini-onerdigi-kimse-ne-yapmalidir/0193c42d-73a1-73ad-9afe-097caa08364e) | Paraphrase only. | A reliable Muslim physician should confirm that an illness is chronic | Hanafi fatwa site | [islamqa seekersguidance-hanafi 32803](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/seekersguidance-hanafi/32803/too-sick-to-fast-in-ramadan-too-poor-to-pay-the-expiatory-payment-fidya/) | Only partly supports the common "experience or a competent doctor" criterion: the **"based on experience" half is unsourced**. |
| Hypertension: where fasting is a health risk, not fasting is religiously appropriate | Press paraphrase; **unsourced at the Diyanet level** | [NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/yasam/galeri-tansiyon-hastasi-oruc-tutabilir-mi-diyanete-gore-tansiyon-hastasi-oruc-tutabilir-mi,PAOWd0gCKUejZcU9qL658A) | "Oruç tutmanın sağlık açısından risk teşkil ettiği durumlarda kişinin oruç tutmamasının dinen uygun olduğunu belirtir." No Kurul hypertension page found. |

The researcher's inference, **not a sourced statement**: by the Diyanet conditions above, a person whose doctor judges fasting harmful while the illness is temporary or manageable owes kaza, not fidye; fidye applies only to permanent inability. No source states that contrast for hypertension specifically.

## 6. Engineering mapping (assumptions, not rulings)

- `agenda/prayer.ts` defaults the fast's violating actions to eating and drinking (Qur'an 2:187). The town scenario adds `tea` (drinking) and `smoke`. With §1, `smoke` now has a contemporary Hanafi source (Diyanet Kurul, citing al-Hidaya and Radd al-Muhtar via the page) and Hanafi fatwa-site support; it is no longer only an engineering assumption. What a broken fast *owes* beyond kaza is disputed (§1 conflict) and is not modelled.
- The town's make-up fasts after an illness exemption match the Diyanet kaza logic for a temporary or manageable illness (§2-§3). Treating Halil's hypertension as kaza rather than fidye is the researcher's inference from general Diyanet conditions, not a hypertension-specific source.
- Suhoor lead and iftar length remain engineering defaults (`RAMADAN_DEFAULTS`).

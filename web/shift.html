Model notes

This is an authored scheduling game using the shared Human component. Its skill curve, success estimates, body costs, job values and early-service bonus are game assumptions, not calibrated human measurements. Equal-duration permitted repair work gets the same practice whether it succeeds or fails; failure is not diagnosis. Condition is revealed only by paid inspection. Controllers use your visible information and are not optimal planners. The three related seals share one repair skill; this does not establish transfer between unrelated tasks. No religious or social scoring is applied.

Score is awarded once per verified job: its service value plus that value multiplied by the fraction of the shift remaining. Further waiting cannot raise earned score. All three running pumps complete the shift; a partial departure preserves what you earned.

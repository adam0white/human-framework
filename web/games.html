About these games and the framework

These are authored games using shared, deterministic human-state components. Their scores concern the stated game tasks. They are not calibrated explanations of people or measures of moral or religious standing. A repeated seed and the same choices reproduce the same game.

The laboratory keeps the earlier experiments, policy comparisons and model notes. New games test whether those boundaries are useful in actual play; familiarity with existing games is expected.

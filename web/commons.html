Model notes

This is an authored persistent worksite, not a calibrated model of human behavior. The unchanged Human 0.1.0 component handles body, capacity, and task practice; this game owns resource yields, structures, durations, commitments, and Meryem’s decision rules. There is no LLM, random outcome, or seed. Her acceptance is explicit and recorded; it does not measure trust, morality, faith, or divine approval.

All people share one integer-minute clock. Jobs can overlap and finish independently. Practice can shorten later work within this game’s authored limits. The game has no sleep cycle, spoilage, weather, travel map, danger, or offline progress. Day numbers are elapsed-time labels. The first milestone is followed by repeatable supply caches, not a full campaign. The laboratory and Solo Repair remain separate.

      About the model

      These are small, authored experiments in a reusable human framework. Paid actions, remembered events, read reports and other people’s responses carry consequences. Reports and inferred options can be wrong; the situation determines what actually happens.

      The rules are not calibrated predictions of people, clinical advice, or measures of moral or religious standing. The scenarios use no language model. Browser saves contain the whole scenario and are not a secure way to hide information from someone inspecting the code.

Model notes

This is an authored solo game, not a calibrated account of human performance. The host owns routes, parcels, static hidden crossing conditions, time and delivery outcomes. The shared human component owns body and routecraft practice. Formulas and coefficients are engineering choices; experience does not transfer to unrelated tasks. No social or religious scoring is used. Estimates use perceived condition and either the inspected crossing or an assumed midpoint; they are not guarantees. Save files contain the hidden state needed for exact resumption. The laboratory holds the research context.

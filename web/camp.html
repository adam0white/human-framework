Work, recovery and materials

Work advances only when you move time. While available, you recover automatically; you can begin a job whenever you choose. Food still needs a portion and time to eat—open People to take a meal.

A started construction stage keeps its materials and work at the site, even while paused. Resume the same stage or agree on a handover. A workbench improves the work still ahead; it does not erase effort already spent. Stopping a gathering trip gives no collected output; stopping a meal returns its reserved portion without hunger relief.

Time spent gathering or building gives you practice that can shorten later jobs, even if you stop before finishing.

Meryem decides how to pursue an accepted project, including food and recovery. Your two jobs can run together. Next Event stops at a work/recovery boundary, with a short review interval when nobody is assigned.

      Model notes

      This is an authored game using deterministic Human/runtime 0.1.1 and host-owned work, resources and consent rules. Condition and task estimates are simulated, not calibrated measurements of people. The ferry, weather and household needs are authored scenario events; camp nights are provisioned, not separately simulated. There is no LLM, moral-worth score, faith measurement or religious evaluator.

      A current save checks the current people, jobs, materials and supply choices. It does not reconstruct or authenticate an earlier play history.

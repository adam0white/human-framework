      How time and reports work

      A radio request takes one minute and one charge on completion. The keeper observes at that moment; the message takes five more minutes to arrive in the original harbors, or two in Clear connection. You can use that interval for another action. A completed lookout observes directly.

      Reports show their source, observation time and delivery time. An older arriving report stays in the delivery list while your notebook keeps the most recent observation. Even a recent observation may change before you arrive.

      A failed canal attempt returns the lens after the full six minutes and spends one fare. Stopping an action keeps its paid effort; a canal fare is spent on departure. Incomplete requests, lookout and meals grant no result. The ridge always reaches the beacon if you finish before minute 32. The evening launch needs the beacon lit before minute 12; after that its crew stays in harbor, even if you restore the overnight beacon.

      1× runs one simulated minute per second. Time pauses when your action finishes or a reply arrives, on leaving the tab, and after reload. No offline progress occurs. With no action or incoming reply, the next event is the visible launch deadline or beacon closing. “Wait until closing” spends the rest of the episode.

    How the afternoon works

    The woodshed, workbench and garden are already built. Timber trips bring 4 bundles, food trips bring 3 portions, and the workbench shortens assembly. Your starting condition and materials come from paid work in a reproducible opening.

    At 90 minutes the ferry waits for your allocation decision. Send it to resume work. At 180 minutes, work stops for the day; you can still assign caches to camp, then see the result. An unfinished job remains unfinished. A job can continue after the ferry checkpoint but cannot finish after dusk in this episode.

    1× plays one simulated minute per second, 4× plays four. Both checkpoints always pause. Switching tabs pauses; there is no offline progress. Original Common Ground saves use a separate format and remain on their original page.

    Model notes

    This is an authored game, not a calibrated model of human behavior. The unchanged Human 0.1.0 handles body, capacity and paid practice; Common Ground 0.1.0 owns the work, materials, yields, Meryem’s choices and consent. This episode adds two allocation outcomes and timing checkpoints. Rain, the ferry and households are authored stakes, not a weather model or additional simulated people.

    No LLM, random outcome, morality score, faith measurement or religious evaluator is used. Reported condition is approximate. The result shows what these rules produce; it cannot establish enjoyment or model realism. The original worksite and Solo Repair remain separate controls.

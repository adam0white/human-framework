How to playtest

Pick any game. Play as you normally would. Stop whenever you want.

Tell us what happened

Open the game menu and choose Send feedback. Some games call it Send word or Send this run to the notebook.

Add your name or a tester nickname so Adam can find your playthrough. Tell us what you tried, what you expected, and what happened. Say what interested you, confused you, or made you want to stop. You do not need to suggest a fix.

Press Send once and read the status message. If it says queued, the upload is still pending. If sending fails, keep the error message and tell Adam which game you were playing. Keep personal details out of your note.

What we record

Participating games record play events, game logs, browser warnings and errors, saves, and replay files while recording is on. These help us reproduce bugs. A random player id connects your visits across supported games in the same browser. Adding a name labels that linked play history in Adam’s private notebook. Your note is sent when you press Send.

We do not record unrelated typing or browser activity. Private play records are kept for up to 180 days in the lab’s storage. Copies Adam downloads need separate deletion.

To stop recording, open the game menu and turn off Record play for the lab. Some games put this switch inside the feedback or notebook dialog. Turning it off stops uploads; it does not delete records already received. Browser privacy settings can also suppress recording. Keeping a fictional person between games is a separate choice. Analysing your personality needs separate permission.

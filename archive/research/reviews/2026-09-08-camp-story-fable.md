**Where the representation does not earn its place**

1. **The journal protects one 180-minute island in a snapshot-authority lifecycle.** Pre-entry the world is whatever the kernel validates, see `camp-story.js:149`. After return only monotone counters are checked, so stock, body and jobs may be anything internally consistent, see `camp-story.js:132-142`. Only inside the window is the world pinned to replay at `camp-story.js:156`. The story contract itself says consistent snapshot edits are undetectable. Everything else in the wrapper exists to serve that replay: the ordinary admission cap, the hard record bound, advance coalescing, the command schema, the trusted set, and the two admission fields in the view. The four first-pass failures listed in the story contract were all in this apparatus, not in the game. The hard bound is also unreachable through the API. Once starts, requests and handovers are blocked, only one cancel, one release, four allocations and the chapter controls remain, so roughly twenty more records can ever be written.

   **Simpler control.** The original Rain control validates the same window facts with local invariants and no journal, see `commons-next.js:32-43`: one receipt per cache, receipts at least one minimum cache duration apart, allocations after their cache, households only before the ferry, at most four. The kernel already floors paid minutes per cache at `camp.js:376` and conserves material at `camp.js:369`. Keep the root for the milestone-at-entry pin at `camp-story.js:73`, drop the command list, and validate the window the Rain way. What is lost is in-window edit detection, which the contract already disclaims for every other phase.

2. **Post-return replay is permanent dead weight.** After return, every restore re-migrates the root and replays the whole window with one-minute kernel advances, see `camp-story.js:152-153`. Each kernel advance validates twice at `camp.js:231`. Each validation re-restores the legacy origin at `camp.js:311`. The reconstructed world is used only for monotone checks. If the journal stays, the smallest fix is to store the settled kernel export at return, clear the commands, and compare against that stored world.

3. **Local failure is a ledger with no downstream use.** Unprovided households and nights persist only as view arithmetic at `camp-story.js:181-182`. Allocation is rejected after return, the window cannot reopen, and the kernel's cache count and commitment baseline ignore allocations. The contract is honest about this. But it means the story delivers what a playlist would: new kernel to milestone, then the Rain control with its fixed opening replaced by the exported milestone world at `commons-next.js:27`. Return could be a plain kernel export plus one frozen outcome field.

4. **Window stakes ignore entry state.** A legacy entry carries every existing cache as allocatable, see `camp-story.js:54`. The contract's twelve-cache acceptance case cannot fail the window. That is defensible for earned surplus but should be stated as a property rather than left under "distinct local failure".

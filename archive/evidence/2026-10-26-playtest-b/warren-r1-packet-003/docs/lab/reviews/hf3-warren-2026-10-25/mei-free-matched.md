## 4. Moments
- **Best (227–245 s):** the stone door was carved ("Meryem rolled it once to test it"). All six sleeping places were filled, and the game confirmed my guess about the bell.
- **Where it lost me:** from about 285 s on. Nothing was left to decide, and the same journal lines repeated every day.
- **Want-quit:** one, at 416 s, BORED: "I have done what the game asked and now I sit through days of the same journal lines… with no date for the riders and nothing new to decide…" It did not pass. Steady speed showed me nothing new.
- **Pulses:** 172 s, engagement 3: "Each step answers with a clear sentence and I like that, but four days have gone in three minutes and now water is dry with no well tool I can see." 332 s, engagement 3: "…I am watching rather than deciding." There was no third pulse before I stopped.

## 5. Confusion and clarity
- **The unreachable warning:** "Nobody can reach that stone yet" appeared even for chalked squares that joined my earlier ones. The picture showed them marked. I never worked out what the warning meant.
- **Who shuts the door:** this became clear when I placed beds. The game said a nearby adult may shut the door when the bell rings, and that false alarms weaken trust.
- **How deep is safe:** never clear. Three squares down was still "reachable from the entrance."
- **Warnings I could not act on:** dry water and empty jars. I had no tool for either. The water warning cleared by itself and the jars warning stayed.
- **The clock:** "Night · 27 Aug" came before "Afternoon · 27 Aug". My guess is that Night meant before dawn.
- **Time passing:** about one day passed per 20–30 s wait, even on Slow. Reading panels paused time.
- **Sleeping:** at night people slept in the houses, not in the beds I had marked.

## 5. Confusion and what became clear
**Confusing:**
- Halil's answer only appeared after I pressed "Let time pass".
- I often couldn't tell whether the clock was running or stopped.
- The jumps were uneven: the clock went from 14:06 to 19:13 in 20 real seconds, but the tea-house scene barely moved in 30 seconds.
- I never understood what "chose something else" means. It appeared next to "he called today", and the in-game help doesn't cover it.
- In the skip summary the shift showed "said yes once and never asked you to wait". I couldn't see why it only happened once.
- "Keep listening" was covered by the speaking sheet.
- Hacer's lines about Osman disappeared after I spoke.

**Became clear:**
- My suggestions stand until a stated time, and he defers with "after I finish this task".
- Urge plus a reason can flip his likely reply.
- The real puzzle is getting Halil to choose things himself before Eid.
- The Today log holds things I missed live, such as Hacer's paper rooms and her gossip.

## Fix priorities supported by the actual asks

Show relevant action windows when planning, not only in a later log. Preserve his freedom to refuse or choose differently; no guaranteed obedience, cost removal or difficulty reduction is licensed.
2. **Let the player see the consequential moments and hear distinct people.** The Nuran memory, Selin call, quiet company, doctor and earned work can hold interest. Several diaries then find important rent/payment events in a side panel or meet the same friend line again. The exact asks retain their alternatives and conditions: visible payoff, replies where speech occurred, distinct authored lines, and a clear warning before replacing a standing suggestion. One good new line does not erase a repetition complaint; a repetition complaint does not prove there are no distinct lines.

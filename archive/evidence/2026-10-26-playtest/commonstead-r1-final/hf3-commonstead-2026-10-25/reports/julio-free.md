## 5. Confusion and what became clear
**Confusing:**
- Tapping the storehouse with Sam selected just cleared my selection.
- With Dai selected, the Workshop hub panel took over the action buttons.
- The "Finish the storehouse" warning appeared after the storehouse was already finished.
- The tally said 3 buildings when I counted 2.
- The helper cost 35 meals and I never learned what she was for.
- Everyone kept saying "I am tired" and I could not see what that cost me.

**Became clear:**
- Selections add up and Clear resets them.
- Right-clicking a destination gives the order.
- Breaks lead to conversations and then a small collaboration.
- The clock runs about one game-minute per real second on Steady.

# App 0.15 — Three moments

Requested 2026-09-14 after the connected-person milestone: a tiny focused simulation showing the framework's progress. Public route: [human.adamwhite.work/person/](https://human.adamwhite.work/person/).

## Delivered behavior

Three moments gives the player three decisions through one persistent person: use a learned method, handle a household responsibility, then coordinate with a colleague. It uses the existing situated-person 0.1.0 candidate and Human 0.1.1 execution component without changing either. The new showcase host has its own version 0.1.0.

Each scene shows current knowledge and circumstances, two action choices where available, their declared costs, and the shared baseline's suggested action. The player may choose differently. Outcomes come from actual attempts and explicit host receipts; the housemate and colleague respond to delivered observations. Completing a run shows what carried forward and offers a replay without one earlier experience. The comparison describes the authored baseline, not a prediction of the player's choice.

The homepage features this simulation first. Existing public examples remain available. The public allowlist adds only the two person modules, one showcase host and its three UI files. Private reports, original sequence/comparison sources, packaging tools and tarballs stay unserved.

## Scope

This is three dated episodes across two weeks, with thirty modeled activity minutes. Gaps do not simulate sleep, recovery, retention or aging. Facts and interaction evidence are attributed records; purposes and decision rules remain authored. General scientific/theological limitations appear in one collapsed Model notes section. Ordinary play explains choices and consequences.

The user authorized this focused showcase; it does not restart old game development queues or scheduled work. The framework remains the product. The next substantive modeling gap is continuous daily condition and retained learning across these situations.

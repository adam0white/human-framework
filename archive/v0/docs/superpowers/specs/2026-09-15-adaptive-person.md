# Persistent adaptation and constrained opportunity

The user authorizes continued autonomous framework progress; games and scheduling remain deferred. This architectural increment addresses the next roadmap gaps in order: paid cue-linked habit formation, evidence-supported voluntary purpose revision, functional limitations/accommodated methods, and canonical institutional allocation. All are private authored engineering candidates.

## Actor adaptation

A new adaptive composition wraps the frozen developing person. Cue and active purpose are captured at actual attempt start; actual paid completion/failure/interruption updates a bounded habit/evidence module. Repeated completed cue/action execution can suggest that action; the actor retains choice and unavailable actions cannot be invented. Passive time, failed preflight, other cues and duplicate receipts earn nothing. Habit suggestion is not proficiency, virtue or an obligation.

Repeated actual failed attempts for a configured active purpose can offer a revision toward a configured withdrawn alternative. A completed paid review and explicitly selected acceptance let the wrapper change statuses through existing situated setPurpose. No second purpose owner; no text interpretation or automatic replacement of the actor's values. Failed evidence is consumed for a revision so later reactivation cannot reuse it indefinitely.

## Functional method constraints

The host owns task-specific demand restrictions and their reassessment records. A method declaring a restricted demand is refused before Human begins; no phantom blocked Human attempt, time or practice is created. An alternative method can avoid that demand but pays its own duration and resource costs. A review date does not cure a restriction. Actor notices are explicit detached deliveries and can be absent or stale; canonical feasibility remains host-owned. This is a functional access representation, not diagnosis or a calibrated health model.

## Institutions

One host-owned facility has explicit grants, capacity one, FIFO requests and time-bounded reservations. One actor's request/release/withdrawal/revocation can change another's opportunity. Actor qualifications do not grant institutional permission, reservations do not execute work, and actors only know delivered offers. Large versus segmented time advances must resolve the same expiries and promotions at their actual times. No general claim of fairness, legitimacy, employment or macro behavior.

## Acceptance and boundaries

Use actual paid attempts, direct equally informed counters/gates/queues, changed-cue/inactive-purpose/absent-review controls, stale notices, accommodation resource absence, review expiry, two-actor allocation, save/restore and independent installed reuse. Preserve negative/direct parity results. Body/clock/purpose owners stay unchanged. Each new module is version0.1.0 with explicit bounds and JSON persistence; existing source/package/public identities remain frozen. Coherently rewritten saves remain host-trusted, not authenticated historical evidence.

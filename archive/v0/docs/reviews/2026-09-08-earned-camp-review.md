Initial regression logs, corrected outputs, probes and source snapshots remain private artifacts. ## Fable's scope and the simpler alternative

One focused Fable call, launched before the user requested reduced Fable usage, completed on actual `claude-fable-5-1` in 709 words, with auxiliary Haiku use. It inspected five frozen new-host/contract and old-Rain files, without UI, full Human runtime, tests, datasets, prior reviews, original user feedback or player saves; it executed nothing. [Result](../../research/reviews/2026-09-08-camp-story-fable.md) · [Exact model/source provenance](../../research/reviews/2026-09-08-camp-story-fable-provenance.json).

It challenged whether the journal earns its complexity. Root commissioned a bounded registered [local-invariant rival](../camp-validator.md), without another simulation engine or any physics tuning. Both validators accept sixteen legal cases. Replay catches four additional specific recorded-history/world mismatches; both retain weaker pre-entry/post-Return snapshot claims. Local cold validation is much faster, about 0.5–1.3 ms in the selected window cases versus up to 117.7 ms for replay. Even a five-command ended window replays 180 paid minutes. The journal is retained for this narrow command/order/completion integrity contract, not because it is necessary for continuity itself or proven to improve human enjoyment. General campaign/replay machinery is not promoted.

The slot adapter separately removes redundant revalidation of deeply frozen, already-proven books. All external/inactive saves still validate. Ordinary measured save medians fall to about 1–4 ms, but cold-load costs remain explicit. This is a measured optimization, not a reason to hide the weaker snapshot boundaries or claim every future game needs a journal.

Two small Fable findings were confirmed and corrected: default automatic recovery now rejects a redundant unadvertised rest command, and closed windows no longer advertise work against their old deadlines. Source tests fail before and pass after the corrections; independent final delta checks are retained. Its fixed-gather release alternative remains a disclosed future design choice: this version explicitly stops accepted project work, preserves construction fractions, and loses unfinished fixed-trip output. We do not claim general partial-trip modeling. Its observation that rich imported camps can satisfy the window is intentionally retained; earned surplus is not confiscated to force difficulty. Provisioned nights and household outcomes still need a genuine later use in future story content.

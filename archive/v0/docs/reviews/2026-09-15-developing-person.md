# Developing-person review disposition

Three focused subagents used GPT-5.6-sol at medium effort for separate relationship, duty/source and adult-course work. Review crossed ownership: the duty/source worker reviewed relationships and appraisal; the relationship worker reviewed adult course and appraisal; the adult worker reviewed duty and integration. The lead owned integration, final verification and disposition. Private material was not sent to external review CLIs.

## Fixed

- Duty history was incorrectly required to have the current delivery time after a clock advance. Insertion remains current-time-only; historical receipts validate at their own times. A notice/advance/outcome/advance/restore regression covers the lifecycle.
- Any notice text previously activated repair. Explicit accepted/uncertain/rejected application now controls the reference policy; private authored understanding remains distinct from the notice source.
- Relationship insertion accepted backdated newly delivered reports. New receipt time must be current; historical occurrence and saved receipt times remain preserved.
- Adult restore did not require an owner and accepted accessor array entries. Owner binding and data-only array checks now reject those snapshots.
- The initial host injected a qualification without an executed assessment. A paid assessment attempt and host-attested threshold result now precede the award. This remains an authored assessment, not an empirical credential claim.
- Adult action sequences initially omitted recovery and could hit real capacity rejection. Explicit paid rest precedes each adult activity. No effects are credited to blocked work.
- Recipient response initially stayed only in recipient relationship state. The host separately communicates an accepted/deferred response to the learner, without changing the physical restitution amount.
- A paid check initially impersonated the colleague's correction. It now records the traveler's own observation; the colleague's report persists and disagreement can remain unresolved.
- Appraisal initially rejected actual Human attempt IDs. It now accepts and owner-binds Human's `actor:ordinal` IDs.
- Appraisal restore accepted deliberate tendency without paid evidence. A matching receipt with concern provenance and a paid interval inside that concern is now required.
- A permanent seen-signature set suppressed concern after resolution followed by real retraction. Last semantic-state tracking now permits a new uncertainty episode while identical reappraisal/relay copies cannot renew an expired one.
- Appraisal data arrays rejected neither accessors nor some inconsistent belief views. Strict data descriptors and status/support consistency checks now cover these cases; the receipt-array bound matches the underlying ledger.

## Reuse follow-through

The consumers exposed a fifth implementation gap: duplicated identity/clock synchronization and caller-assembled regulation payment. A composite lifecycle now owns synchronization and derives the paid receipt from its actual finished attempt. World outcomes remain external. Reference and installed consumers use this boundary; pending commands and sparse calendar changes are explicitly restricted. No human authoring metric is inferred from removing duplicated calls. Review required projection of adult assessment inputs for actors with several skills, raw composite validation before snapshot reconstruction, and clearing last-attempt eligibility across calendar gaps. The saved last-attempt record remains host-trusted: changing a coherent snapshot cannot be detected from frozen Human post-finish state. This is documented rather than claiming cryptographic or historical authentication. Split advancement uses the frozen Human body tolerance of 1e-12; exact replay follows identical command segmentation.

## Explicit contracts and limits

Relationship `originId` identifies a directed semantic event. Repair links use retained local IDs; a host must resolve an ignored relay to the retained event. Outcome IDs identify a partner/context proposition rather than an unrestricted world event. Only direct recipient acknowledgment is supported, not forwarded acknowledgment authority. These bounded choices are documented, not silently generalized.

Sparse adult gaps carry no simulated physiology. Subsequent modeled actions retain the software person and use explicit recovery, but their body projections are not longitudinal observations after a year. The source memo was corrected to the actual one-item return case, with no unmodeled damage claim.

Unknown and conflicting evidence can both suggest checking. The additional resolved-report control demonstrates a causal action difference under identical world physics. Appraisal remains an authored tendency interface; caller-selected actions can override it. Reflection never resolves a belief or proves a mental state.

The direct notebook and concern/timer controls match all authored comparison cases. Independent installation establishes exported portability, not human authoring savings. No qualified scholarly review, general psychological calibration, clinical claim, or full-framework completion is asserted.

# A validation program for Human Framework

Prepared 2026-09-06; sequencing revised 2026-09-07 after [independent review](post-mvp-review.md). This is a proposed validation program. The [MVP record](mvp-status.md) and [benchmark report](benchmark-report.md) separately identify executed checks; a similarly named proposed test is not automatically complete. Passing engineering or play tests would not establish empirical realism or theological completeness. The proposal is in [framework-proposal.md](framework-proposal.md).

## A later situation that tests the combined mechanisms

This retains the original research example. It follows the one-worker host integration in the revised sequence; it does not require several adults or two settings in the next implementation.

A tired person has promised to help someone. A report suggests danger; the report might be wrong. Food, time and assistance are limited. The person has learned skills, relationships, reasons to care, competing desires, and a capacity to choose.

This small situation can test body–mind coupling, belief error, competing needs, commitment, intention, ability, social context and learning without requiring a simulated civilization. Begin with a few adults and explicitly authored histories. Childhood, clinical conditions, long-term aging and detailed bodily systems remain declared unsupported areas in the demonstrator, with coverage entries and later research milestones. This bounds the first experiment, not the eventual framework.

Render it in two contrasting settings: tactical defense and a cooperative workshop or expedition. The first emphasizes immediate action and uncertain scouting; the second emphasizes scheduling, tool use, communication and obligations. These are test fixtures, not a permanent choice of product genre. Survival RPG and settlement strategy are alternative adapters described in the architecture memo.

## Compare models before combining them

Use the same initial world, observation-access rules, action vocabulary, authored histories, exogenous-disturbance scheme, outcome definitions and comparable budgets for three candidates:

1. A compact trait/skill plus utility baseline.
2. A modular body–belief–commitment–learning model.
3. A competing deliberation module, such as active inference or a selected cognitive-architecture implementation, only where it makes distinct predictions.

Realized observations may differ because agents take different actions or gather different information. Fixed observation streams are appropriate only for explicitly passive or recorded-stream comparisons. For active sensing and information seeking, compare closed-loop runs under equivalent access and cost rules; record observation acquisition as part of behavior. Do not give one model privileged world knowledge, extra actions or an unlimited planning budget. Start with a frozen calibration scenario set; then evaluate held-out situations, altered testimony, resource changes, and the second adapter. Record every exception and parameter changed. A genre-specific outcome model may change; a supposedly shared human-learning mechanism cannot be silently retuned and still count as unchanged transfer.

## Engineering and representational checks

| Experiment | Intervention | What would count against the design |
|---|---|---|
| Authored agency | Keep state fixed, supply two different feasible player choices | Policy silently substitutes its preferred action, or moral approval is used as physical feasibility |
| Intention versus outcome | Supply the same outward act with different declared intentions; separately alter chance of success | Intention is inferred from success/reputation, or other characters see private intention automatically |
| Intrusive thought and coercion | Vary suggestion, deliberative assent, coercion and conduct independently | Every thought becomes a chosen act, or compulsion and available alternatives disappear from the record |
| Perception versus reality | Independently vary actual danger and received testimony | The character reacts to unseen truth without a modeled observation or inference pathway |
| Hidden-state access | Change unseen locks or actual fatigue while holding deliberatively available evidence fixed | Policy reads hidden state directly, or free validation rejections reveal obstacles without action/observation cost; declared bodily effects remain allowed |
| Social opportunity | Hold personal capacities fixed, change tool access, support, institutional permission or physical accessibility | Failure is recorded as reduced intrinsic ability or poor character rather than the relevant barrier |
| Need coexistence | Impose hunger or fatigue while retaining meaningful commitments | Higher commitments vanish automatically, or intention magically cancels bodily limits |
| Spiritual representation | Vary outward practice, intention, distress and worldly outcome separately | A single piety score dictates all four, or distress implies weak faith/divine disapproval |
| Transfer accounting | Add circular and unsupported skill links | Transferred XP recursively produces more XP, unrelated practice grants universal improvement, or costs are counted twice |
| Exact replay | Replay the same model, numerical mode, schedule and logged inputs | Recorded choices and states differ outside the declared numerical reproducibility contract |
| Random-stream separation | Perturb an unrelated module's random stream | Another module changes without a causal pathway or a declared coupling |
| Numerical refinement | Reduce integration step size under a declared disturbance-coupling scheme | Continuous trajectories or outcome distributions fail declared convergence tolerances; identical discrete choices are not required merely because steps were refined |
| Module ablation | Remove one claimed mechanism while keeping other inputs fixed | It has no discriminating effect or shifts every unrelated outcome without explanation |
| LLM independence | Disable generation and replay cached proposals | Canonical memory, physical outcomes or private intentions are invented, lost, or changed by prose |
| Cross-genre reuse | Add the second adapter after freezing common contracts | Shared mechanisms accumulate genre-name conditionals or hidden redefinitions of existing variables |
| Representation invariance | Convert all task quantities and the declared value conversion consistently | Identical physical situations change policy merely because progress uses different units |
| Host integration and persistence | Deliver an external observation/outcome; save and resume an interrupted attempt; redeliver a completed event | Unrecorded host effects disappear on replay, one outcome applies twice, or the host must edit private human state |

Acceptance rules fixed before implementation: supplied valid choices remain recorded as supplied; private facts travel only through declared interfaces; each resource debit has one owner; transfer credits originate once from direct practice; unsupported operations report their status; replay records all nondeterministic inputs. Numerical tolerances, disturbance coupling and performance budgets must be specified with the actual integrator and target hardware, before viewing benchmark results. For stochastic refinement, specify whether comparison is pathwise under coupled disturbances or distributional; a changed step schedule is not an exact replay.

## Empirical validation is a separate track

Synthetic tests above detect internal contradictions. To claim a model explains real people, reproduce a **specific evidence-backed task** under matched conditions and compare predictions with observed data. Suitable initial candidates from the empirical memo include sleep restriction and performance, practice/retention on a selected skill, or information seeking in a bounded choice task. Do not claim all three from one parameter fit.

For a first empirical task, define the population, outcome, measurement procedure and available data. Separate calibration from held-out evaluation. Check probability calibration/log loss or continuous error as appropriate, compare with a simple baseline, report uncertainty and parameter identifiability, and test a competing causal explanation. Evidence that several parameter combinations fit equally well should reduce mechanistic confidence. No numerical success threshold is fabricated before the data and measurement scale are known.

Prediction improvement, causal plausibility, and interpretability are different outcomes. A complicated model that predicts no better may still fulfill a necessary representation, but that is a design benefit rather than empirical confirmation. Test explicit interventions when causal claims matter; fitting a correlation does not establish the direction of causation.

Culture, disability, age, gender, education and circumstance define applicability questions, not automatic parameter presets. Test measurement and model portability before generalizing across groups. Do not synthesize demographic moral or intelligence rankings from this framework.

## Theological evaluation is also distinct

For each spiritual or moral mechanism, present a qualified Hanafi–Maturidi reviewer with the exact source, translation, interpretive argument, proposed variable/transition, example, and counterexample. Ask whether it preserves the source's meaning, whether a disputed position is labeled, and whether the model claims more than the evidence permits.

Prioritize these cases: voluntary choice and acquisition, involuntary thought versus assent, intention versus outcome, capacity and coercion, repentance without guaranteed worldly reward, and spiritual struggle without a soul meter. Review should include positive representation of worship and purpose, not merely prohibitions on representation. An engine trace can demonstrate consistent implementation of an interpretation; it cannot verify divine acceptance or settle theology by simulation.

## Play and usefulness tests

Use a simple interface with a small number of choices and an optional causal inspector. Ask players to explain what they believed, why an outcome occurred, and what they could reasonably try next. Test whether uncertain reports are interpretable, whether characters have recognizable histories, and whether trade-offs produce meaningful decisions without exposing dozens of stats.

Measure comprehension, perceived choice, investigation success, authoring time and runtime cost separately. Believability is useful but can be produced by stereotypes or persuasive narration. An explanation shown to the player must be traceable to the actual model, and must not leak facts the viewpoint cannot know.

## Sequence and expansion criteria

| Stage | Concrete output | Expansion condition |
|---|---|---|
| Existing laboratory checkpoint | Versioned 0.2.0 executable, four aggregate-task presets, benchmark and historical replays | Preserve controls; do not count themes as independent game integration |
| Contract and evaluation repair | Explicit task units/value, competent simple comparator, fixed-world policy comparisons and controlled-action mechanism tests | Representational invariance, equal information access and meaningful contrary cases |
| First host integration | One worker in a host-owned object/location game, documented observations/outcomes, persistence and optional inspector | Host controls its world and scheduling; interrupted resume, duplicate-event accounting and bounded active state pass |
| Independent consumer and game usefulness | A second interaction authored against a frozen boundary; simple host-native comparison; measured authoring/runtime/play results | No host-specific core edits or duplicated human effects; useful benefit for the declared game |
| One additional mechanism | A social response, evidence revision or learning experiment required by the host | A serious rival and reserved condition families can discriminate the change; Solo remains a control |
| Human calibration and theological review, separate parallel tracks | A bounded dataset and prediction report; or exact source-to-representation review with examples/counterexamples | Evidence supports the respective narrow empirical or interpretive claim; neither is inferred from game wins |

This replaces the original order that put engine integration after calibration and broader coverage. Choose language, scheduling and interfaces from the first host's needs rather than the historical proposals. The current JavaScript/browser runtime is sufficient to attempt that small consumer; multiple native-engine bindings are deferred. These are gates for subsequent implementation, not claims that this review delivered the integration.

## What would cause a change of direction

Prefer the simpler baseline if the hybrid adds complexity without useful coverage or discriminating predictions. Narrow the claimed domain if evidence fails to transport. Split a module if its concepts refer to incompatible quantities. Remove unsupported transfer relations. Preserve alternative interpretation records when a doctrinal mapping remains disputed. If the cross-genre tests require rewriting the kernel, revisit the interface rather than merely calling it reusable.

The desired next result is modest and concrete: demonstrate that one small causal framework can produce understandable, consequential choices in two settings, with uncertainty and provenance intact. It is the first test of the larger ambition, not a substitute for it.

## Playable changes

**[Last Light](https://human.adamwhite.work/signals/)** asks the player to deliver a replacement lens before the beacon closes at minute 32; arrival before 12 also lets the evening launch sail. A reliable ridge uses more effort and time; a canal crossing uses a fare and needs an open landing on arrival. Paid radio requests and direct lookout observations can disagree because reports arrive after the world changes. The public notebook keeps the newest observed report, with sources/timestamps and older late replies visible. [Game and comparison](signals-game.md).

The original four harbors preserve the safe immediate-ridge and blind-canal/fallback baselines. The original radio's earliest informed canal arrival misses the launch, and the fresh carrier does not need recovery; neither limitation is concealed. Separate **Clear connection**, **After the long shift** and **Without lunch** profiles make shorter reply transit, paid rest and a completed meal consequential through unchanged Human mechanics. Ten traced routes preserve refusals, interrupted-meal costs, later arrivals and a faster blind route. All twelve original comparison results and original artifacts remain exact. [Profiles and evidence](signals-profiles.md).

**Save a play note** is now a collapsed optional form in Last Light, Watch and Before the rain. It downloads the player's words, timestamp and explicitly selected public summary. It does not score, identify participants, submit to a server, gather a full save or establish understanding. Draft lifetime and separate save export are explained. All automated note fixtures are labeled synthetic and count as browser QA only. [Play-note contract](play-notes.md).

The previous Watch/rain solutions and original Common Ground control remain executable. Public changes to those pages are presentation-only; their actor behavior and save formats are unchanged. Last Light uses a new host 0.1.0/save 1 around Human/runtime 0.1.1 and clock 0.1.0. The laboratory remains 0.3.0. Frozen Human versions, transitive model and runtime-release locks were not changed.

## Framework decisions

- **Coordination helper:** both actual host adaptations preserve 2,754 paired steps, 10,000 delivered events, full states and serialized saves. Seven mechanical obligations move out of callers, but total source increases: original measurement +335 bytes/20 nonblank lines, reviewed identity-guard version **+510 bytes/21 lines**. Keep direct wiring and the candidate private. This small size margin and related-host ancestry do not establish poor human usability in general. [Report](coordination-probe.md).
- **Body isolation:** pooled stamina with the identical saturating practice function falls within declared tolerance in **13/25 diagnostic pairs**. Equal-load people can require opposite authored food/rest choices with two channels, while the pooled state aliases them. Matched learning checks are expected identities, disaggregated into **702 zero-exposure, 249 immediately after shared practice, and 438 retained-positive-exposure checks**. No curve confound, human physiology claim or empirical learning validation follows. [Report](body-isolation.md).
- **Report representation:** in twelve authored Last Light cases, notebook/candidate/no-retention providers deliver the lens **11/10/10** times, enable **2/2/1** launches and incur **1/2/1** failed crossings. Both paid exposure and offered options match. Candidate expiry cannot bind; stale delivery-order replacement can matter. The smaller notebook stays in this host and the general candidate remains private. This is not a human-memory study or a global policy ranking.

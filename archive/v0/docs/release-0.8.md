## What changed

Protect the inlet at minute 24, then supply the clinic before 64. Gate repairs preserve morning water but consume parts and effort; diversion protects the inlet with less work, loses morning water and requires a paid reopening. The clinic can receive two piped units or one cart unit. The same two people, owned parts, meals, fatigue, practice and paid partial work carry between obligations. No body reset or replacement inventory occurs. Deniz keeps an independent clinic commitment and may refuse incompatible requests or cancellation of self-chosen work. [Host design](service-day-design.md) · [UI contract](service-day-ui.md).

The visible day supports concurrent requests/work, short paid rests, part reservations/handovers, optional local notes, exact save/download/import and event-by-event or paced time. Reload and tab hiding pause play. The interface explains the strict closing boundary, mechanically optional keeper meal, and sole clinic collection slot. Deniz may choose a cart from 42 without accounting for keeper's remaining plan; that can foreclose a feasible piped delivery. This authored limitation is explicit and preserved for the next coordination experiment.

Service Day has a new host 0.1.0/save 1 contract around Human/runtime 0.1.1 and clock 0.1.0. Existing games, Solo Repair's social isolation, historical engines, transitive model and runtime-release lock remain unchanged. General planning, memory or social cognition is not inferred from these local rules.

## What the comparison establishes

Across 18 frozen runs over nine conditions, both simple public-view priorities protect the inlet and deliver two clinic units. They differ in morning water, materials and paid work. Deadline-first wastes 14 extra work minutes plus 9 rest minutes in the interrupted-diversion case for the same service outcomes; the negative result remains. This is a controller/carryover comparison under the same Human physics, not a new body-model comparison or optimum-plan claim. [Full report](service-day-comparison.md).

Matched earlier gate/diversion choices retain real body/inventory differences. At 24 both pump requests are busy, although their capacity forecasts differ. A common later controller gives the gate branch one cart unit at 60 and diversion two piped units at 45; the missed second gate-branch unit specifically involves Deniz's minute 42 fallback preempting keeper's work. Stronger early coordination can still complete both gate-route services. The standard-day keeper meal is physically unnecessary, and common partner scheduling/elective recovery influence timing/costs. These limitations are recorded without retuning frozen policies.

Three manifests distinguish original frozen results from later host validation. The final 302,079-byte compact artifact binds its runner, replays all 18 trials and both continuations, and matches original final saves/outcomes. Original large trajectories and policy quirks remain private, immutable evidence. No new general faculty was promoted.

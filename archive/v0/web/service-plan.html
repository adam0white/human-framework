    How this day works

    Gate: 12 minutes, 2 parts. A diversion: 6 minutes, 1 part; it protects the inlet but loses morning water. Reopen it after the surge in 3 minutes.

    Clinic pump: 12 minutes, 2 parts. Pipe delivery: 6 minutes for 2 units. Cart: 18 minutes for 1 unit. The clinic has one receiving slot. Starting either trip commits it immediately. Stopping that trip forfeits the slot; no replacement delivery can use it. A delivery finishing at minute 64 is too late.

    Everyone owns their supplies. Fetch a shed part in 8 minutes or hand over one of your parts in 2. Installed work stays when stopped; unused reserved parts return. Rest can be stopped early; a meal must finish.

    An accepted plan can change Deniz’s waiting time. A proposed revision changes nothing until Deniz accepts it. A refused or interrupted discussion leaves the current terms in place. Your promise is separate from actual work and from Deniz’s clinic obligation.

    Play pauses at visible events, on leaving this tab, and after reload. There is no offline progress. +1 min lets you stop mid-task. Reading, choosing and writing a play note pause time.

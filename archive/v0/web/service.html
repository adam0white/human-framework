        How work and time carry forward

        Repair both gate sections (12 minutes, 2 parts) to protect the inlet and keep morning water service. A diversion (6 minutes, 1 part) protects the inlet but stops morning water. Reopening after minute 24 costs 3 minutes. If the inlet floods, finishing the gate later can still restore clinic supply; the missed morning service remains lost.

        The clinic needs two units. Repair its pump (12 minutes, 2 parts), then deliver through an open inlet (6 minutes). A cart (18 minutes) supplies one unit without pump parts or inlet service. The clinic makes one collection: the cart commits that slot and cannot be topped up later. One shed part takes 8 minutes to fetch. Each person owns their parts and meal; a handover takes 2 minutes.

        Parts install on the first paid minute of each section. Installed work remains when stopped; unused reserved supplies return. A meal takes 4 full minutes to finish. A 6-minute rest can be stopped early with its paid recovery retained.

        1× advances one simulated minute per second. Play pauses at each visible event, when you leave this tab, and after reload. There is no offline progress. +1 min can stop partway through recovery or work. A gate-work minute ending at the surge counts; a delivery finishing at minute 64 is too late.

        Deniz has their own clinic commitment. Requests can be refused, and they choose clinic work or recovery when time advances. Their current reason is visible. From minute 42, Deniz may choose a cart when they cannot repair or deliver themselves. Their self-chosen cart cannot be canceled and can prevent a later pipe delivery even if your remaining pump work would finish in time. A refused request uses no time. Reading or writing a play note pauses the day.

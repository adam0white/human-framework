A few things to try

Alternate carrying water and filling your barrel. Your cans hold six, and you can pour three in one move. A careful trip never spills; a quick lift may save a move but takes more strength.

Meryem can refuse a gift if her cans are full. If you borrow, leave time and two buckets for the return. Filling your own barrel and helping hers are separate outcomes.

    Model notes

    This is an authored small game, not a calibrated explanation of people. Work and recovery use the shared body/practice component. Meryem uses structured rules, without an LLM or hidden-state inference. Gifts and loan records describe exchanges; they are not moral-worth, faith or divine-approval scores. The project’s Islamic starting point does not supply this game’s numerical rules.

    Turns are atomic: your choice settles first, then Meryem responds to what she can observe. The active save retains the current world and last turn; the optional replay retains choices separately.

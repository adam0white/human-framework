Hi there!

You are going play a new game today.

It is called: Lolli-toss.



In the game of Lolli-toss, you will see a board.

The goal of this game is to toss sticky lollipops toward the board and gain the most points.


How do you get the most points?

If you hit the lollipop at the center of the board (the yellow region), you will gain 50 points.


Aside from the bullseye, if you hit the red ring, you will get 30 points. Hitting anywhere within the blue ring is worth 20 points. The black ring is worth 10 points. And, if you hit the white ring, you will get 5 points. However, if you missed the board, you will get 0 point.

Hitting the center will get you the most points on a given round. But as long as you hit the board, you will get some points as well.



Okay, you already know what scores you will get by hitting the board.

Now we will introduce you to the sticky lollipops you will be using in this game.

To make this game more fun, the lollipop will be moving sideways on the screen:







The lollipop will sometime starts from the left side, and sometimes from the right side.

It will go back and forth for 5 times before coming to a stopping position.


In this game, there are two keys you will use: the space bar and the enter key.


* Space bar *

You will hit the space bar to stop the lollipop from moving sideways.

Please press space now to confirm that you can use it in this game.



* Enter (Return) key *

Then, you will use the enter key to toss lollipops.

The duration you hold the enter key will decide how far your lollipop is tossed.

The longer you hold the enter key down, the farther the lollipop will go.

Please press enter /return now to confirm that you can use it in the game.

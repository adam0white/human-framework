## Current direction and stopping point

**The framework is the product.** Its full goal is situated human action and development: an embodied person perceives imperfectly, understands through knowledge and experience, has purposes and obligations, chooses, acts, and changes through consequences. The same mechanisms should support different environments and eventually longer lifespans and social settings. The current body/action/time kit is a useful intermediate deliverable; the broader person model remains early.

The project is grounded in Islam with a Sunni Hanafi–Maturidi starting point and informed by empirical research. Preserve agency and distinct source/interpretation/empirical/model/engineering claims. Positive representation of meaning, worship, service, reflection and repair remains part of the ambition. No soul meter, divine-acceptance score or LLM-dependent actor loop is planned.

**Leave games aside for now. The scheduled task remains paused.** The user requested documentation and a remote push after reviewing the broader gap. This does not resume automatic work or select the next implementation. The laboratory stays retired; prior permission to replace experiments and break old saves applies when application work is requested again. No mandatory campaign, all-game tutorial or indefinite migration/route support is required.

The [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md) recommends sustained adults over days/weeks, then deeper knowledge/purposes/relationships, adult development and aging, and broader reuse/social scale. This population/horizon and the individual mechanisms are not yet selected. Social context and positive moral/spiritual work belong alongside development, not at the end of a physiology queue. Three anchor human situations, horizon priority and suitable scholarly review are useful next discussion inputs; no new implementation or experiment is queued.

The interrupted Camp reconsideration study is complete. No pending agent lane remains. Keep its candidate private and its negative/positive results intact. Do not restart completed studies, tune a paid-time cutoff, refit the learning pilot, or add another game from an obsolete “next” paragraph.

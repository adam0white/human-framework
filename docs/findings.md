## HF 4.0.1 step cost (2026-10-27)

Scratch measurements on one Apple Silicon machine while other agents ran (load average 9 to 45, stated per pair). Each pair alternates 4.0.0 and 4.0.1 on the same inputs. They are exploratory engineering proxies from one matched pair or a best of three, not stable speedup estimates or a budget proof. Harnesses, raw output and commands are kept outside the repository in `.lab-scratch/hf4/hf401-receipts/`. An independent review reproduced flat, cheaper fixed step cost and identical hashes on its own engine control, but not the game figures.

- **The diagnosis was wrong.** A 4.0.0 `step` does not copy, normalize or hash the whole run: history is an append node, a commit clones only the new transaction, and hashes are computed only by `exportRun` and replay. A 50 ms step costs the same after eight simulated hours as at the start. The time went to fixed per-step work: two physiology probes per processed hazard frame (32% of a Smoke night), the retained and physics probes, the end-of-step actor check, memory re-validation inside `memory.advance`, a per-delivery id hash and a RegExp built on every id parse.
- **Cuts in 4.0.1, all neutral.** Hazard draining skips its probes when nothing they read has changed since the last quiet probe. Memory validated on entry is not validated again. The validators build an issue path only when they report one. `sha256` reuses one schedule buffer. Stream origins and per-span id hashes are memoized. Replay reads the newest history node, not the whole history per step, which removes a quadratic term. Across 20 Smoke nights (5 bot strategies, seeds 1–4) and the Child proxy below, `hashes.current` and `hashes.history` are byte-identical to 4.0.0.
- **Smoke frame (Chromium 4x CPU throttle, pushy seed 1 log, frames from tick 1600, 198 requests in both runs, load 24–29):** whole-frame p95 25.0 ms on 4.0.0 and 16.6 ms on 4.0.1; frames over 16 ms fell from 199 to 101 of 1,600. A second pair was noisier (25.4 and 20.7 ms; the 4.0.1 run took fewer requests). 4.0.1 still misses the 16 ms budget at p95.
- **Smoke in Node (one pushy night, CPU time, best of three, load 36–42):** 2,747 ms on 4.0.0 and 1,805 ms on 4.0.1; a busy tick's p50 fell from 4.4 to 2.8 ms.
- **Smoke bot test.** Twenty nights (seeds 1–4) of play, export and replay took 29–42 s of CPU on 4.0.0 and 21 s on 4.0.1; replay costs about as much as play. The full 100-night test is therefore still about 100 s against its 30 s per-test limit.

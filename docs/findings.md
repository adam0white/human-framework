# Findings

Short records of results that cut against expectations. Keep them; do not tune them away silently.

- **2026-10-03 — Twice at the Well, solo beats the script.** On the shipped seed the Human village left alone (no orders) stores more meals (19–20) than under the director's scripted orders (11), with equal house progress. The people's own priorities beat the tutorial's orders on food. Kept as-is: it is the framework's claim working, and the spec shows results without comment.
- **2026-10-03 — Human injuries exceed Classic's** (8 vs 3) on the shipped seed: Human villagers keep working timber in the storm/squall and walk home late. Open: risk perception of storm work is weak relative to commitment and role-goal pull.
- **2026-10-03 — Habits alone don't carry prayer against moderate sleep need** (Game 2 design probe): a plateau habit term (~0.55) loses to a moderate sleep need (~0.77); stacked with a held norm and the commitment it wins from about day 10. Habits reduce how much prompting is needed rather than replacing understood commitments.
- **2026-10-03 — Single suggestions cannot win the Human roof** (colony v2 fix pass). Following every director card leaves the Human roof at 8.9 on the shipped seed, below Solo (9.78); skipping one card at a time shows the cedar (Idris injured, −0.7) and the dawn card (−0.4) cost most. Adding D2 cards (rush Yusuf or Tariq to the house at 08–14, Samira or Danyal to the forest, Idris to the forest) moved it by at most +0.1. The Human roof answers only to sustained, re-issued orders (good2rush roofs 6/6 seeds). Kept: cards are moment setups, and the goal card says winning takes your own orders.
- **2026-10-03 — Orders cannot starve the Human store** (colony v2 fix pass). With the cook thresholds lowered to 6 / 12, Human storm stock is 13–17 under every tested pattern, including all-to-the-forest (13–14), because Maryam's meal duties keep the pot going and she refuses to leave it. Careless orders cost the Human roof instead (8.2 all-to-forest, 9.3–9.85 Insist-everything).
- **2026-10-03 — Body depletion had to be halved for a day-long fast.** With the pre-1.2.0 rates (satiety 0.0015/min, hydration 0.002/min at rest) a dawn-to-dusk Ramadan fast from a full stomach reached the conscience's necessity bound (desperation ≥ 0.75) by mid-afternoon every day, so no one could keep it. Rates are now 0.0008/min (hunger 0.7 after ~9 h at rest, thirst after ~10 h). Two costs: the reservoirs are linear, so full→threshold and threshold→necessity take the same time, and the pre-1.2.0 ordering (thirst before hunger at rest) is lost; and the colony game was tuned on the old cadence, so it pins the old four rates per body (`BodyState.rates`, `COLONY_RATES`) instead of adapting its moments. Even on the new rates, a fasting man awake from suhoor with two 2-hour work blocks broke the fast on four of thirty days; the town shop keeps one block a day.
- **2026-10-03 — Conversation had no memory of what was said, and judgement no habituation.** Rıza, having heard "Osman is stingy" from Hacer, retold it at every tea; Halil judged each retelling as fresh backbiting and his trust in his friend fell from 0.6 to 3e-10 in ten days (respect to −1). Two guards now stand in `converse`: a claim the listener already heard from this speaker (its belief sources) is not retold, and a claim the listener already holds at the told confidence yields no percept. `social.judge` itself still has no habituation; a repeated deed through any other channel is judged at full weight.
- **2026-10-03 — The mortality hazard cap defeats compressed-time multipliers.** `mortalityHazard` caps at 1/year, so a `lifecourse.multiplier` of 2000 on five middle-aged townspeople over 30 days produced no deaths (the cap makes 30 days ≈ 8 % each). Tests that want deaths must use many people or the very old, not a multiplier.
- **2026-10-03 — Per-person state settles at ~200 KB at 30 days, chronicle excepted.** Snapshot JSON (town, five people): 560 KB after 3 days, 1.19 MB after 30, chronicle included; without the chronicle 30 days < 2 × 3 days + 60 KB. The decision trace is the largest slice (~80 KB a person, 32 records with their considered lists); the chronicle grows ~3 KB a day until `CHRONICLE_DEFAULTS.maxDays` (120).
- **2026-10-03 — Thirst is undamped while fasting (by design, Fleischmann 2026), so water, not food, is what breaks a fast in the model.** Every necessity break seen in the town runs was hydration-led. `fastingDays` for the damping ramp is read from consecutive kept-fast chronicle days, so a person with no chronicle gets day-one damping every day.
- **2026-10-03 — Material scarcity has no empirical citation.** `ConsiderContext.scarcity` (debt relative to income, from the host) scales short-horizon terms by an engineering default; the scarcity-and-cognition literature it gestures at is not cited in the module doc and should be before it is relied on.
- **2026-10-03 — Joint activities are one-sided in the driver.** An affordance's `with` list is the host's word that the other party is present; `partnersOf` only checks they are alive, and the conversation convention only that they are awake. In the town Rıza "has tea with Halil" while Halil is at work, and the talk happens. Requiring the partner to be idle or in company killed tea altogether (the two men's Ramadan sleep schedules never aligned at a decision), so the seam stays; a joint-activity handshake (`proposeJoint`) is the right fix for Game 2.

- **2026-10-03 — Review fixes: three behaviours had been carried by bugs.** (1) Chronic onsets were rolled as acute illnesses and cleared by immune drift in ~20 days. (2) A joint conversation both partners finished in the same minute ran twice (double social events, double contagion roll, advice re-queued after it had been heard). (3) A sleeper reviewed every 120 minutes slept through any duty whose wake-pressure stretch (the last ~17 min of Asr) fell between reviews: Halil missed Asr on 20 of 30 days. With the review capped at the wake-pressure minute he keeps all five prayers on all 30 days, so missed prayers in the town now come only from choices, not from the scheduler. Fajr is still "kept" at 08:00–10:00 because `prayerWindow` ends Fajr at Dhuhr; ending it at sunrise (Muslim 612, per the reviewer) could not be verified here (sunnah.com 403) and waits for the user.
- **2026-10-03 — Contagion now needs co-presence, which makes the town's placeIds matter.** Contagion skips calls, sleeping partners and mismatched places. Most town "contacts" were one-sided (a visitor at the teahouse while the partner slept or prayed), so with a mild carrier the 20-day test saw one transmission where it used to see several. The town also names every house `home`, so Hacer's visit to "home" and Halil asleep at "home" count as the same place; houses need distinct placeIds.
- **2026-10-03 — Scarcity double-counted money.** With both the gain (×(1+s)) and the scale shrink on, the material term averaged 1.20 (max 2.25) over 40 town days, above every commitment, norm and voice. The gain is now 0 (scale shrink only, ceiling 2 × materialWeight). It is still strong: at scarcity 1 a bare suggestion at strength 0.9 from a voice trusted 0.9 scores 0.78 against paid repair's 0.96; with a reason (an appeal) it wins.
- **2026-10-03 — A break of the fast under necessity was booked as a sin.** The capacity bound (2:286) let a desperate faster drink, and then the bookkeeping recorded a `sawm-ramadan` breach with guilt and no make-up; eating forbidden food under 2:173 likewise recorded a full breach although the verse says "they will not be sinful" (quran.com). Now such an act is flagged at begin (`Activity.necessity`), records no breach for necessity-eligible norms, and excuses the fast with a make-up owed (interpretation by analogy with the illness exemption of 2:184). Consequence for Game 2: the overbearing player's "broken fast" chain now ends in an excused day with a make-up, not a breach; trust in the pushing voice no longer falls on that account.
- **2026-10-03 — Involuntary recall was a positive-mood pump.** Every meal recalled an earlier meal at home (valence 0.34–0.56), boosted its salience and re-appraised it as a pleasant event; after 150 days a 150-day-old "ate at home" sat at salience 0.80. Ordinary memories now need |valence| ≥ 0.5 to intrude and do not gain salience from intruding; loss memories keep the lower floor and the boost.

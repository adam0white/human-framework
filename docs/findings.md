Short records of results that cut against expectations. Keep them; do not tune them away silently.

- **2026-10-04 — Engine 1.8.0 (family, bonds, ambient).** (1) *Family approval first worked as a mandate.* With relatives' approval passed as suggestions at full strength and a `duty` appeal, two parents trusted 0.85 objecting at −0.4 outweighed even a 40-meeting courtship (decline 1.07 against accept 0.43), which contradicts the decision that the spouses' own consent decides. `familyVoices` now scales strength by `familyWeight` 0.5 with no appeal, and the proposal term was raised (scale 2.5, floor 0.4): mild objection loses to a ready courtship, strong objection wins against a just-ready one and loses to a long one (`partnering.test.ts`). The balance is an engineering choice, not a finding from research. (2) *An engine bump alone moves the playtest fixture hashes:* Games 1–2 hash the people with their `engine` stamp, so `playtest-colony.json` and `playtest-voice.json` were regenerated (`PLAYTEST_REGEN=1`) with identical logs; with the engine set back to 1.7.0 the old hashes replay unchanged. (3) *Courtship warmth* at gain 0.08 reached only 0.49 after 24 meetings over three months; it is 0.12 now (ready after about 16–20 meetings). (4) *Upbringing has no effect without warmth*: a household whose caregivers have no affection for the child teaches it nothing, by construction; hosts must seed parent ties (as `sim.birth` does).

- **2026-10-04 — `npm run check` still had a wall-clock limit: Vitest's default 5 s test timeout.** Under other agents' load on the dev machine (load average ~6–7), `village.test.ts` "runs 20 people for 30 days" timed out at 5000 ms once in four full runs; it takes ~2.4 s under Vitest when quiet. - **2026-10-04 — Engine 1.6.0 (RimWorld-gap deliverables): what was left out or behaves unexpectedly.** (1) *Migration* covers person snapshots from 1.4.0 and 1.5.0 only, and the steps are stamp-only: a migrated save continues under 1.6.0 rules and does not replay the old engine where rules changed. Village continuations from both fixtures stayed byte-identical to the old engine's own; community and world state are not migrated, and older saves are refused. (2) *Duties missed during a mental break* are recorded as missed like any other. Probe: a devout villager held in a 14 h break from 06:00 booked three missed prayers and three `salah` breaches (plus a missed field shift); the control booked none. That may be harsher than "keep faith gentle" asks; whether a break should excuse a duty needs a sourced ruling from `research/`, and none was invented. (3) *The part table* (`DEFAULT_PARTS`) is read-time only and changes nothing unless an offer declares `requires` or a person has `enableDowned`; a village forager with five stacked night leg wounds (severity 0.5 each) would read moving 0.24, so derived downing is opt-in per person rather than on by default. (4) *A downed villager still "rests at home"*: the village has no travel, and its rest offer is tagged `rest`, which a downed person may take; hosts with places should offer a `floor` option where the person fell. (5) *A bleed stops when its injury heals away* (the injury is dropped at severity 0.001), whatever its rate. (6) *Threat fear fades in hours* (fear half-life 2 h), so a host must keep sending a standing threat (wolves still about) for avoidance to last; in the camp scenario the gatherer is back in the forest by the afternoon. (7) *The village timing test* (`runs 20 people for 30 days`, 4000 ms locally) failed under the full suite at 4.1–4.4 s both before and after these changes and passes alone at ~2.4–2.7 s. Corrected the same day: it is not a regression, and not only load. Timed with plain Node (native imports), the 20-person run takes 1.55–1.58 s at 456c794 (engine 1.4.0) and at every framework commit through 9cf9f69 (3 runs each). Under Vitest it takes ~2.4 s at 456c794 as well as at HEAD: Vite's module runner turns each cross-module call into a live-binding getter (`module-runner.js` `get`, 205 ms self time of 2.4 s) and the rest of the gap is consistent with lost inlining of small hot helpers (`advanceMemory` self time 40 → 130 ms; an inference from the profile, not proven). The ~2.4 s figure has been the vitest number since engine 1.4.0; no figure below 2 s under vitest is recorded in the repo. The ×2 local allowance (4000 ms) hid that the 2 s budget was missed even alone; under full-suite load the run reached 4.1–4.9 s and failed it. Fix: timing budgets live in `*.timing.ts`, run by `npm run bench` with the module runner off for the framework, and `npm run check` has no wall-clock assertions.

- **2026-10-03 — Body depletion had to be halved for a day-long fast.** With the pre-1.2.0 rates (satiety 0.0015/min, hydration 0.002/min at rest) a dawn-to-dusk Ramadan fast from a full stomach reached the conscience's necessity bound (desperation ≥ 0.75) by mid-afternoon every day, so no one could keep it.

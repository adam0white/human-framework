# A reconciled base for human goals in HF

Research date: 2026-10-21. Read-only research. No implementation or release is proposed as already authorized.

**Assumed — recommendation:** Keep concurrent needs. Add a small vocabulary of motive families and a record of held purposes. Do not replace the current model with a larger pyramid. Give everyone access to the same motive families. Let situations, development, learning and commitments shape their expression. Treat this shared repertoire as an engineering hypothesis, not proof that everyone wants the same things.

Evidence labels apply to each paragraph or table cell. **Confirmed** means the stated content was seen in a source today; it does not mean the theory is proven. **R** identifies revealed text, **I** attributed interpretation, **E** empirical research, **M** explanatory theory, and **C** existing code or documentation. **Assumed** identifies a proposal, inference or utopic premise. Links identify the source and access limits appear where material.

## A. Prior art and relevant, buildable ideas

### What HF already represents

**Confirmed C — local inspection today:** HF reads five physiological needs: food, water, sleep, rest and pain relief. It stores seven psychological reservoirs: safety, belonging, esteem, autonomy, competence, leisure and meaning. They act concurrently. Thresholds vary with temperament or values. Meaning's threshold currently rises with `tradition`; meaning replenishes during activities tagged worship, service, charity or meaningful. These are code rules, not established human constants. [needs.ts](https://github.com/adam0white/human-framework/blob/da16fe7b5f7078ce5b4bd7fbea9a767b31e80e51/packages/human/src/needs/needs.ts).

**Confirmed C:** `proposeGoals` supplies deficit-triggered friendship, mastery, service and respect goals. A `Goal` has importance, progress and achieved/abandoned status. Projects also let relaxed people adopt perceived opportunities; persistence supports a started pursuit. Affect supplies emotions and action tendencies, including fear, grief, love, boredom and guilt. Upbringing changes values, norm understanding and an attachment approximation. [agenda.ts](https://github.com/adam0white/human-framework/blob/da16fe7b5f7078ce5b4bd7fbea9a767b31e80e51/packages/human/src/agenda/agenda.ts), [framework.md](https://github.com/adam0white/human-framework/blob/da16fe7b5f7078ce5b4bd7fbea9a767b31e80e51/docs/framework.md).

**Assumed — diagnosis:** The main gap is continuity between ordinary projects and a person's larger reasons. A completed task can serve a purpose without completing it. A full meaning reservoir should not erase that purpose. Tradition should not serve as the general proxy for wanting a meaningful life.

### Islamic reconciliation without invented equivalence

**Confirmed R:** Qur'an 30:30 speaks of created fitra. Bukhari 1358 links birth upon fitra with parental influence. Qur'an 51:56 locates human purpose in worship. These are textual anchors; translations include interpretation. [30:30](https://quran.com/30/30), [Bukhari 1358](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1358), [51:56](https://quran.com/51/56). **Assumed:** Fitra can inform the framework's account of the person without becoming a measurable predisposition, personality profile or proof of psychological universality.

**Confirmed I:** Al-Ghazali's jurisprudential account protects religion, life, intellect, offspring and property. [Griffel's scholarly account](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/al-ghazali/). Kamali attributes systematic maqasid development to al-Shatibi and describes five essential protections. [Publisher-hosted exposition, indexed text; PDF opening blocked](https://iiit.org/wp-content/uploads/Maqasid-Al-Shari%E2%80%99ah-Ijtihad-and-Civilisational-Renewal-Combined.pdf). **Assumed:** Use maqasid as a held account of protected interests. They are neither five psychological appetites nor permission to generate rulings. Protecting property does not imply an innate desire for unlimited accumulation. Protecting lineage does not require every person to seek children.

**Confirmed R:** Qur'an 22:46 connects hearts with reasoning. The nafs passages describe inclination toward evil, self-reproach and tranquillity. [22:46](https://quran.com/22/46), [12:53](https://quran.com/12/53), [75:2](https://quran.com/75/2), [89:27](https://quran.com/89/27). **Confirmed I:** Al-Ghazali distinguishes overlapping meanings of qalb, nafs, ruh and ʿaql. [Skellie translation, chapter 1](https://www.emaanlibrary.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Al-ghazalisMarvelsOfTheHeart.pdf). **Assumed:** Keep these as attributed lenses. A staged nafs reading belongs to a character's held interpretation; the engine must not assign a stage or rank.

**Confirmed R:** Qur'an 17:85 stresses limited human knowledge of ruh. Bukhari 1968 describes claims of Lord, self and family. [17:85](https://quran.com/17/85), [Bukhari 1968](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1968). **Assumed — boundary:** Model conflicting reasons and observed acts. Never compute ruh, divine acceptance, spiritual worth or the truth of a person's ultimate purpose. Qalb, nafs, fitra and ʿaql remain non-scalar lenses and never decision inputs.

### Purpose drive and life goals

**Assumed — design:** Separate four things: present felt meaning; a held life purpose; a finite goal serving it; and the actual consequence of an act. Keep `meaning` provisionally as felt meaningfulness. Do not let its numeric value declare whether a life has meaning.

**Assumed:** Store a bounded purpose record: the person's wording, provenance, beneficiaries, supporting memories, acknowledged conflicts, linked projects and status. Status can be exploring, endorsed, questioned, suspended or relinquished. Allow several purposes or no articulated purpose. A child's recurring wish need not be an adult mission. Store religious content as held belief; store no sacredness, fitra, nafs or spiritual-progress score.

**Assumed:** On reflection or a relevant perceived opportunity, an endorsed purpose can propose a finite project. “Care for my family” may produce “repair their roof.” Finishing the roof ends the project. It does not complete family care. Numeric task importance may express a practical preference through existing agenda terms. It must not measure divine approval or rank purposes by sacredness. Descriptive lenses remain downstream readers.

**Assumed:** Credit experienced meaning through the person's appraisal of an outcome and its relation to an endorsed purpose. A generic `meaningful` tag is insufficient evidence. Permit mistaken appraisal: someone can feel fulfilled by harmful work. Public consequences, observers' beliefs and the actor's felt meaning must remain distinct. Avoid paying twice for the same outcome through both new purpose terms and existing goal terms.

## B. Utopic ideas, unlinked from feasibility

**Assumed — utopic premise:** A history simulation could follow generations inventing answers to shared concerns. One community organizes care through households, another through guilds, another through public institutions. People inherit practices, misunderstand them, reject them or repair them. Similar motives need not produce similar societies.

**Assumed — utopic premise:** Let a person disagree with their own life. A respected elder questions a career built around status. A child carries a caregiver's unfinished concern into a different vocation. A comfortable citizen abandons comfort for a commitment. No universal fulfillment score resolves these conflicts.

**Assumed — utopic premise:** Present the same historical event through incompatible lived accounts. A reform feels like freedom to one person and broken continuity to another. Slow the player's view when reasons conflict, while other lives continue. Show remembered promises, hesitation and chosen companions instead of exposing motive weights.

**Assumed — utopic premise:** The ultimate artifact is an ensemble of modeled human histories with explicit alternative assumptions. Compare which institutions and purposes recur across them. Exact historical prediction remains an ambition, not a capability established by these psychological theories. Even an imagined complete behavioral simulation should leave the unseen uncomputed.

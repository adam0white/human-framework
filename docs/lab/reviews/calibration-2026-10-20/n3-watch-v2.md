# The Night Watch — Callum

**Limited test.** The first night was playable. At dawn my automation could not scroll the lower report, so later nights are untested. The final stop is `TOOL`, not a judgment that the game lacks a continuation. Session timing and the diary appear below.

## Setup and limits

- Persona: Callum, 44, night-shift security guard. Plays Frostpunk, Kingdom Two Crowns and FTL. Patient; likes tense defence and putting people where they matter. A night off, on the sofa with his phone, with this time set aside.
- URL: https://human.adamwhite.work/watch/
- Build: no build identifier seen in the screens I reached.
- Device: 360 × 690 CSS px, touch emulation, light mode, default text size, device scale 1.
- Access: Playwright Chromium, one page in a persistent local Node process. I used screenshots and coordinate taps. My wheel implementation did not target the scrollable content correctly. I did not read game source, bundles, tests, network payloads, hidden DOM or simulation state.
- Budget: 15 real minutes minimum, 25 maximum. Timing starts below from navigation; the first usable screen was observed at +3.2 s. The loading screenshot at +0.1 s said “Lighting the lantern…”.
- No run was sent. No consent setting was changed. No export was downloaded.

**Method deviations:** I wrote the first-minute checkpoint at +72.9 s, 69.7 s after the first usable screen. It uses only observations already recorded by +58.8 s, and no rule learned later. It is a late checkpoint, not exact 60-second compliance. I also attempted to repair the automation with Node’s inspector. That used a debug API at the tool level, outside the brief’s stated boundary. It did not inspect or evaluate game state, did not repair the scroll control, and was stopped. These limitations prevent treating this as a fully compliant completed playtest.

## What playing felt like

The best moment was at +220.1 s, about 3:37 after the first usable screen. Tamar was asleep at the Gate. I rang the bell and got “Holding!” Her speech changed back to keeping watch. I had a specific worry, took a physical action, and got a reply from the person I was worried about. The rope then looked badly frayed. [017-tamar-call.png](n3-watch-v2-shots/017-tamar-call.png), [018-second-bell.png](n3-watch-v2-shots/018-second-bell.png).

The grain loss while I was looking east was another strong moment. Tamar reported that Kian had gone to the hall, and “Grain is gone: something got over the gate.” I took that as a consequence of leaving the tracked approach unwatched. That causal explanation is my inference; I did not establish the game’s rule. [010-quiet-east.png](n3-watch-v2-shots/010-quiet-east.png).

The worst game-facing moment was trying to read Kian while the fixed controls occupied the bottom of the screen. I could see “I’m keeping watch. The scout warned us about this stretch. I can’t make anything out” and could not establish whether that was his complete answer. The worst overall moment was the automation failing to scroll the dawn report. Those are separate findings.

The first `want_quit` was +442.2 s, reason `TOOL`: I wanted the next night and could not reach it with this controller. It did not pass during the session. There was no recorded wish to quit the playable first night.

Pulse series: +184.0 s **4/5**; +360.1 s **3/5**; +541.9 s **2/5**; +726.8 s **1/5**; +916.9 s **1/5**. The decline after dawn measures the blocked test setup, not the game’s later pacing.

## Where I stopped and what I would want

Stopped at the first dawn, after two morning conversations, with reason **TOOL**. Stop timestamp: 2026-10-20T16:20:12.427Z. Navigation-to-stop time: **916.9 s (15 min 16.9 s)**. First-usable-screen-to-stop time: **913.7 s (15 min 13.7 s)**. This satisfies the wall-clock floor only; it is not 15 minutes of uninterrupted gameplay. Dawn was first observed at +244.5 s. Much of the remaining session was blocked by the controller. The phone still said “Six souls still here” and “Grain: plenty above spring food needs.” The first want_quit did not pass.

After stopping, I took exactly one look at 1280 × 800. The scene occupies the left and the dawn report the right. It exposes more report text, including “Nobody stood the mill wall. Nothing came” and the equivalent east-wall statement. The report still extends below the viewport. I made no gameplay choice from that look. [028-desktop-after-stop.png](n3-watch-v2-shots/028-desktop-after-stop.png).

As Callum, I would voluntarily try another night with working scrolling. I cannot say whether the later game would hold me through a winter.

Up to three changes I would want, in order:

1. Keep a watcher’s complete immediate answer and available reply visible above the lantern controls.
2. Let me read the complete bell explanation while the bell remains available.
3. Make an unheard watcher call visibly different from a call that opened an answer, especially while night messages arrive.

The automation’s scrolling needs repair before another test. That is a test setup change, not a requested game change.

Best: Yusuf moved from prayer to Building, and the roof number rose. I understood the delay and saw work happen. The cedar and cold-pot suggestions also gave me understandable risks to refuse.

Worst: trying to shelter injured Idris. Show Idris found him, but the next order started with Worker · Choose. The Home tap did not produce a visible response. After picking him again, he was already Asleep and I was still being asked to choose a place.

Frustration started at 2:24: “It takes several taps to send one person.” At 2:27 I questioned whether an order mattered because Idris was already Gathering timber. At 3:30 the expanded goal plus sheet left very little scene. I wanted faster results at 3:43 and selected 2×. I quit at 4:40, well before the eight-minute ceiling.

## 3. New quit points and strategies

1. **The people's work is easier to inspect and easier to miss.** `app.ts:575–581` replaces the previous event preview with the fixed label “Open journal”; the event survives only in the button title. A phone has no ordinary hover. At 180 s I opened the journal and found “Hasan broke through the first chalked wall…” and “The rolling stone stood in its groove. Hasan rolled it once to see.” Both had occurred while the main control simply said Open journal. The patch removes the name and consequence from ordinary watching, then asks the player to open a list to find them. This undercuts Ines's wish to know people.
2. **Long previews still exceed the essential reading area.** At 260 s the excavation response's final explanation, “People choose when to dig,” was incomplete at the visible edge. The mixed-protection counts and open-door warning above it fit. `#inspection-body` had 191 px of content in 169 px of visible space; `#cell-words` ended at y=529.6, while the visible reading ended at y=511.7. The canvas was 120 px high. No text was lost from the DOM, but the player had to scroll a fixed panel to finish the immediate response. This is a bounded DL-30 failure involving the autonomy/timing explanation, not a claim that the bed or door remedies I tested were clipped.
3. **Reading becomes the default way to slow time.** This is a change in playback, not a proven dominant simulation strategy. A receipt left open keeps the work slow indefinitely; repeated fast-speed selections do not overcome it. It can make construction feel less responsive and encourage a ritual of dismissing every response. The screen names the slowdown, so this is friction rather than concealed punishment.

No globally dominant construction strategy was established in one live run and one bot seed. Room designation is cheap and growth capacity counts beds independently of protection (`warren.ts:2317–2334`); that predates 20b. Do not call it a new exploit.

## 4. What did it cost?

**Autonomy:** preserved. No simulation source changed. People still choose digging, sheltering and door closure. A clearer condition report supplies information, not a command.

**Their words:** preserved in data but less present in play. Personal speech is behind Activity and words (`app.ts:708–714`). Named breakthroughs and other event previews disappear behind Open journal. This is the clearest cost to something the reports valued. The expanded person disclosure remained readable in my Hasan inspection; I did not infer that all people's long disclosures fit.

**Difficulty:** no rule or edition change. Inspection now slows all card types, which gives the player more time to read and also slows the response they await. The harmless scouting visit and the standing raid call predate this layout adoption.

**Ending:** no changed ending found. The supplied testers did not reach one. Neither their reports nor my ten-minute run establish a beloved ending that this patch could have lost.

## 5. Ranked changes, one round each

1. **Restore one visible, complete event sentence beside the journal control.** Keep Open journal labelled. Prioritise a named breakthrough, completed door, arrival or raid result. Give the sentence room or shorten it; do not restore an ellipsis. Reason: it reconnects the player's mark with a person doing something. Check: ordinary phone play reveals the first worker and completed door without opening the journal, while the scene and commit controls remain usable.
2. **Shorten the immediate plan reading so every branch fits without an inner scroll.** Keep the entire essential response visible: remedy, protection, capacity and the explanation that people choose when to dig. Move only genuinely optional context to a labelled disclosure. Test the mixed-protection/open-door case from 33-deep-plan.png, as well as the longest bed and door readings. Reason: the patch's central promise still fails in an ordinary reachable case. Check: the entire essential response lies above the commit row at 360×690, with the selected work visible.
3. **Separate a pure acknowledgement from a sustained inspection.** Keep explicit successful store and lamp receipts. Keep slowdown for plan/bed safety readings, failure remedies and deliberate inspection. Let a pure receipt appear without leaving a persistent inspection open. Reason: a player should not have to dismiss every success to watch at the chosen rate. Check: a successful store or lamp mark does not leave the next minute at the inspection rate unless the player deliberately keeps inspecting; the chosen speed and actual rate stay explicit.

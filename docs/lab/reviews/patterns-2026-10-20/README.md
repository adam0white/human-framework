# Cross-game patterns round, 2026-10-20

The design round ([previous folder](../design-round-2026-10-20/README.md)) ended with two patterns across games: (A) phone panels with no room (text clipped, sheets overlapping, state unreadable while ordering) and (B) watching or filling forms instead of doing. The owner directed fixes at the lab level. The checklist gained DL-30, DL-31 (§6) and §8 Hands on, DL-32, DL-33 ([design language](../../design-language.md)). Lab kit 0.2.0 adds the phone stage and the phone check (`npm run lab:phone`). Each game's doc records its change under "Design change 2026-10-20b (owner may veto)".

This folder holds the source-unread Codex playtests of the live builds ([canonical prompt](../../playtester-prompt.md), 360×690 touch, fresh personas), one read-only adversarial Codex fun review per changed game, and the live phone audit of all 14 games (`phone-audit.md`). Reports are the testers' own words; local paths were replaced by "(driver scratch)" or repository-relative paths, and screenshots stayed in the driver's scratch folder.

## Best and worst moments

**Best:** finishing the salad and getting “Table 8 noticed the care in their food” at 132s. My work had an explicit consequence. Turning and resting the lamb gave me actual cooking acts, without a menu of planning fields.

**Worst:** two attempts to take a portion returned “already plated” by 49s. By the first-minute checkpoint I was annoyed that the crew got there first. Browser overhead contributed, so this does not establish that a human could never catch them at default speed.

At 211s I became bored with reading a step and tapping the same place. At 215s I chose to leave after completing the lamb. The money goal was understandable; it did not make me want four weeks of this loop.

**Is it fun?**

Watch relief is the right kind of decision: move someone from another stretch, spend travel time and attention, and ask two people who may answer differently. Kian’s “I’ll take the Gate” was the best new moment. It connected a person to a threatened post. Help and down refused in my run, so these acts are plainly not a guaranteed repair button. I found no evidence that they drain dusk posting of its weight. I also did not complete a successful handover or compare matched dusk plans, so long-term balance remains untested.

The cost wording undermines that decision. Sending Kian from Mill wall to relieve Joss warned that Mill wall would be **empty**, although Tamar and Yunus were there. The Help warning also claimed Kian would leave Mill wall empty while Tamar and Yunus occupied it. Source inspection confirms the warning tests the departing person’s post, not remaining coverage (`apps/site/src/watch/sim/night-actions.ts:166–177`). That is a false tactical consequence, not useful uncertainty.

The requests become a chore when the player must scroll past several unavailable helpers and generic “That isn’t on offer here now” forecasts, only to receive “I can see more…” as the reason for leaving an injury unattended. The current account also fails to say that Tamar is hurt. The buttons carry more of that meaning than the person’s words do. Preserve refusal; make the care decision and its known reason legible.

Well’s persistent status makes the people easier to understand. A drink followed by a completed job feels like a person managing a need. A repeated role refusal attached to a suggested roof helper feels like stubbornness when I am still waiting on roof 8. The design has improved accountability more than influence. The satisfying acts were Yusuf building after dusk, Tariq finishing after his drink, and Maryam’s final Kitchen order reaching **done** while meals rose 15→17. I would continue Watch after its response layout is fixed. This Well run did not make me want another lunch-break session built around the same helper refusals. That is my judgment, not a new human acceptance result.

**Blockers before the live retest, ranked (five total)**

1. **Watch hides the exact response the retest needs.** Keep a short condition sentence and Rest/Send down visible above the footer. Put helper selection behind one labelled **Send relief / Send help** control, with the chosen helper’s cost visible before dispatch. Reclaim or collapse lantern controls while that account is open. Retest tired and hurt accounts with at least the six-watcher roster reached here (nine souls in the village) at 360×690; no scroll should be needed to discover the intervention.
2. **Watch’s tiredness impression does not explain relief availability.** Yunus was “tiring” at dusk but had no relief/rest request at night. The relief options appeared briefly in the Tamar observations. Source inspection at the tested commit distinguishes retained Keeper estimates (`reads.ts:34–48`) from current outward signs/self-report (`night-actions.ts:39–47`); this does not prove fatigue stayed unchanged or reserve caused the mismatch. Small fix: keep a Rest/Relief request visible for someone just described as tiring, while allowing their current condition to determine the answer. Mark an older impression as “seemed tiring at dusk.” Aligning the display and request rules is a design recommendation. Evidence: Yunus at 377.853→401.744; Tamar’s transient rows at 241.458→247.742 and 317.588→323.885.
3. **Watch falsely warns that a staffed post becomes empty.** Count remaining on-post watchers when constructing the warning. Say “leaves Yunus and Tamar at Mill wall” or “leaves Mill wall empty” according to the actual known arrangement. Apply the same correction to down requests. Evidence: [21](watch-shots/21-relief-cost.png), helper/target positions in [20](watch-shots/20-joss-relief.png), and the source branch above.
4. **Well steers the player back into a known helper refusal.** The second-day Samira suggestion already disclosed her earlier “gather grain” answer and warned that it was old. Its current preview still said **Will refuse**, followed by the original roof-8 frustration. Small fix: when that helper is currently refusing, replace the suggestion’s **Review order** route with her reason plus an available alternative, such as selecting another helper or **Insist · may cost trust** with its warning. Keep ordinary refusal intact. Do not make a suggestion whose current preview predicts refusal the obvious route to speeding the roof.
5. **Well clips order status and crowds people while ordering.** Clamp tag placement to the scene’s visible bounds and wrap within that width; reserve space for the selected person in the preview scene. Keep the full selected-person status as a labelled way to reopen the latest order. This also gives the player a stable selection route when camera movement makes a person hard to reacquire. Evidence: [04](well-shots/04-idris-tap-person.png), [16](well-shots/16-samira-rush-preview.png), [17](well-shots/17-samira-second-day-refusal.png).

**Nice-to-haves, not additional blockers:** put the bell’s recall purpose and rope wear beside Ring before the first pull; give help/down refusals a care-specific reason when known; restate retained Rush costs on Ask again; distinguish helper accepted, travelling, arrived and outgoing watcher resting. None requires removing refusal or adding numerical fatigue meters. Verify the pre-press camera case separately; my stale-coordinate mistakes do not diagnose its cause.

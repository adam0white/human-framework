## Best and worst moments

**Best:** The first-light outcome. “Mill Lane slept dry” answers my goal immediately. The graph distinguishes what the water did from what Dai told me. His fatigue becomes a consequence I can understand, rather than a stamina number. The opening gate tradeoff also suited me: lower the dam now, raise the town river later. I did not need a formula to make a cautious first choice.

**Worst:** At 04:28 I wanted a larger opening after the 4.10 m report, but Dai was not answering. I could identify the immediate obstacle, but not whether a useful response remained. Waiting felt tense, not completely pointless; his sleep was explained at dawn. I do not claim there is no other response in the game. I found none in the Gate and Dai sheets I opened.

**Boredom:** Around 242.7 s, after leaving two turns open and waiting through the quiet stretch. The weather report and increasingly old reading gave me a reason to act again. I wanted one morning’s result, not five nights in this sitting.

**Input friction:** The sheet height changed, so two taps based on an earlier close position missed. The second sent an unintended stove request. These were my coordinate errors. The screenshots do not establish overlapping hit areas. They do show that close is not at one stable screen position.

## Stop and return

I stopped at **354.9 real seconds (5 min 54.9 s)**, on the first-light report, before the eight-minute ceiling. My last intentional gameplay choice was to request another level reading at 256.8 s. One accidental stove request followed at 322.7 s. The dam had gone over its spillway lip, but Mill Lane remained dry. I would return voluntarily for Tuesday on another train ride. I would want confidence that the reading times are consistent. The personal uncertainty around Dai is interesting; a timestamp that may mean two different things is distracting.

## Three changes I would want, ranked

1. **Make each reading’s time unambiguous (DL-13).** If the gauge means observation time and the log means radio time, say so. If they are meant to match, make them match. I need to judge how stale my evidence is.
2. **Keep Game menu and About fully reachable at 360×690 as log wrapping changes (DL-26, DL-23).** I should not have to tap a thin exposed edge.
3. **Keep the close control easier to find as sheets change height (DL-24/input friction).** I sent a stove request while trying to leave a sheet. A stable location would help on the train. This is a preference based on my two misses, not proof of overlapping controls.

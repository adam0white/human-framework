## Best, worst and stopping

Best moment: “He remembers: iftar with Nuran at our table, last Ramadan.” It made eating feel connected to a shared life. His answer about repairing before talking to Hacer also gave him a priority of his own.

Frustration began when the log passed the Hacer answer before I read it, around 2:29. By 3:36 I wrote that the repeated scheduling felt like a form. The worst moment was the Why sheet: it named grief, then represented it as a decimal contribution. I quit at 4:09.0. I would not return voluntarily in this state. I did not finish a day or reach an ending.

## Uncertainties

These are earliest recorded explicit wishes, not measured private onsets. Earlier boredom, impatience, intent to leave a house or reading panel, and desire to advance time do not alone establish a wish to quit. Raw elapsed values control timing where retrospective summaries round, shift or misplace reactions. Post-stop excerpts repeat existing evidence and do not create extra wishes. Their quoted BORED/CONFUSED/FRUSTRATED labels are not treated as verified classifications.

The input does not provide independent prompt schedules or formal protocol metadata. No raw reaction explicitly identifies a forced quit probe. Explicit wishes appear in participants’ own words; this does not prove they were unprompted. Required layout checks and time-set-aside/promised-time statements are recorded where visible. Continuing after a wish can reflect obligation or outcome checking; it does not establish enjoyment, recovery, or voluntary retention. Reduced wish is distinguished from an expressly passed wish.

Blinding is imperfect. The visible directory supplies labels; diaries mention earlier sessions, prepared starts, edition wording and possible familiar characters. No identity, source, condition, arm, reason or control assignment is inferred from those words, driver identifiers or paths. No group comparison is made. Participant calculations, household guesses and impressions of game causality remain reported interpretations, without source inspection or independent verification.

Every complete diary, including all raw reactions and its retrospective record, was read separately before persisting its row. All 16 rows are complete. Original inputs were preserved.

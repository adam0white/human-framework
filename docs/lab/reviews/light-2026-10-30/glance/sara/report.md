## 4. Moments

**What held me:** the misread clue. On the desktop I fell for the 4.5 reading and opened the gate to 4 turns. On the phone I compared the lightning line, asked for a re-read and caught the 4.4 reading without moving the gate. Learning from the risky choice that failed is exactly what I enjoy. I also liked finding the standing rule in Watch arrangements.

**What lost me:**
- On the desktop, after about 23:30 the level only fell. The game became ask, wait, skip.
- On the phone, the night had the same structure: lightning at 22:34 and a high misread soon after.
- On both devices I never saw the town, so the gate had only one thing to weigh.

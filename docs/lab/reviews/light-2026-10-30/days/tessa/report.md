## 4. Moments

**What held me**
- Each choice had its cost written next to it before I chose.
- The story changed with my choices. Paying from savings, reading the page aloud and lending the notebook shaped later scenes.
- On the laptop, tapping the visit with only 1 hour left gave a stated reason and moved on. I could fail, and the game said why.
- Both endings named what I had done. The phone ending said nobody had addressed the harm of reading the page aloud. The laptop ending said the visit had not happened and the page stayed private.
- The line from Selin about not having to explain the marriage was lovely.

**What lost me**
- Two buttons per screen, with no way to say or try anything of my own. This is my usual complaint.
- Each run was over in about 75 seconds, before I felt I had made anything mine.

**Quit wishes:** two want_quit logs, both FINISHED, because the game ended on its own.

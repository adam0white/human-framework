# 4. Moments

**What held me**
- The cost of each choice is printed before I press it.
- The privacy theme: keeping or sharing the closed notebook.
- The honest ending text. It says plainly "the promised visit has not happened" on desktop and "The visit was kept" on phone.

**What lost me**
- The desktop visit button was offered, I pressed it, and it failed.
- Both runs ended in about 80 seconds.

**Quit wishes**
- Desktop, 79.2 s, FINISHED. It did not pass; I stopped 7 s later.
- Phone, 76.0 s, FINISHED. It did not pass; I stopped 7 s later.
- Neither was CONFUSED or BORED. The game simply ended.

**Pulses:** none, because neither session reached 180 s.

# 5. Confusion and clarity

**Clear**
- Hours left, tiredness and door status are shown at the top of each scene.
- The cost lines on the choices are accurate to what happened.
- The ending states plainly what was and was not done.

**Confusing**
- On desktop I pressed "Make the journey yourself, if there is time" with 1 hour left against a 2-hour cost. The page said "The visit needs 2 hours; 1 remains." It then moved on, and I could not retry.
- On the phone the same button said "2 hours for the visit; change the later arrangement". It worked, because I had 2 hours.
- "Obtain the original receipt" was offered after Burak had already brought it.
- On desktop, after the failed visit only "Close the door" remained. I had no way to arrange a delivery.
- "No door cover agreed" stayed in the header all game, and I never learned what would change it.
- The two devices told slightly different stories (Burak or Selin holding the receipt, Selin refusing to stay). I do not know if that is a seed or my choices. I am guessing.

  - The agreement list says in advance who will refuse and why.
  - The signing screen shows "minutes needed versus minutes remaining", and on the phone it counts the delivery wait too.

- **The good moment:** I noticed that fetching the letter (6 minutes) brings me back exactly when Rıza returns, at 10 minutes left.

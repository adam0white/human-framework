## 4. Moments
- **What held me:**
  - Each person answers in their own words.
  - Refusal is possible.
  - The journal has ordinary details, such as Nell's promise to call her mother and Wren coming because Oskar said she wouldn't.
  - The phone picture of the climbers on the slope was lovely.
- **What lost me:**
  - Long waits that ended in pressing Skip.
  - The rope terms.
  - The phone buttons moving when the warning appeared.
- **Quit wishes:**
  - A, 207.0 s, CONFUSED. It passed once "Close up" worked, but I stopped anyway.
  - B ended BORED at 77.9 s. I never logged a separate want_quit there.
- **Pulses:** one, at 186.0 s on the phone, engagement 3. The desktop session ended before 180 s, so no pulse there.
- **First-minute notes:** logged at 66.9 s (A) and 68.3 s (B), after 60 s each time.
- **Pace:** I could not tell what Skip does or how far it jumps. In A it moved the clock from 05:37 to 06:49, then from 07:05 to 08:58. That was a jump of an hour or two. I did not know that would happen.

## 5. Confusion and clarity, from actual play
- Unclear: "lead on" versus "climb on". The hint "Tap Lead on to start walking… Ask them to join you" mixes the two.
- Unclear: "A call needs attention". I looked at the screen and could not find what it pointed to.
- Unclear: "Something I need for that is missing." This was Tamar's refusal when I asked her to rope up with Nell. The next sentence, "Nell is too far away to attach to the same rope", did explain it.
- Clear: the hints named the next thing to do, one at a time.
- Clear: "Close up: Tamar Vale + Nell" fixed the refusal at once.
- Clear: the weather warning gave an estimate, "not a deadline".
- Clear: "Insisting… can damage their trust in you".
- Clear: the journal explained crampons as "spikes for walking on ice".

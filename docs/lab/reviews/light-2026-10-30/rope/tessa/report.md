## 4. Moments

**What held me**
- Choosing one person and a strength (ask gently, urge, insist) was playful.
- Insist was greyed with the reason "after a refusal" on the laptop. That is the opposite of an invisible boundary.
- The text said insisting "can turn not now into an unwilling yes", and it did.
- On the phone I asked Oskar to turn back and he asked "Who will come with me?". I could then send Wren, which felt like a real choice.
- Small story lines pleased me: Nell's crampons, Bilal's stove and tea, "Wren came because Oskar said she wouldn't", cowbells below.

**What lost me**
- The same "ask me after I climb the hut path" line came back for different acts and different people. That was eat, rest, give-me-your-pack and meet-Oskar, from Wren and Oskar. It continued well after we had left the hut on the laptop.
- On the phone I never left the hut. Oskar flipped from "I want to go down" to "I am ready" within seconds. Four people then said nearly the same "I will do it". The final bail turned into everyone wanting to go down.
- Wren once refused with "I can't take that on. I do not have what I need for that." I could not tell what she meant.

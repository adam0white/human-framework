## 4. Moments
- **What held me:** talking to Sevim. Her lines are individual ("Not without the books. Stay and help me bundle them."). The grey hint line named the next step each time. Sevim's rescue chain works: Come now, Torch, Show the stair, Walk with me, Street.
- **What lost me:** walking. On the phone I could not reach landing 3 after about five taps, a held tap, a key press and a point tap. I do not know why. My best guess is that the walk needs uninterrupted time and my extra taps reset it. That is unverified.
- **Desktop:** I found that one tap plus about 12 s of waiting works. I lost about 45 s learning it, and the stair then closed before I got to Derya.
- **Quit wishes:** phone CONFUSED at 105.5 s, desktop FRUSTRATED at 156.6 s. Neither passed. I stopped right after each one, and both stops came within about 10 s of the want_quit.
- **Pulses:** none logged. Both sessions ended before a pulse was due. I did not log one, so there is no engagement rating.

## 5. Confusion and clarity
- **Clear:** the goal, the timer, the grey hint line and the speech buttons.
- **Confusing:**
  - Walking gives no feedback. I could not tell whether a tap had registered.
  - The speech buttons stay grey for a long time, and I could not tell where I was.
  - Neighbour statuses ("Smoke may kill me. Get me outside.") gave no countdown.
  - The Feedback box opens pre-filled, and it has no Back button, only "Not now".
  - The stair-closes timer does not say what it means. Hacer and Derya died around 9 s before the end on desktop.
  - I did not understand "Street", a button that did nothing visible on desktop while Sevim was following. I may have misread it.

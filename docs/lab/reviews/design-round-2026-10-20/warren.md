## What playing felt like

**Best moment:** finding Hasan actually carving the rolling stone. The chalk, the lamp and the person working made this feel like building a place with people rather than painting a completed tunnel. The unreachable-stone explanation was useful without solving the room layout for me.

**Worst moment:** ringing the bell, then finding “Call ended” about 6.5 real seconds later when I opened Check shelter. The game clearly reported that the call ended. I could not tell why that duration was so short or what I should expect from an affirmative answer. I had only three exposed sleeping places, so I am not claiming the game should have made everybody safe.

I was not bored. At 256 seconds the smoke warning interrupted my attempt to understand the bed warning. By 345 seconds I was frustrated by affirmative bell replies beside people sleeping above ground. I wanted to quit because I could not yet trust my plan or understand the bell's practical effect.

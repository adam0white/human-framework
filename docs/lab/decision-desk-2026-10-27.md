# Decision desk proposal details, 2026-10-27

These are the original proposal details behind the decisions indexed in [durable directions](exit-knowledge-2026-11-12.md). They preserve design intent, not a claim every game implements it. The later owner direction lifted the Rope freeze. Do not reimpose the historical freeze shown in the category table. Public sharing still requires the separate owner readiness and employment/IP decisions.

## Approaches

| Approach    | Follows                                                     | Spine                                                                                                                | Forbids                           | Look                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chronicle   | Tarn Adams, Tynan Sylvester, Frostpunk's Jakub Stokalski    | You set conditions; people act alone; each season ends in a written chronicle; a village can fail, by name.          | Orders to one person              | Serif chronicle, parchment and iron-gall ink, ink-line map; the book is the menu       |
| Inner Voice | Robert Kurvitz, Emily Short, Kentucky Route Zero            | One person; you are one voice among their faculties, which answer back; every choice is a sentence they could say.   | A visible score or meter          | Large text on near-black, one colour per voice, one shifting charcoal portrait         |
| Desk        | Lucas Pope, Sam Barlow                                      | One desk, one object; every claim has a source; a wrong read costs someone else; you can lie, and lies are compared. | A narrator who confirms the truth | Typewriter documents, manila and red stamps; the drawer is the menu                    |
| Clock       | Overcooked's Phil Duncan and Oli De-Vine, Miyamoto's Pikmin | One continuous clock; a body in a place; short rounds that can be lost late; reading happens between rounds.         | Turns                             | Condensed type, high contrast, one hot danger colour, light cones at night; no numbers |

An approach applies to type, palette, renderer, menu and title card. The proposal folds Walk into Clock; the owner adopted all four approaches and the whole-game scope. Chronicle forbids orders to one person. Inner Voice forbids visible scores and meters. Desk forbids a narrator who confirms truth. Clock forbids turns. These prohibitions belong to the adopted approach definitions; implementation and taste acceptance need their own evidence.

Each shelf card has a plain-word length badge, such as “ten minutes” or “an evening”, and names its approach. A category describes what the player does; an approach describes the presentation. The home page shows one or two games per category. The desk proposed Pass as Clock and Glance as Desk as a two-game probe, with Valley as Chronicle and Two Voices as Inner Voice in a four-game alternative. The owner chose no probe first; move all games after 4.x.

The classic candidates were a wagon trail (Chronicle), night-village deception (Desk), household life (Chronicle), clan council (Chronicle), squad manager (Desk), and farm village (Clock). The owner chose the wagon trail. A space version was an open proposal, not an accepted setting. Working titles in the original desk were unverified nostalgic suggestions, not cleared product names.

The local desk HTML used external saved-answer state. This document preserves the proposal tables and the owner answers already recorded in owner directions; it is not a backup of unexported desk answers. That state needs separate private custody verification.

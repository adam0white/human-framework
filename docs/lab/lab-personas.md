# Lab persona panel

> Panel status: The owner authorized HF 3 lab-panel rounds under the playtest director: twelve standing testers and two cold slots. Each game gets a round, fixes and a retest by the same testers. The cycle ends when a retest raises no new blocking ask, or after two iterations; remaining asks retain dated Deferred entries. Completion requires actual round and retest receipts. Friends’ playtests and Show HN stay paused until owner readiness acceptance. This document does not dispatch a round.

These are fictional working personas, not recruited people or evidence about a population. Their countries and ages supply individual context, not national or age stereotypes. Temperaments use HF trait names as qualitative premises, not measured scores. This document defines briefs; it does not add runtime presets to `packages/playtester`.

Follow the [playtest harness rules](playtest-harness.md) for build records, raw diaries, ledger rows, browser sessions and timing complaints.

## Count and use

The coordinator proposed “17 standing”; this roster implements **17 positions: 12 recurring players + 2 rotating never-HF cold positions + 3 recurring designers/reviewers**. There are **15 maintained identities**, not 17 permanent people. The two cold positions receive two new identities each round. After N rounds with distinct cold entrants, the panel has used 15 + 2N identities. Do not reuse a cold entrant as a never-HF player. Record any later return explicitly as a returner outside those two positions.

Every round includes both cold positions. Draw the recurring players appropriate to the question; record who was selected and why. Rotate the recurring selection across rounds. Mix patient, curious and impatient players. Keep at most one targeted-dislike probe per round and identify it. Designer positions are separate reviews, not player sessions or votes. This roster does not require 17 concurrent agents or override the repository's review count.

Only a coordinator reads this full panel. Give each tester only their own brief, the player prompt and their permitted diary extract. Do not supply cast biographies, cast registry entries, secrets, design documents, source, reviews or anyone else's play reports. Never put a game character's canonical biography into a tester brief. A tester may recognize a character only from ordinary visible play and their own earlier diary.

## Two rotating cold positions each round

- **Cold A — game-familiar newcomer.** Recruit a new fictional identity with age, country, everyday background, genre experience, HF temperament, likes, dislikes and play habits at the same depth as a recurring brief. They have never played, watched, read about or reviewed any HF game. Do not describe them as a franchise returner. Vary genre background and temperament between rounds.
- **Cold B — little-game-experience newcomer.** Recruit a different new identity with the same complete fields. They have never encountered HF and do not share Cold A's context. Give them ordinary leisure interests and a reason to try the page, rather than a caricature of incompetence. They may understand people and situations without knowing game conventions.

## Diary and recognition rules

Keep each recurring player's own first-person observations in their linked diary across games. A diary is a record of actual play, not a backstory expansion. Preserve original wording, uncertainty, session date, round, arm, game, visible build, report/log reference and feedback receipt or failure. Do not invent visits, friendships, remembered faces or improved opinions. Append corrections beside the original claim; do not rewrite past reactions after learning a rule.

For a first visit to a game, supply only that player's diary entries about **other games**, frozen before the round. Do not supply entries about the target game, even from an older edition. If they have played the target before, label the session a return test, not a cold per-game test; hiding their diary does not erase exposure. A source-unread first visit may therefore be franchise-experienced. Only the two rotating positions are never-HF.

Record recognition when it happens, before verification: elapsed time, visible cue, remembered earlier encounter, confidence and the tester's own words. An uncertain guess stays uncertain. After play the coordinator may compare it with canonical facts, separately; never feed that answer into the session. Lack of recognition is evidence too. Do not quiz a tester with a cast name before it appears.

For paired arms, freeze one pre-game diary snapshot and clone it into two independent fresh contexts. The coordinator also creates isolated browser profiles or validated independent storage snapshots for each arm, including game saves and the central shelf. Clone the same declared gameplay starting state; give each arm its own recording/session identity and never share shelf writes across arms. Never run arm B in the page or storage left by arm A. Record starting-state provenance and isolation checks in round metadata, hidden from the tester. Prepare consent consistently before dispatch; the tester does not change it. A failed isolation check invalidates the paired comparison. Give identical persona text, prior diary extract and ordinary starting conditions to both. Neither arm may read the other, its feedback, its diary additions or its exports. The two cold positions are two identities, each independently instantiated in both arms; neither instance learns from its counterpart. Mark paired instances with the arm in metadata, never in the feedback name. Archive both arm records separately under the person's diary after both finish. These are alternative exposures, not two sequential autobiographical visits. Future briefs must select one declared continuity branch (or neither); do not merge incompatible memories. A later round is a return test for that target regardless of which branch is retained.

# Feedback ledger

Every piece of feedback the lab receives gets one row here: what was said, by whom, about which game, and where it goes. Triage it once, route it everywhere it helps. A row can have several routes, because one player's complaint about one game is often a lesson for every game. The owner asked for this on 2026-10-19: "benefit the entire lab from almost each specific feedback."

**How to add a row.** Append to the table under `## Ledger`. Take the next id (`F-nnn`, never reused). Copy the words exactly, in double quotes, at most about 40 words, trimmed with an ellipsis. Do not paraphrase in the `words` column. Use a single quote inside a quote. Replace any pipe character with a slash. Then pick routes. If no route helps, write `none`. Add the row on the day the feedback arrives, and add the source link.

**Columns.** `kind` is one of owner, playtester (LLM persona playtesters), human (a real person other than the owner), harness (the HF-character playtester), cohort, telemetry. `verdict` is keep (would come back, positive), quit (stopped, bored, frustrated), mixed, or `-` (not a verdict). `game` is one of pass, glance, murmur, rope, repair, neighbours, valley, well, voice, watch, lab (about the lab or all games), or a new game's short name.

**Route vocabulary** (separate several with `; `):
- `fix:<game> <what>` is a fix for that game. The text ends with `(done)` or `(open)` where known on the day of writing.
- `lesson:L<n>` is a lab-wide lesson in [lessons.md](lessons.md).
- `hf:<ref>` is an HF request. The ref is an existing request (`hf:game-requests-2026-10-18#<title>`, `hf:<game>/hf-requests.md#<title>`) or a new one in [../game-requests-2026-10-19.md](../game-requests-2026-10-19.md).
- `calibration:C<n>` is a calibration item for the simulated playtesters in [packages/playtester/CALIBRATION-NOTES.md](../../packages/playtester/CALIBRATION-NOTES.md).
- `none` means recorded, no action.

**Source links** read `<repo>/<path>`. Repos sit under `~/code/`: `pass`, `glance`, `murmur`, `valley`, `rope`, `concordia@neighbours` (the `neighbours` branch of `concordia`). Paths without a repo prefix are in this repository. Where a source gave a tester's words and the build changed because of them, the `(done)` marker is what that source reports; it is not rechecked here.

**What this ledger is not.** It is not a complete record of every playtest line. It holds the lines that were most telling (quitting points, boredom, confusion, delight, owner words) from 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-19, plus a few earlier owner lines. Seeded 2026-10-19. Sources not seen: repair (no playtest dated 2026-10-18 or 2026-10-19), warren and stairwell (their `docs/playtests/` folders are empty on 2026-10-19).

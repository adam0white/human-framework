# Game ranking, 2026-10-20

Owner, 2026-10-20: "Have a single list of games on human subdomain. I think it's time we rank them there, and then freeze some experiments that rank towards the bottom" ([owner directions](../owner-directions-2026-10-18.md)). The ranked list is live at [human.adamwhite.work/games/](https://human.adamwhite.work/games/), read from `apps/site/src/games.ts`. This file gives the reasons.

## How the order was set

Evidence, strongest first:

1. **The owner's own verdict after play**, read under the [Taste](design-language.md#taste) rule: "can't stop, even though it's hard" and "I need more" outrank "good start" and "potential". Verdicts are quoted from each game's `docs/playtests/owner-*.md` and the [ledger](feedback-ledger.md).
2. **How far the owner played** (game time and inputs; real minutes are unknown on every game, [calibration](playtester-calibration-2026-10-20.md#unknown)).
3. **Source-unread LLM sessions** (protocol 2 where available). They count for reproducible defects and for agreement with the owner, not as a fun score.
4. **Depth potential** (how much more game the current core can carry) and **HF showcase value** (how much of what only HF does is visible in play).

Evidence grade per row: **A** owner played and gave a verdict; **B** owner verdict is older than 10 days and many builds; **C** no owner verdict (LLM and design evidence only).

Close calls, said plainly:
- **The Pass over The Glance.** Both are clear owner keeps. The Pass got the longest run and "I need more!"; The Glance three short runs. The LLM quits on The Pass are about agency density (DL-32, DL-33), a fixable defect, not the core.
- **The Night Watch at 5.** Its verdict is 15 days old and its LLM evidence is the most consistent in the lab: one missing verb (relieve a tired watcher). Fixing that verb is the cheapest large gain on this list.
- **Hearsay above The Valley.** Both are "potential" verdicts. The owner played Hearsay much longer (248 inputs against 16) and listed concrete problems, which the Taste rule reads as a stake.
- **The Day You Say Nothing and Twice at the Well stay active.** Both have only positive owner verdicts and are graduated; ranking low is not freezing. Twice at the Well is also under work by another agent today.

## Frozen

Frozen, 2026-10-20: **Small Works, Neighbours, The Rope.** Frozen means:
- It stays playable at its current URL and listed on /games/ as "Frozen experiment".
- No further work: no polish rounds, playtests, HF upgrades or new features. Open ledger fixes for these games are marked frozen, not open.
- **The owner can unfreeze any of them** by saying so; work then resumes from the open rows, which are kept.

Why these three:
- **The Rope** is the only game the owner has rejected, twice. His "Keep it though" is honoured: it stays playable. Its open night-end bug (F-196, "after auto ending the night it still didn't end") stays unfixed under the freeze; say so if it should be fixed first.
- **Neighbours** has no owner verdict and is a study tool more than a game. Its failure mode is the one the lab already names: the town runs without the player (L15) and the screen tends toward a dashboard.
- **Small Works** has no owner verdict and its testers found little pressure. The same watching, low-agency pattern as the other two. Its HF faculty (demonstrated methods, 2.2) stays in HF and in its tests.

Why not a fourth: the next candidates, The Day You Say Nothing and Twice at the Well, have only positive owner verdicts. Good Hands and Hearsay were built today and have not had a fair owner play on their latest build. Freezing them would break the owner's "we shouldn't leave things half baked".

The game repositories were not edited by this ranking. Each game's own docs keep their state; this file, `apps/site/src/games.ts`, the ledger and the roadmap carry the freeze.

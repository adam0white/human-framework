# Next concrete policy question: released work and an underway supply trip

The [completed practice study](practice-incentive-results.md) identifies a current Camp policy consequence worth testing separately. In C1, a player holding the woodshed for one more minute causes Meryem to start gathering salvage for the next stage. The player then releases the current stage, but Meryem continues her underway trip. The frame completes at 135 rather than 101 in the immediate-garden control. This is an observed effect of job timing and policy, not a learning-law defect or proof that immediate interruption is preferable.

Current `desire` in [Camp 0.3](../src/games/camp-current.js) prepares next-stage materials while another worker occupies the current stage; `neighbor` retains an existing job until its completion. That can be sensible. The narrower question is whether Meryem should reconsider **her own gathering job** after a co-located worker releases an available stage of her accepted project. An owned meal, another person's action and a promise are different responsibilities; do not collapse them into a blanket cancel-and-replan rule.

Start with a source-pinned private comparison, not a new public planner. Reuse C1 as an explicitly known zero-elapsed development case: at the player's minute-85 cancellation, Meryem's just-started salvage trip has not yet paid a minute. Freeze a bounded ordinary timing variation with positive gathering time already paid and feasible released work, where completing the supply trip protects useful output or roof timing. A separate no-feasible-work guard can be checked, but cannot substitute for that substantive negative control. Compare the unchanged finish-current policy, the existing player's release/re-request intervention, and at most one minimal event-triggered reconsideration candidate. Read the actual `release`/`request` semantics before treating that intervention as available; retain consent, paid cancellations and output forfeiture. Do not remove this strong existing control merely to make an automatic candidate useful.

Record full continuations through useful project completion or a predeclared observation horizon, including both frame **and roof**, other concurrent work, current stock and installed materials, food, effort, bodies, practice and paid time. Earlier frame completion alone is not enough when supply collection or the roof is delayed. Distinguish eliminating a redundant player interaction from improving service/output. Preserve cases where keeping the current job wins.

If a candidate is implemented, keep it private and explicitly versioned. It can act only on legitimately shared current facts and the actor's own accepted work/owned pending task, while respecting actual capacity, materials and sunk time. It must not repeatedly cancel and restart at the same timestamp, invent free output, lose paid practice, preempt another actor or implicitly consume a ration. Compare the smallest direct host rule first; no shared cognition/faculty export is presumed. Keep the existing Camp/runtime sources and completed comparisons as attributable controls.

Promote only a reviewed, source-specific benefit with an intelligible user-facing consequence. Otherwise close the question without a policy change. This is not a request to reopen the completed practice, meal, action-offer or empirical learning studies, and their known results are not new independent validation cases. Further UI, physical-device and real-player understanding evidence remain separate scopes.

## Where computation closes

An engine-created state, supported scenario, valid configuration, seed, and sequence of supported commands are enough to compute a run without an LLM or external service. Actions have five kinds: `work`, `rest`, `eat`, `observe`, and `help`. Commands either delegate a round or supply one actor's action; every actor acts once in stable order, followed by shared clock advancement and consumption. Execution can fail after an admissible attempt consumes time and resources. The replay manifest records the scenario, options, commands, and engine version.

This is computational closure **within the supported schema**, not coverage of every situation a human can encounter. Unknown action kinds, command types, action/actor references, task skills, policies, and module keys are rejected. Invalid finite-value ranges and incompatible replay versions are rejected. Extra JSON metadata may be retained, but adding a field named `emotion`, `worship`, or `intelligence` does not give it causal behavior. Arbitrary state edits outside the command interface are not a documented replay intervention protocol. See the [API contract](mvp-spec.md) and [validation code](../src/core/model.js).

Courier Crossing, Repair Bench, and Water Commons reuse the same kernel without scenario-name branches. All three have the same aggregate-task structure: two actors make contributions toward one shared target while managing body proxies, a hidden scalar hazard, and shared rations. Commons adds recurring consumption. Solo Repair is the fourth preset and removes peers, promises and help; social ablations have no effect in that control. This demonstrates data reuse within that structure.

The separate workshop owns two-location travel, inventory, a repair target, an optional consumed part, paid inspection, success resolution and a deadline. Its human component supplies generic duration-aware body/practice transitions, one pending attempt and validated save/resume. Capacity rejection does not obtain food or select recovery. Independent hosts now include multiple pumps, parcel routing, household exchanges and continuing construction. The courtyard owns separate water and loan accounts; Common Ground owns reservations and concurrent jobs through the optional event clock. No general bargaining, navigation, social-cognition module or Full-policy embedding has been delivered. The laboratory retains its round scheduler and growing audit transcript; bounded active state is a property of the human/clock and newer host path.

|---|---|---|
| Body and action capacity | **Proxy.** Bounded fatigue/hunger accumulate with simulated minutes; exertion adds effort, rest recovers fatigue, and a confirmed ration reduces hunger. The laboratory substitutes recovery when full exertion exceeds capacity; the workshop stops exertion and leaves recovery choice to its host/player. The human component already supports partial-duration transitions and observed body projections. A logistic rule combines skill, difficulty, body and authored context into work success. No metabolic units, sleep, pain, injury, disease, sensory organs, or motor control. | Task-specific physiology and richer capacity/observation models remain proposed. Add detail only if it distinguishes measured responses that the coarse model misses. Reject a universal “energy” quantity presented as a physiological measurement without calibration. |
| Perception and access to information | **Implemented projection; proxy sensing.** `getView` excludes hidden hazard, replay seed, and other actors' private weights/intentions. It exposes rounded body estimates, public task descriptions, and peer state. Paid inspection produces a noisy report; survey proficiency affects its noise. Disabling belief learning removes stored hazard updates and inspection information value together; it does not implement a learned stopping rule. No vision, hearing, spatial occlusion, or general testimony system. | Give observations explicit channel, observer, source, and cost. Changing inaccessible world facts must not change a policy's choice before accessible evidence or effects differ. A renderer's diagnostics are not permissible NPC input. |
| Attention and working capacity | **Unmodeled.** Ranking reads the accessible fields directly. Inspection's time cost is not an attention bottleneck, and displaying recent events does not establish working-memory capacity. | A cue-selection or bounded-buffer mechanism should predict errors under distraction or load. Reject it if the same choices follow from simpler observation-access rules or if it silently grants extra information. |
| Beliefs, understanding, and metacognition | **Proxy beliefs; unmodeled general understanding.** One hazard estimate is blended with inspection reports. Confidence increases by a skill-dependent heuristic. This is not full Bayesian inference, probability calibration, causal discovery, or reasoning about another mind. The trace's `understand` phase formats body/resource facts; it does not execute a separate comprehension process. | Compare explicit belief-updating and planning alternatives on misleading, redundant, and changing evidence. | Memory and knowledge | **Audit records and persistent state only.** Hazard belief, proficiency, a promise, directed trust, support, and last action persist. `history` is an audit log, not episodic memory. The actor view contains recent public outcomes, but the default ranking policy does not retrieve or reason over that list. No semantic knowledge base, autobiographical recall, or forgetting/retrieval mechanism. | An actor-owned memory interface should store only experienced information and expose bounded retrieval. | Learning, retention, and transfer | **Proxy.** Work and inspection produce bounded task-proficiency gains, including failed work attempts. | Reasoning, planning, and imagination | **Proxy choice rule; other functions unmodeled.** The full policy scores immediate authored considerations with limited deadline terms. The baseline uses a simpler output/proficiency score. Neither searches action sequences, invents actions, solves arbitrary problems, or imagines counterfactual worlds. | A planner should compete behind the actor-view/choice boundary under an explicit computation budget. ## Islamic commitments and normative boundaries

The research starts from Islam as true, with Sunni Hanafi–Maturidi interpretation as the initial scholarly program. That commitment concerns what the project seeks to understand and how it handles personhood, accountability, purpose, and sources. It is not authenticated by the toy engine's results. The [Islamic foundations memo](../research/islamic-foundations.md) separates revealed text, interpretation, empirical claims, and implementation choices; the [historical audit](../research/historical-source-audit.md) rejects inherited sacred-variable equations.

**There is currently no normative or fiqh evaluator.** The kernel does not judge permissibility, obligation, sincerity, repentance, divine acceptance, or ultimate standing. Promise weights are authored reasons for action. Private intention is a recorded string, not a verified inward state. The engine has no scalar for qalb, nafs, ruh, fitrah, faith, spiritual health, or salvation, and no mapping that identifies its ranking function with ʿaql. These absences are limits of implementation, not claims that the theological referents do not exist.

A future reviewed scenario could positively represent worship, truth-seeking, service, hope, love, repentance, or a conflict between advantage and understood duty. It would need distinct records for the sourced normative reference, the character's understanding, chosen intention, observable action, and consequences. This interface is proposed. Its rejection tests are concrete: success cannot establish divine approval; distress cannot establish deficient faith; an observer cannot infer sincerity without evidence; and an unresolved contextual ruling must remain unresolved. No formula for divine judgment is an eligible extension. The restraint and intention distinctions are discussed with their textual sources in the [foundations memo](../research/islamic-foundations.md), including [Qur'an 17:85](https://quran.com/17/85) and [Bukhari 1](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1).

## Candidate discriminating experiments

These are proposed experiments, not completed acceptance claims. The [benchmark report](benchmark-report.md) records the executed comparison and its limits. Value/units repair and independent host/package integrations are implemented. The current [roadmap](roadmap.md) records the completed finite-game and simpler-mechanism studies, then prioritizes a two-host coordination-helper comparison and changing-evidence play before faculty promotion. The list below is a research menu, not a competing delivery order; a new social task alone does not prove host independence.

1. **Test information seeking beyond a fixed hazard.** Compare the current inspection policy, a simple stopping rule, and a simple threshold policy across redundant, misleading, and changing reports. Keep observation access, cost, and body dynamics matched. Record belief error, inspections, task outcomes, and why inspection stops. The historical 0.1 frozen-belief ablation repeated inspections; 0.2 fixes that score/update mismatch by disabling information value together with updates. This is not yet a learned stopping rule or an isolated estimate of a human faculty's benefit.
2. **Require value before promoting social reuse.** The [completed miniature-host probe](social-contract-probe.md) matches direct host rules while using more saved state, so its shared contract remains private and experimental. The courtyard and Common Ground still own their separate social rules. Any next real-host integration must identify a player need, hold body/learning fixed, measure bookkeeping and response clarity, and retain direct rules as the rival.
3. **Separate learning from output and retention.** The [matched-exposure practice probe](../research/learning-through-work.md) already compares trained-task, active-control and idle exposure and task retests. Extend it to a delayed retest only with an explicit retention hypothesis; temporary body impairment remains separate. The existing unused retention helper alone is not a simulated retention process or human learning validation.
4. **Measure tradeoffs under comparable exposure and parameter ranges.** Add a specified fixed-duration follow-up protocol instead of comparing only unequal early-stop endpoints. Vary uncertainty, scarcity, action costs, and authored coefficients; record cases where rankings reverse or mechanisms have no effect. Report task success, time, body costs, and promises separately. Do not tune the full policy until it wins.
5. **Require external evidence before human-validity claims.** Select one population and bounded task; specify observed choices/errors/timing, data provenance, calibration/evaluation separation, and simpler competing explanations before fitting. Synthetic checks establish software behavior. Matching outputs generated by this engine cannot validate the human mechanisms that generated them.

Additional physiology, cognitive modules, religious scenario evaluation, presentation experiments, and macro scale should follow the failures these tests reveal. No coverage percentage or claim of a complete human model follows from this ledger.

## Production admission decision: retain privately

The corrected candidate demonstrates earlier access to supported high-body work and reports, with extra explicit refusals and no service improvement in the registered continuation. It is **not promoted to the active UI or a framework API**. The four changed public files are restored byte-for-byte to app 0.14; the reviewed source, UI, original contract tests and documentation remain in [the private candidate archive](../artifacts/action-offers/candidate-source.json.gz) and commit `41430e89c6402ff149e092261bfd81ab15a30aa5`. Current candidate contract tests load its source-frozen graph, not active production modules. This closes the experiment under its declared stop rule.

A post-outcome ordinary-use audit provides the missing admission constraint. The actual browser constructor fixes both keeper body channels at .15, chooses a six-minute valve and limits the shift to thirty paid minutes. Even the loose assumption of maximal .015 exertion every minute bounds fatigue by `.15 + 30 × (.0015 + .015) = .645`, and hunger by `.15 + 30 × .002 = .21`. Recovery and meals only reduce those bounds. Rounded upper estimates are at most .675/.225. Adding the largest ordinary offered interval, six-minute repair, gives .756 fatigue and .237 hunger—below both admission ceilings. One-minute reports are cheaper. Hence no ordinary fresh-shift work or report offer can enter the uncertainty branch. This is an analytic source bound, not additional sampled policies or a physiological finding. [Source-checked calculation and assumptions](../artifacts/action-offers/ordinary-bounds.json) · [Reproducer](../artifacts/action-offers/ordinary-bounds.mjs).

The independent code reviewer separately derived the same reachability limit. Custom/imported high-body states are supported and remain legitimate framework cases, but their timing gains do not establish a present player-use need, measured clarity benefit or second consumer's authoring need. Do not add fatigue profiles or extend the timer merely to activate this candidate. Camp's already-authoritative offers remain unchanged. The smaller current interface is retained; the useful quantization and refusal evidence stays available for a concrete future consumer.

This ordinary-use audit was **post-outcome**, so it is not mislabeled preregistered. It changes the production admission decision, not any frozen case, caller, coefficient or result. Future mechanisms should pass an ordinary-use reachability check before candidate implementation whenever that check is cheap.

# Performance review, HF 2.0 (H2), 2026-10-04

Adversarial, read-only. Scope: `73f063a..ff95cb5` (HF L1–L6, engine 1.8/1.9, `answerNow`, Game 3 sim and saves, playtest exports). Earlier round: [2026-10-04-perf.md](2026-10-04-perf.md), decisions in [2026-10-04-summary.md](2026-10-04-summary.md); its items are not re-raised unless the new code changes their weight (V10, V12 below).

**How measured.** Node 26, Apple Silicon. Scratch scripts (not committed) bundle the game and the framework *source* with esbuild into one file and drive Game 3 with the repo's headless Keeper (`apps/site/test/watch/years.ts`), seed 1 unless noted. Sizes are `JSON.stringify` lengths. Profiles are `node --cpu-prof`; profile names are mapped back to source lines below. Vitest timings come from `vitest run --project site apps/site/src/watch/sim/years.test.ts`. The 50-year `years.timing.ts` was **not** re-run (see P4); its figure is extrapolated. Other agents may have been using the machine.

**Code measured.** Sizes, phase timings, the per-year series, the years 1–5 profile and the first Vitest run ran on clean `ff95cb5`. The hash compare (P3), the years 25–27 profile and the Vitest re-run (P4, P9) ran after other agents had begun uncommitted H2 edits in this worktree (bell targeting, opening carry, `restore` bounds, a `tick` guard). Those edits change game rules, not the cost structure, and the P3 hash compare runs both modes on the same code. File:line references are at `ff95cb5`.

## Top line

1. **Saves are bounded, but the bound is ~290 KB per person and is reached around year 25.** No per-person slice grows without bound: the year-1→12 growth the findings recorded is slices *filling toward caps*, led by the day chronicle (120 lived days ≈ 20+ years at Game 3's ~5 lived days a year). Two slices Game 3 never reads, `trace` and the day `chronicle`, are ~60% of a mature person.
2. **Played nights are ~79% of a year, and the cost is the per-minute advance of every person, not decisions.** `stepCommunity` ticks *everyone* to `until` at the end of each call (`sim/sim.ts:1107`); Game 3 calls it every minute, so each of ~30 people runs every faculty in 1-minute segments. `tick`/`advanceSegment` is 42–44% of total time; `decide` ~8%.
3. **Cost per person-minute rises ~35% over 30 years** (17.5 → ~24 µs), tracking `s.order` (everyone ever: 107 at year 30) through `presentIds` → `personOf`, and `childrenOf` scanning relationships that fill from 14 to 67 per person. By years 25–27 these lookups are ~10–15% of the run.
4. **The 50-year check is slow mostly because of the sim; the harness adds ~1.5–2×.** The same 3-year replay takes 4.3 s bundled and 6.5 s under Vitest's module runner. The site bench keeps the runner only because of two parameter-property constructors.

## Where a night minute goes (profile, years 1–5, 9.8 s)

`stepMinute` 85% → `stepPeople`/`stepCommunity` 61–64% → `tick` 44% (`advanceSegment` 42%: `advanceMemory` 12.7%, `advanceHabits` 2.8%, `advanceSocial` 2.8%, `advanceAffect` 2.4%, `advanceBody` 2.2%) and `decide` 7.7%; `checkMoments` 18.7% (`wavering` 17.1%, `predict` 14.6%). Seasons (`stepSeason`) 9.6%, of it `liveCommunity` 11% including dawn days. GC 1.9%.

**Late profile (years 25–27, resumed from a year-25 page, 31 people, 89 in the cast):** `tick` 44.5%, `advanceMemory` 15.7% (self 5.4%), `decay`/`dpow` 19.2% (all callers), `checkMoments` 13.0% (`wavering` 11.3%), `decide` 6.7%, `liveCommunity` 14.2%. Lookups that scale with years lived now show: `childrenOf` 5.5% and `householdOf` 5.0% (overlapping; via `routineFor`, `life.ts:255`), `personOf` 4.4%, `presentIds` 4.3%. Absolute times under the profiler are inflated; use the shares.

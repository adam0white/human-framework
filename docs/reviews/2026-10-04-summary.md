# Review actions, 2026-10-04

What was done about each finding in the four R2 reviews: performance, quality, security and mobile. The four review files were deleted on 2026-10-05 once every open row was here or in the [roadmap](../roadmap.md); they remain in the repository history (for example at commit 2b7acc5, `docs/reviews/2026-10-04-{perf,quality,security,mobile}.md`; the mobile review has the Galaxy S26 viewport research). One row per finding. Status is **acted** (with the commit), **done earlier** (acted before this pass, with the commit), **deferred** (with the reason and the phase that picks it up) or **rejected** (with the reason). Informational findings that asked for nothing are listed as **no action**.

Phases are Goal 2's (see [HANDOFF.md](../../HANDOFF.md)): G3-2 to G3-4 (Game 3), H2 (the HF 2.0 release). **Status 2026-10-05:** of the 17 deferred rows, V12 was done in 2.0 (`Retention.trace`, H2 P1), 3a in part (H2 Q3) and 5 in part (H2 Q34); the rest are open and listed in the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) (V10 as H2 P6).

**Counts (88 rows):** 52 acted (46 in this pass, 3 of them in part; 6 earlier), 17 deferred, 7 rejected, 12 no action.

Verification for this pass: `npm run check` and `npm run build` pass. The production build was served by `wrangler dev`, which applies `_headers`, and loaded in Playwright (separate contexts, mobile emulation, DPR 3) at 360×690, 384×832, 412×891, 780×360 and 891×412. All three games load, their workers start, the colony and voice clocks advance, no console errors or CSP violations appear, and the full-screen control sets `document.fullscreenElement` in all three games. The new headers have not been checked on the live site; that happens after the next deploy.

Must before tagging: Q1 (test or drop `betroth`/`observeSkill`), Q6–Q7 (the `dayFold`/`foldDay` and three-way `consolidate` names), Q20–Q22 (`restore` lets negative weights, unkeyed character state and unbounded bond values into live decision terms), Q40–Q41 (README engine version, stale api.md), Q49 (Game 3 pregnancy outside the framework).

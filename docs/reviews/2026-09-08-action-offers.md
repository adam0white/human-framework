## Why the candidate was not promoted

The twenty records show earlier inspection, repair and reporting in supported high-body cases, additional deliberate refusals, six physical endpoint ties, and no service increase in the registered deadline continuation. All records reproduce exactly on minimum Node 22. The whole-repair caller beats a competent short-interval/recovery control by one minute; its greater ending fatigue remains a cost. These narrow outcomes do not establish human clarity or a second consumer's need.

An independent post-outcome source analysis showed that ordinary fresh Across shifts cannot reach uncertainty at all. Their .15/.15 starting condition, thirty-minute horizon and greatest authored effort rate bound actual fatigue/hunger by .645/.21. The largest ordinary offered interval fits even upper-bin estimates. [Reviewer admission note](../../artifacts/action-offers/reviews/code/admission-note.md) · [Root source-checked calculation](../../artifacts/action-offers/ordinary-bounds.json). The reviewer used source reads and reported outcomes for this admission recommendation, without independently rerunning the twenty records.

Root therefore retains the corrected candidate, original/corrected reviews and complete results privately. Current public controls, runtime, routes and save versions remain unchanged. No fatigue profiles, longer shift or additional game were invented to manufacture a use. This is a completed negative production-admission decision with useful private boundary evidence.

# Preregistered body and practice mechanism comparison

Registered 2026-09-08 before running comparison outcomes. This is a private synthetic software experiment, not a study of people. Human/runtime 0.1.1 and its transitive model stay frozen at source base `33418c4`. The protocol and its numeric condition table are committed before implementation. An implementation commit and hashed freeze manifest precede reserved and sensitivity execution. Never overwrite retained reports. Any outcome-informed change requires a dated amendment and a new reserved partition; do not relabel inspected outcomes as unseen.

## Question and competing explanations

Does the current two-channel fatigue/hunger plus saturating task-practice kit provide distinctions needed by a small host that a genuinely smaller stamina and paid-practice-counter model loses? More output is not a model win: neither model is observed human truth. We compare operational equivalence, prediction scope, recovery/resource consequences, and implementation costs. Preserve exact agreement and conditions favoring the small model. The result can support a game-authoring choice, not physiological accuracy, human learning validity, player understanding or measured human authoring effort.

Three alternatives were considered: reuse the existing controller comparison (does not isolate body/practice); remove capacity from the rival (unfair); compare the frozen kit with an independent scalar pool and task counters under prescribed actions (selected). No model gets a controller, future information, automatic recovery, free ration, hidden safety state copied from the other model, or fitted condition-specific coefficient.

## Frozen models

Human uses its released create/begin/advance/finish/export/restore boundary and its existing effort and success functions. It retains two body numbers and per-task proficiency. The rival has one stamina number, initial task proficiency and paid-minute counters. Its initial stamina is `1 - (2*fatigue + hunger)/3`; this makes its initial success load equal to Human's. Each elapsed minute drains `1/600` stamina, active effort drains `2/3 * effort * elapsed / duration`, rest restores `1/60` per minute, and a completed owned meal restores `11/60`. Clamp stamina to [0,1]. Exertive work is admitted only if the entire projected drain fits remaining stamina (tolerance 1e-12). The same rule applies to all conditions. No recovery is earned by blocked or resource-rejected work. A meal adds relief only once after completing its paid interval.

Rival proficiency is `initial + (1-initial)*min(1, 0.0052*paidPracticeMinutes)` and counters cap at `1/0.0052`; there is no unbounded historical list. This matches Human's initial learning slope, not its diminishing-return curve. Rival success uses the same logistic form, task difficulty, hazard and exposure, with body load `2.4*(1-stamina)` in place of `1.6*fatigue+0.8*hunger`. These constants are engineering choices analytically matched at initialization; they are not fitted to trial outcomes. Sensitivity multiplies only the rival's learning rate and separately its recovery effectiveness by 0.75 and 1.25. The asymmetry is deliberate: released Human stays fixed while the rival's arbitrary parameter dependence is disclosed.

The competing capacity rules are part of the mechanism comparison. Both enforce their own finite budget. Scalar pooling can permit exertion that one Human channel blocks, or conflate a meal and rest. We record every admission disagreement; extra output from a looser gate does not establish better physical performance. A common hidden fatigue/hunger gate would make the rival larger and would cease testing independent capacity predictions.

## One host and matched information

The host owns action definitions, deterministic time, parts, food and expected output. A and B work have equal effort, duration, difficulty (0.5), hazard (0.1), exposure (0.2), output (10), and part cost (1); only practiced task differs. A permitted work admission consumes its part immediately. Output is the start-of-attempt success probability times 10, credited only for a completed work interval. There is no sampled success and no random seed. Report expected output, never realized wins. A blocked interval lasts its entire prescribed duration as maintenance-only time, with zero practice, parts, output or implicit recovery. This is the supplied schedule's time budget, not an extra model-specific penalty. Missing food/parts similarly becomes maintenance-only idle for the supplied interval. An interrupted admitted work interval keeps its paid practice and spent part, but produces no output. This is the common host contract, not a Human rule.

Both adapters receive exactly the same action fields and initial conditions. The host has a single schedule and resource path and no model-specific policy branch. Model state is used only for admission, forecast, transition and serialization. Traces retain offered actions, admitted/blocked/resource status, paid minutes by activity, practice by task, parts/food consumed, success forecasts and output. No access to an inaccessible world variable is needed: this host has no hidden world variables.

## Fixed conditions and partitions

The exact conditions are in [protocol.json](../artifacts/mechanism-comparison/protocol.json). All partitions are authored in advance and visible in source; “reserved” means outputs withheld until implementation freeze, not blind design or a real external test set.

Development: idle control; balanced A/B work, rest and meals; sustained work; hungry rest/work; fatigued meal/work; scarce food/parts; and matched-retet protocols from novice and practiced starts. Reserved: independent body-channel alias pairs with identical initial stamina/load; a longer mixed workload; a material-shortage schedule; and a new mixed-skill, strained retest start. Sensitivity: repeat all development/reserved conditions with each of the four predeclared rival coefficient variants; never tune after inspecting this partition.

Every retest compares six 20-minute exposure intervals of A practice, B practice, or idle (120 minutes), followed by 40 minutes rest and a 10-minute owned meal in every arm. From that endpoint, independently branch into the same paid 10-minute A or B retest. No body reset or free washout occurs. Initial body and resources match within a condition. Compare A versus B exposure to isolate task specificity under equal effort/time: their body paths must match within each model. Idle has different exertion and therefore need not have matching body. Record actual admitted practice; blocked exposure is not training. Report both stored proficiency and executable retest forecast/admission. Do not infer learning from aggregate production or erase practice costs.

## Measures and predeclared interpretation

For every paired prescribed schedule, record total attempted duration, admitted work/rest/meal minutes, blocked/resource-idle minutes, food/part cost, expected output, per-task paid practice, admissions and start forecasts. Operational equivalence requires identical per-step capacity/resource decisions and resources, absolute expected-output difference at most 5% of offered output (10 times offered work intervals), and mean absolute work forecast difference at most 0.03 over jointly admitted work; a zero-work pair is equivalent only with identical resources/decisions and zero output. In retests, equivalence additionally requires identical admission and forecast difference at most 0.03 for each A/B branch. Thresholds are a declared small-game tolerance, not statistical significance. No aggregate winning score or p-value.

Record individual counterexamples and partitions separately, then counts of equivalent/divergent pairs. Describe which distinctions the smaller model cannot represent even where outputs are close: separate food/fatigue bottlenecks and diminishing returns. Both lack forgetting, transfer by default, general cognition, physiology and empirical calibration. Report comparisons of recovery schedules as consequences of authored rules, not independently confirmed recovery benefits.

Count UTF-8 bytes and nonblank source lines for model/adapters, the common host, and frozen transitive source; state both inclusive and narrow scopes to avoid counting unrelated exported model helpers as required mechanics. Count persistent body/skill fields and host exceptional paths by code inspection, not automated “cognitive complexity.” Report any model-specific host rules or edits to frozen source (required zero). Source lines and agent work are not measured human authoring effort.

Performance: after implementation freeze, run each adapter/host for 10,000 fixed commands (repeated work A 20 / rest 20 / meal 10), with 4,000 parts and food owned at initialization. Include actual admission, forecast, lifecycle and host accounting. Warm up 300 commands, then five measured fresh 10,000-command runs per model, alternating order. Predeclared desktop p95 budget is 5 ms/command and active saved-state budget is 8 KiB/person+host for this fixed two-skill host; these are engineering budgets, not requirements of human thought.

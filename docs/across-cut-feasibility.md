# Actor-local information: completed private feasibility milestone

Completed 2026-09-08. The new private headless host makes separately owned observations, paid reports, independent contributions and actual work executable around frozen Human/runtime 0.1.1. The public app remains 0.9; no page, public source, package export or runtime formula changed.

**Decision:** proceed to a narrow [factual-report playable slice](across-cut-player-slice.md), while leaving the proposal/confirmation-centered premise and general cognition promotion unearned. The tested facts change useful water/work decisions. More elaborate notebook/proposal protocols do not improve the observed service count over simpler factual reports, and final confirmation adds paid messages without a service gain here. Human usefulness still needs actual feedback.

## What is implemented

A keeper and receiver operate separated stations. Each has its own body, inventory, paid jobs, observations, inbox, outgoing records, known proposal revisions and contribution decisions. Actor views exclude hidden peer conditions, private launch facts, actual future message outcomes, global IDs/queues and the other person's command budget. Equal-local-history pairs exercise views, action errors and known-event forecasts. A local advance may stop when a receipt actually becomes observable after elapsed time; it cannot reveal a hidden future arrival beforehand.

Radio/contact transmissions, inspection, repair, travel, attendance, releases and cart trips pay actual Human exposure. Parts and water conserve; partial work and positions persist. Own acceptance/withdrawal, sent response, received response and actual fulfillment are separate. A sent message never gains hidden delivery status. The ordinary episode ends at a public 30-minute horizon while the launch deadline may remain privately unknown. [Exact frozen contract](across-cut-contract.md) · [Independent isolation evidence](across-cut-isolation.md).

These are accessible records and authored decision rules, not a model of human comprehension or complete deductive knowledge. The current notebook supports this finite cue set; it is not a general witness/trust representation. In particular, `peerPresent` is observer-relative and should stay a local contact cue, not be presented as an unqualified remote fact. The proposed public slice limits reports to first-hop absolute observations and preserves source times. Passive radio reception does not implement attention. No older memory/coordination/social candidate was promoted or retuned.

## Discriminating results

The frozen batch comprises **155 development runs** (144 policy runs across 12 arms and 12 configurations, plus 11 scripts or mixed continuations) and **15 withheld runs** (12 policy runs plus 3 scripts). All 170 complete journals and both actors' full local decision inputs replay on minimum Node 22. Source freeze `74d84d1` binds `dc87a06` and 18 exact source files before the withheld runs. All first, corrected and strengthened results remain in the private repo; no post-withheld tuning occurred. [Complete comparison, controls and reproduction](across-cut-comparison.md).

The reliable matrix covers every valve/inlet/launch combination; four earlier delayed/lossy variants remain. Across the twelve development configurations, two-way factual reports, retained last-report radio and both notebook arms each serve 21 units. Two-way reports and retained last-report radio each use 24 radio charges versus 45 with confirmation and 38 without. One-way report and cart-first no-radio each serve 20; the one-way strategy avoids lost/excess water in those cases, while cart-first uses no radio but loses 8 units and has 2 excess units. Attendance costs also differ. These are separately reported tradeoffs across authored cases, not a weighted score or universal ranking.

The original easy fixed no-radio route remains, including literal cart insurance: two units served plus one unnecessary excess unit. Root's cart-first control secures two units with a short valve and one with a long valve at the early launch, without messages. This corrected a tempting claim that the backward report was uniquely necessary for the raw service guarantee. Factual reports can still save real repair, release, cart/attendance work and water. [Root exploratory sequence](../artifacts/across-cut-root/cart-first-control.json).

In source-scripted prefixes followed by actual local notebook decisions, a received withdrawal stops the keeper releasing two remaining units; a lost withdrawal leaves that actor releasing and losing them. Positive response or confirmation is still not a physical prerequisite. These continuations demonstrate a causal choice from received information, not autonomous negotiation.

Withheld outcomes retain failures: a delayed report makes the one-way strategy serve one and lose two units, while a conservative fixed schedule serves two without radio in that same case. Two-way reports also serve two at 17 with two charges; notebook variants serve two at 18 with five/four charges. Lost confirmation still permits two units, and deliberately interrupted attendance loses both pipe units. Final confirmation did not gain service; retry remains unexercised, so this is not an impossibility claim about every possible protocol.

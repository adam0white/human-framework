# Service day: small policies and retained consequences

Two small public-view policies cover the authored day without a new planning faculty. In the frozen 12 development and 6 reserved runs, both policies protect the inlet and deliver both clinic units. They differ in morning water service, materials and paid work. This establishes a policy/carryover demonstration under identical Human physics, not Human-versus-body-model evidence, human validity or measured usefulness.

## Every condition

All times are absolute minutes from creation, including legal paid/idle prefixes. Closing remains 64; there is no refunded evaluation time. Both arms protect the inlet in every row. `water` means morning water service, not later clinic supply. Every clinic result below is **two units**, so cells give its completion minute. `work/rest` are combined paid person-minutes through closing; they include elective recovery after a service is already finished and are not minimum required costs. Full per-person/task breakdowns and idle time are in each artifact. Each person consumes its one owned meal in all 18 runs; no meals remain or are granted between events.

Both late-start policies converge on diversion. All runs end without a policy error, refused policy request or liveness failure. The simpler resource/recovery rules suffice for these cases; this is no guarantee for other conditions or a new human planning result.

The interrupted-diversion reserved case is a retained negative finding: deadline-first begins additional gate work after a paid partial diversion, later reverts, uses the shed spare and pays 14 extra work minutes plus 9 extra rest minutes for the same service outcomes. Its original rule remains frozen. Reserve-clinic continues the partial diversion and leaves the shed spare. Do not describe either priority as universally dominant.

The usual direct route installs four parts (including the fetched shed spare), while diversion installs three and leaves the shed spare. Final owned and reserved parts are zero in every policy run. In the fresh deadline-first run, keeper/partner install 3/1 parts and pay 18/22 work minutes; the partner's work includes eight minutes fetching the spare and two minutes handing it over. Fresh reserve-clinic installs 2/1 parts and pays 15/12 work minutes. All transfers are requests/accepted work with ownership receipts, and Deniz's own clinic work remains independently controlled. No lack of refusals in this small matrix establishes absence of meaningful refusal rules; host tests and explicit refused-command harness tests exercise them separately.

## Matched earlier-choice branches

Both branches start from the same fresh exported save and complete the keeper's meal at 4. The gate branch completes sections at 10/16; the diversion branch finishes diversion at 10. Both wait to 24 with the same automatic partner rule, retaining all body/practice/resource consequences. At 24 the gate branch has zero keeper parts and fatigue estimate .85; the diversion branch has one keeper part and fatigue estimate .65. Hunger estimate is .35 in both. No body or inventory is reset.

The prescribed pump request at 24 **does not discriminate**: Deniz has already reserved the pump in both branches, so both return `TARGET_BUSY`. This negative check remains in the artifact. After the common advance to 30, the same frozen deadline-first continuation gives the gate branch one cart unit at 60, and the diversion branch two piped units at 45. The former retains morning water; the latter forfeits it. The gate continuation fetches the spare, pays more recovery and misses full piped service under this controller. It is not proof that the gate route is physically impossible: the full-policy development rows demonstrate successful gate routes with earlier coordination.

These branches show bundled earlier-choice consequences persisting into later options and a shared continuation. They do not isolate fatigue from resources, prove an optimal schedule or provide a physiological causal estimate. The original target-busy test is not relabeled as a successful immediate availability contrast.

## Implementation bounds and limits

The frozen selector is 1,332 UTF-8 bytes/26 nonblank lines, plus 2,945 bytes/51 shared-helper lines: **4,277 bytes/77 nonblank lines inclusive for either arm**. Both branches of the shared selector are included in that accounting. The host is 25,910 bytes/160 nonblank lines; the evidence harness is 9,777 bytes/109 nonblank lines. Compact formatting strongly affects line counts. This measures source, not human authoring effort, usability or cognitive complexity.

Across the frozen 18 runs, maximum active save is 5,815 bytes, detached policy view 11,304 bytes, and pending events four. Policy persistent state is empty (`null` serialization is four bytes). These remain below predeclared 64/32/4 KiB save/view/controller budgets. Evidence traces are intentionally separate and larger. CPU latency and rendering were not measured; the runtime metadata names Apple M4, arm64 Darwin and Node 26.8.1. No physical-mobile claim follows.

Initial hunger cannot physically block an on-time heavy action before minute 64 in this authored scenario. Meals change actual Human hunger and the partner's authored preferences, but these runs do not establish that eating is mechanically necessary. Fatigue, scarce parts and paid timing matter. Preserve this limitation instead of retuning the host to manufacture a stronger food claim. Human explanation testing, empirical calibration, theological review and useful authoring effort remain separate open tracks.

## Reproduction

Use Node >=22, fresh output paths and the original frozen checkout for manifest 1. The CLI refuses overwrite, uncommitted manifests, changed host/policy/runtime/protocol source, or reserved evaluation without a committed source freeze. Later host corrections require a new manifest and explicitly labeled validation; they cannot silently reuse an earlier reserved result.

```sh
PATH=/opt/homebrew/bin:$PATH node scripts/service-day-comparison.js run --partition development --freeze artifacts/service-day/freeze.json --out /tmp/service-development-new.json
PATH=/opt/homebrew/bin:$PATH node scripts/service-day-comparison.js run --partition reserved --freeze artifacts/service-day/freeze.json --out /tmp/service-reserved-new.json
```

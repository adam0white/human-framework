Historical selected proposal, 2026-09-08, after the completed private feasibility study. The [current actor-local execution boundary](actor-local-player-execution.md), selected after app 0.13, supersedes the obsolete release numbers, route/save preservation and replay obligations below. ## Scope and evidence

Build one mobile-first, keeper-controlled thirty-minute shift: deliver up to two units before the launch while choosing how much repair, radio time and water to spend. The receiver follows a disclosed deterministic policy. Preserve the frozen private 155-development/15-reserved study, core `b3ec694`, policies `dc87a06`, and its negative confirmation result. Public app 0.9 remains the current delivery until a separately verified release.

Recommend a timestamped factual notebook with optional first-hop reports. A one-way read-only radio game is cheaper but removes useful player information choices. Full proposal/acceptance/confirmation adds costs without demonstrated value here; omit those commands and displays. This is a candidate game, not a promoted cognition faculty.

## Mobile flow

1. **Start shift.** Explain valve → inlet → launch geometry, two-minute release plus three-minute transit, actual inlet attendance, one-unit cart fallback, and the public channel bounds. Show the receiver’s policy. Use “New shift” and an opaque episode identifier; no condition names, private parameter selectors or scenario-bearing URLs.
2. **Work surface.** A single column shows minute, your location, your job, owned water/radio/fitting, and useful body/capacity estimates. The inlet panel says “No report received” until evidence arrives. No remote actor animation, progress bar or global service score appears through hidden state.
3. **Act.** Show inspect, repair with a short-interval option, release, send selected facts, and paid travel/rest/meal where legal. Primary controls remain visible above the notebook. Every button states duration and reserved/consumed resources. Keep “Stop work” beside an active job and “Continue” below it. Release states that stopping before completion returns both reserved units; completed release cannot be recalled.
4. **Receive and reconsider.** Pause elapsed time when a report actually arrives. Expand its concise factual card while keeping the active job visible: receipt pauses the interface, not the job’s lifecycle. The player can continue or explicitly stop and choose again. Never automatically finish a release while opening its report.

## Facts and provenance

Render ordinary play exclusively from the keeper’s detached actor view and player-owned UI state. Reports display the envelope sender, station, observation time, send time and actual receipt time: “Receiver reports inlet repair needs 14 minutes; observed 1, sent 2, received 8.” Separate “observed here” from “received report”; remote reports never become current-world assertions.

Allow only enumerated absolute facts: repair requirement/progress, launch notice/departure, service tally and own cart-delivery result. At admission, require selected records to be the sender’s own local observations; reject relays and other message kinds. Send the chosen source records unchanged. Exclude `peerPresent`: it is observer-relative, and existing records lack a general witness chain. Do not invent one in presentation. Retain late old records, but select current notebook claims by source time, not last arrival. Sending consumes time and charge; its display never gains hidden delivery status.

## Receiver and time driver

Use a small public receiver policy, separately versioned and described as authored behavior. It inspects, reports its local launch/repair facts, repairs in paid one-minute decisions, then attends continuously while pipe service remains possible. If its own remaining repair cannot finish before launch, it takes the useful cart immediately. It may also switch to cart when actually received upstream facts establish that even the earliest possible pipe arrival is too late. A mere missing report establishes neither failure nor refusal. Stop attending after locally observed full service or departure. Record actual attending cost; this deliberately broad policy supports a human who departs from the study’s earliest schedule.

The policy receives only its own detached view/state. Derive any impossibility bound conservatively from received source-time facts and declared physics, never assumed human action times. A late report may trigger an explicit interrupted job before the cart request. Keep decisions and reasons private to that actor until legitimately reported or the episode ends.

The driver pays integer minutes internally so the NPC can react between them. Capture both actor views before same-minute decisions and use a documented bounded dispatch order. Ordinary rendering receives only the keeper view. “Continue” advances until own completion, an actually received report, a meaningful locally observed change, a known local boundary, or horizon. Compare inbox receipts and selected local changes; do not use `nextReceipt` alone, because continuous own repair generates receipts every minute. Never peek at queued arrivals to name or schedule a user-visible stop. A hidden early departure cannot prematurely end the keeper’s shift. No wall-clock time accrues while paused.

## Acceptance and debrief

- At bounded delay six, valve six/inlet fourteen/launch fifteen: inspect 0–1, repair 1–7, start release 7; receive the receiver’s completed minute-two report at 8. Stop preserves two pipe units and one paid release minute; continuing consumes and loses them. Demonstrate both through the real UI without hidden setup labels.
- Full-view, DOM, labels, affordances, errors and future-boundary equality for private launch/work/channel twins until legitimate observation; first-hop rejection and stale-report ordering.
- Save/resume at that mid-release receipt, repeated pauses, interrupted travel/cart, horizon closure, receiver adaptation and budget exhaustion; no unpaid work, lost stop control or double delivery.
- Retain immediate cart, cart-first no-radio, fixed early/conservative and paid contact as serious exact-world comparison controls. Show service, water loss/excess, work and radio separately. Existing study totals cannot certify the new NPC. Verify representative successes and failures without retuning frozen evidence.
- Keyboard and narrow-screen controls, strict save validation, public import graph/private-path 404s, and existing-game regression. Automated checks do not establish human understanding.

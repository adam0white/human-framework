## Question, information and evaluation unit

Can paying for a received response or an optional confirmation change a worthwhile local choice when physical service does not require messages? Compare actual service units and times, lost water, owned supplies, completed/partial work, active/travel/radio/receiving exposure and recovery separately. There is no weighted reward, tuned communication bonus, correctness-by-agreement score, optimality claim, human evidence or faculty promotion.

Each replaceable controller receives only one detached actor-local host view and a detached copy of its own policy state. Common task possibilities, costs, channel mode and bounds are allowed. The runner owns setup, cases, world progression and researcher accounting; it never passes case IDs, setup, raw saves/world, future channel outcomes, hidden peer progress or queue information to a controller. Both actors' decisions at a shared minute are computed from pre-dispatch views. Full local decision inputs and returned decisions are retained through a deduplicated content-addressed dictionary, not replaced by a few selected facts. No policy can discover an incoming-message time by asking for a hidden next event. Ordinary one-minute advancement is allowed; elapsed work is paid before a receipt is observed.

Reliable fixed/bounded-delay modes permit inference that a completed transmission has arrived after its known maximum delay. That inference does not establish agreement, actual work or return-message delivery. Lossy missing replies remain ambiguous. Observation source time and receipt time, proposal identity/revision, a person's own response/withdrawal, response transmission and actual physical progress remain separate.

## Frozen rival families

These names denote local policies, not instructions selected from hidden conditions. Each arm uses the same legal host action set and initial conditions.

| Arm | Local decision rule and serious alternative retained |
|---|---|
| `cart-only` | Receiver immediately takes its owned one-unit cart if physically feasible; keeper may recover. This is the guaranteed-service candidate, not a straw baseline. |
| `no-radio-earliest` | Both inspect and repair locally as soon as paid capacity permits. Keeper releases at the earliest locally feasible completion; receiver attends the earliest declared arrival interval while preserving a feasible ten-minute cart fallback. Neither reads remote facts. |
| `fixed-early` | Common pre-agreed early release/receive rendezvous using the declared short-repair timing; receiver allows useful attendance before cart insurance. No messages. |
| `fixed-conservative` | Common rendezvous covering the declared longer keeper repair bound where locally feasible. Receiver uses its own departure notice/repair readiness to decide whether to attend or take cart; keeper can adapt to its own repair progress and use a declared late fallback schedule. Reliable transport bounds do not forbid improved fixed schedules. |
| `contact` | Pay useful six-minute each-way travel to see the other's facts and/or convey plans in person; preserve the resulting travel overhead, own-site return and lost cart/work opportunities. Physical contact may dominate messages, in which case narrow/reject the game. |
| `reactive-radio` | Inspect, send a truthful local report, act from the most recent currently received remote message and common fallback. No retained remote report history, but ordinary own work/completion bookkeeping is allowed. No arbitrary forced waiting. |
| `notebook` | Retain all received claims by source/observation time plus exact proposal/revision responses. Send truthful reports and feasible proposals, accept/refuse independently, revise when useful, honor chosen work or withdraw, use reliable-bound inference, and use an affordable timeout/retry in lossy mode. Confirmation is optional. |
| `notebook-no-confirm` | Same notebook family with final receipt-only confirmation omitted. It may honor accepted terms and receive water without that confirmation. Time/radio saved or extra retries are measured interventions. |

Repair failure/capacity refusal must trigger sensible owned recovery where remaining local time permits, without inspecting hidden state. Fixed schedules may be improved during development; every original losing controller/source/result is retained if an outcome informs revision. No tuning follows reserved evaluation. A clairvoyant schedule, if needed to diagnose physical feasibility, is labeled **physical-feasibility oracle** and is never pooled with actor-local rivals or called a policy win.

## Separate analyses

**Policy interventions:** different legal policies produce different communication, recovery, travel and work. Compare physical outputs and costs directly, report simple wins and overhead/failures, and do not attribute a difference solely to a representation or acknowledgment.

**Identical paid-script representation check:** feed exactly the same actor-local received envelopes and executed physical/communication journal into a timestamped notebook and a last-arrival record. Report whether stale arrivals overwrite newer observations or exact proposal revisions, with identical world outcomes and paid exposures. This is bookkeeping behavior, not service superiority. The old candidate is not modified/imported/promoted. A confirmation-only envelope and an equal-cost alternative envelope must carry identical fresh-readiness information (none); acknowledge if such a pair changes no consequential choice.

## Evidence and rejection rules

Retain initial configuration only in researcher artifacts; initial/final strict saves and hashes; complete compact replay commands; both local decision views/state and decisions; sent/delivered envelopes and consent; owned/reserved/consumed resources; final physical service/loss; actual paid Human exposure by actor/task; unfinished/interrupted work; command count and peak active-save bytes. Replay verifies each recorded transition hash, the exact final save/projection and a mid-run import/resume continuation. A later replay must also validate local decision-input bytes against the replayed host, not merely rerun world commands. Full source hashes and byte/nonblank-line counts include host, comparison and frozen runtime/model dependencies. Output files refuse overwrite.

Reject/narrow the proposed game if strong fixed/cart/contact schedules weakly dominate every useful condition, if confirmation never changes a consequential local choice, or if any apparent benefit requires forced waiting, disabling useful travel/work, telepathy, free communication, treating a promise as completed work or mechanically gating receipt on acknowledgment. A competent notebook sufficing rejects a new generic abstraction. Private local-information engineering may still be useful without a new public game. No public deployment or version bump belongs in this lane.

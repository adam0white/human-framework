# Across the Cut private host contract

Frozen before host outcomes, 2026-09-08. Host `0.1.0`, save `1`; imports frozen Human/runtime `0.1.1` and clock `0.1.0`. This authored software preflight makes no empirical claim. No existing runtime, public asset or package changes.

## Pure API and configuration

`create(options={})`, `request(state, actorId, action)`, `interrupt(state, actorId)`, `advance(state, target, options={})`, `getActorView(state, actorId)`, `getKnownLocalBoundary(state, actorId)`, `exportState(state)`, `restoreState(save)`, `getWorldSummary(state)`. All return detached values/new states; no actor policies run inside transitions. IDs are `keeper` and `receiver`. Both may submit legal decisions at the same clock minute; callers should capture both views before deciding. Researcher state/saves/summary must never be policy inputs.

Setup accepts only `valveMinutes` (6 or 12; default6), `inletMinutes` (2 or14; default2), `launchAt` (15 or27; default27), `channelMode` (`reliable`, `bounded`, `lossy`; default `reliable`), `channelSeed` (nonnegative integer; default0), `channelOverrides` (map of `sender:completionMinute` to2,6,or `loss`, allowed by mode), `bodies` (optional keeper/receiver `{fatigue,hunger}`; default .15/.15). Overrides are researcher-only exogenous time slots: all radio transmissions completed by a sender at that minute use the same channel result, regardless of earlier traffic. Reliable delay2; bounded delay2/6; lossy delay2/6/loss. Actors know only mode and possible delay bounds.

`request` accepts:

- `{task:'inspect'}`: one active minute inspects current station's repair requirement. Dock notice is visible without inspection on being at dock.
- `{task:'repair', minutes?:n}`: own station only, after inspection; default all remaining work, or a positive integer no larger than remaining. Work accumulates every paid minute; the one owned fitting is installed on the first paid repair minute, retained after interruption. No second fitting is charged.
- `{task:'release'}`: keeper at repaired valve, two active minutes; reserve both own water units at start, consume on completion, restore on interruption. Blind as to remote readiness. Water arrives three minutes after completion.
- `{task:'attend',minutes:n}`: receiver at dock, positive integer interval. Can attend before repair. Arrival succeeds only with repaired inlet and an uninterrupted paid attending interval covering the arrival endpoint.
- `{task:'cart'}`: receiver at dock with own cart water. Ten active minutes: five outbound, delivery at minute5, five return. Supply reserved at start, consumed at delivery; cart delivery possible through the launch endpoint. Receiver is away during the trip. Interrupted outbound work preserves carried supply and position; interrupted return never restores delivered supply.
- `{task:'travel',to:'valve'|'dock'}`: physical distance to station, one active minute per segment. Stations six minutes apart; cart destination five beyond dock. Stops retain position; return travel pays remaining distance. There is no teleport on stop. Both actors may travel. Work at a role's own station stays role-restricted.
- `{task:'rest',minutes:n}`, `{task:'meal'}`: paid rest or two-minute consumption of the one owned meal; interruption restores uneaten meal. Idle time pays active maintenance, not recovery.
- `{task:'propose',terms:{releaseAt,attendFrom,attendUntil},proposalId?:id,revision?:n,via?:'radio'|'contact'}`: records a local proposal and begins its one-minute transmission. New IDs are sender-local `keeper:p1` / `receiver:p1`, revision1; revisions increment that proposal's previous locally authored revision. Terms are integer minutes0..30, releaseAt<=28, attendFrom<attendUntil. Interrupted transmission retains the local draft without sending it.
- `{task:'decide',proposalId,revision,decision:'accept'|'refuse'|'withdraw'}`: immediate own contribution decision about an exact locally known revision. Accept may concern an own proposal. At most one accepted own contribution at once; explicitly withdraw before changing it. Neither acceptance nor withdrawal starts/stops physical work. Withdrawal does not recall a sent response. No transmission or radio charge is included.
- `{task:'transmit',message,via?:'radio'|'contact'}`: one paid active minute. `message` is `{kind:'report',observationIds:[ownReceiptNumbers...]}`, `{kind:'proposal',proposalId,revision}`, `{kind:'response',proposalId,revision,decision}`, or `{kind:'confirm',messageId}`. Reports carry exact known observation records and their original source time; responses require a matching own decision; confirmation names an actually received peer message. Sending a response snapshots the own decision at request time; a later withdrawal does not rewrite in-flight content.

Radio reserves one of four owned charges; completion consumes it and queues an envelope. Interruption restores charge and sends nothing. Contact transmits for one active minute without charge; both must be at the same station at start and finish, otherwise paid contact completes without delivery. Passive radio reception happens during any job and never stops it: this is not attention. Station arrival exposes local physical facts; direct contact messages use the same notebook/consent semantics as radio.

## State returned to actors

View fields: `actorId`, `now`, `horizon` (always30), `ended` (only now30), `channel` (mode/minDelay/maxDelay/lossPossible), `body` (own Human view), `position`, `location`, `inventory` (own available/reserved/consumed supplies), `job` (own task/start/end/elapsed), `local` (observed local station facts), `notebook` (own numbered observations), `inbox` (actually received envelopes), `sent` (completed outgoing envelopes with no outcome/due time), `proposals` (exact known revisions), `contributions` (own decisions/actual promised-work status), `paid`, `lastResult`, `budget` (own admission/stop/withdraw room), `nextBoundary` (known local boundary). No scenario label, hidden repair fact, remote current state, world result, global event/message IDs, remote journal counter or predicted unobserved arrival leaks.

Notebook records have actor-local increasing `receipt`, `cue`, `value`, `source`, `observedAt`, `receivedAt`, `via`. Reports preserve cue/source/source time. `latest` is derived by highest observation time per source/cue, with receipt order as tie-break; older arrivals remain in notebook. Envelope `messageId` is sender-local `keeper:m1` / `receiver:m1`; receiving appends own `receipt`. Outgoing records never acquire delivery truth. Own contribution, sent response, received response, acknowledgment and physical task completion remain separate fields.

Known boundary is `{at,kind}` and uses only own job endpoint, known local launch notice, and public horizon. It never consults inbound queues, hidden launch or remote work. `advance(state,target,{actorId,stopOnReceipt:true})` pays actual elapsed integer minutes and may then stop at the first newly observable notebook/inbox receipt for that actor; it does not return a future receipt forecast. Without that option, advance pays through target (clamped30). No actor policy acts within an advance, so dispatchers needing intervening decisions use one-minute steps or their own known boundaries. A remote private launch closes physical service without ending or otherwise changing a remote actor's view.

## Physics and simultaneous order

Effort is per elapsed minute: repair .012, release .015, attending .006, cart/travel .010, inspect/transmit .003; each whole requested attempt is assessed by actual Human capacity before admission. All attempts, including idle, advance the same person's time/body. Repair practices `repair`, travel/cart practices `carry`. Paid rests/meals use Human recovery with no reset.

At each integer boundary: pay both actors' preceding interval and repair/install progress; finish jobs and cart outbound delivery; settle water arrivals using the preceding interval's attending coverage (end-inclusive, start-exclusive); deliver radio/contact envelopes; observe accessible station facts; close launch after same-minute successful deliveries; update actual contribution fulfillment/expiry; close episode30 and interrupt remaining jobs. Thus attendance ending exactly at arrival succeeds, attendance requested at that timestamp is too late; launch endpoint deliveries count. Pipe/carts serve at most two total units; excess and all late/unattended pipe units are counted separately. Pipe releases consume2 and generate2 arrivals; cart consumes at most1. Delivered+lost+excess+in-transit+available+reserved always reconcile to initial3 water units.

Receiver acceptance promises its own paid `attend` coverage for every minute in `(attendFrom,attendUntil]`; keeper acceptance promises its own release started exactly `releaseAt` and completed `releaseAt+2`. Both require acceptance no later than promised work start. Actual fulfillment does not depend on messages. Stopping preserves exposure/progress and does not silently withdraw consent. Withdrawal ends the own promise immediately and preserves all prior physical work/fulfilled history. Unfulfilled promises expire at their work endpoint; no forced work follows.

## Journal and replay

Strict plain JSON setup/actions/saves only; unknown fields/accessors/nonfinite values fail. Save includes source setup, complete commands and exact state; restore reruns commands from setup and requires canonical structural equality. No trusted receipt injection. Request refusal throws a stable local error and leaves input unchanged; no hidden-state-dependent errors for actor commands.

Each actor has128 decision/control entries. Admission reserves one stop for its current job plus one withdrawal for its active contribution; those controls remain legal when ordinary requests close. Advances have a separate fixed30-entry reserve because time is integer and finite; consecutive advances coalesce only when their local-stop options agree. Thus no other actor's activity can consume the local decision budget, and the combined journal remains bounded by286 entries. Unilateral controls cannot manufacture new commitments. Export/restore checks strict replay, paid Human intervals and reservations; ordinary actor views expose only own budget.

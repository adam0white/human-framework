## The grand goal

Build a reusable, inspectable framework for how an embodied person notices a situation, understands it through experience and knowledge, encounters motives and obligations, chooses, acts, and develops through what follows. The same underlying person should carry condition, acquired capabilities, purposes, relationships and consequential history between situations and across time. A host supplies its world, opportunities and task outcomes; it should not need to invent a different human model for each setting.

The ambition includes development and aging, cognition, affect, self-regulation, relationships, moral and spiritual life, and the environment and institutions that shape opportunity. These are coverage responsibilities with explicit implementation and evidence status, not a compulsory set of numeric meters. Games are optional consumers and experiments. The [original proposal](framework-proposal.md) and [recovered user direction](../research/prior-work-assessment.md) preserve the broader lineage; inherited LLM equations and names are not mandatory architecture.

The framework is grounded in Islam with a Sunni Hanafi–Maturidi starting point and informed by empirical research. Keep revealed sources, attributed interpretations, empirical findings, explanatory models and engineering conventions distinct. Preserve supplied choice, intention, capacity, action and consequence as different things. Positive representation of worship, service, reflection and repair belongs in the research program alongside the boundary against computing divine acceptance or assigning quantities to ruh. [Foundations](../research/islamic-foundations.md).

## How close we are

The narrow body/action/time kit is technically usable and has substantial lifecycle and external-installation evidence. The broader developing-person model is early. Human predictive accuracy and measured developer usefulness remain preliminary or unestablished for most of the ambition. No overall completion percentage or calendar promise is supported.

| Capability | Executable scope now | Important missing scope |
|---|---|---|
| Execution and time | Shared paid attempts, interruption, recovery effects, persistence and integer event clock | Consistent long-horizon and mixed-resolution simulation; broader integration and scale evidence |
| Bodily life | Shared fatigue/hunger/capacity and perceived-condition proxies | Sleep/wake, hydration, pain, injury, illness, sensory and motor limits; calibrated physiology |
| Learning and knowledge | Shared task practice; hosts apply skill to work; private empirical pilot | Knowledge acquisition, instruction, delayed retention, supported transfer, differing learning trajectories |
| Perception and cognition | Perceived body projection; host-local observations, factual reports and notebooks; private bounded memory candidate | Portable beliefs/knowledge, attention, working/episodic/semantic memory, inference, planning and metacognition |
| Affect, motives and development | Authored host priorities and narrow commitments | Appraisal and emotion regulation, persistent changing purposes, habits, self-regulation and character development |
| Relationships | Host-specific requests, consent, loans, promises, revision and withdrawal | Continuing care/attachment, evidence-based trust, misunderstanding, deception, conflict, reconciliation and networks |
| Moral and spiritual life | Documented source/agency boundaries and limited intention records | Reviewed positive representations of understood duty, worship, reflection, repentance, repair and life purpose |
| Lifespan | Elapsed component minutes only; no age model | Childhood, maturation, adult aging, health trajectories, parentage, reproduction, inheritance and death |

In particular, automatic recovery policy, durable work, agreements and actor-local reports are not general faculties exported by the kit. Passing software tests does not establish explanatory adequacy. The [learning pilot](learning-pilot-results.md) earned no universal learning curve or per-minute calibration.

## Proposed stages and decision checks

These stages express dependencies and candidate outcomes, not an approved waterfall. Keep social context present from the first sustained-person case. Empirical and qualified interpretive work can proceed in parallel with engineering. Introduce candidate mechanisms separately enough to identify their contributions; do not activate an entire faculty inventory at once.

| Stage | Candidate deliverable | What must be resolved or demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Define the first fuller-person milestone | A small set of headless reference situations, a population/horizon, and a versioned account of persistent person state and host responsibilities | Select three anchor situations; state what the same person must carry between them, what each actor can know, and which differences matter. A few adults over days/weeks is the recommended default, still unselected. |
| 2. Make life across days meaningful | A bounded sleep/wake and repeated-day recovery candidate, with continuing work, learning and obligations | Separate temporary condition from lasting change. Compare a simpler recovery model. Check detailed versus accelerated time against declared tolerances, including interruptions and resource/commitment continuity. Sleep is first only if the chosen context warrants it. |
| 3. Develop knowledge, purposes and choice | Separate experiments in experienced-fact retrieval, knowledge prerequisites, continuing goals, short planning, then habit and revision | Same current body/skill but different acquired knowledge or remembered experience should yield traceable differences where predicted. Compare notebook, direct-rule and simple-policy controls with equivalent information and cost. |
| 4. Deepen relationships and inner life alongside stages 2–3 | Continuing interaction histories, care, obligation, trust/misunderstanding, and selected moral/spiritual situations | Distinguish actual events, each person's knowledge, intention, understood duties and observers' interpretations. Review normative mappings with qualified scholarship. Measure relevant behavior or representational fidelity; productivity is not the universal objective. |
| 5. Extend into adult development and aging | One defined trajectory over months/years with explicit history and evidence; later childhood/family/inheritance candidates when needed | Separate age-related change from practice, health, current fatigue, cohort and opportunity. Compare longitudinal outcomes where suitable data exist. A life-course boundary and evidence search can begin earlier. |
| 6. Broaden reuse and social scale | Independent consumers and, when required, household/institution/network mechanisms | Demonstrate useful cross-context transfer without host-specific core rewrites, and declare scale/approximation budgets. Institutions need their own causal rules and outcome tests; actor count alone is insufficient. |

Independent consumption is a recurring check whenever a mechanism is proposed for shared export, not a gate postponed until stage 6. Local source-byte reduction can support a maintenance question; it is not by itself proof of human authoring benefit. Existing direct implementations remain serious alternatives.

## Aging and development are explicit open work

Chronological age is a time/history input, not a complete mechanism. Do not stretch the current minute-based workday coefficients across decades or add a universal age penalty. Separate temporary condition, learned skill/knowledge, relatively persistent differences, health and developmental change. Declare the particular outcome, population, time scale and uncertainty a candidate addresses.

Adult aging does not require a genome, reproduction or childhood model first. Childhood needs its own developmental account rather than an adult initialized with smaller numbers. Inheritance and nurture remain open interacting domains; population heritability must not become an individual's genetic share or fixed potential cap. Task-specific retention/development can return without adopting universal forgetting or lifetime limits. [Deferred domains and rejected formulations](../research/decision-status.md).

## What each new mechanism needs

Choose the intended value before selecting a model: a useful practical capability, a discriminating empirical prediction, or an honest representation of the project's normative and human concerns. A mechanism for care, principled sacrifice, grief or aging need not make a worker more productive. It must still have a declared scope, causal consequences and meaningful alternative.

Before execution, specify the state owner, permitted observations, units/time scale, update rule, intervention and comparison conditions, computational cost, and evidence that would count against the proposal. Keep software correctness, developer usefulness, empirical fit and interpretive fidelity separate. Source-linked human data are needed for human predictive claims; real developer observation is needed for human authoring claims; qualified review is needed for substantive interpretive claims. One user's experience and AI reviews can inform development without replacing those evidence types.

Retain cheaper sufficient rules and negative results. Do not demand victory over a deliberately weak rival, but do not make every new domain pass a short work-scheduling puzzle either. The [validation program](validation-program.md) and [architecture alternatives](../research/architecture-alternatives.md) supply candidate tests, not executed certification.

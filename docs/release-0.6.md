Delivered 2026-09-08 at [human.adamwhite.work/games/](https://human.adamwhite.work/games/). Seven games now accompany the laboratory. The user authorized autonomous development, parallel Astra Ultra tracks, overlapping adversarial review and hourly continuation; the next queue lives in [the handoff](../HANDOFF.md) and [roadmap](roadmap.md).

## Playable additions

- **[Before the rain](https://human.adamwhite.work/commons-next/):** an established camp has one afternoon to pack supplies. A cache can equip a household before the ferry or cover two wet nights at camp. The finite wrapper preserves Common Ground's original world and the continuing `/commons/` control. [Game and six-run comparison](commons-next.md).
- **[Before the Water](https://human.adamwhite.work/watch/):** two people share scarce owned parts and a deadline. Repair the gate to keep service, or prepare/open a diversion. Paid lookout, refusal, transfers, partial work and recovery change feasible schedules. It uses the combined Human/runtime 0.1.1 boundary without changing the earlier headless consumer. [Game, routes and scope](watch-game.md).

Review found better schedules without changing the rules: interrupting a rest after three paid minutes allows the short-notice gate repair to finish at 21 before the surge at 22. In the rain chapter, visible event-boundary coordination after 14 paid rest minutes produces caches at 40 and 88, before the ferry at 90; the nearby full-rest route misses that second delivery. Both exact routes were played through actual browser controls locally and in production. [Two-cache probe](commons-next-two-cache-probe.md). These are scheduling capabilities, not proof that people will discover or enjoy them.

The original six rain runs preserve a dominated allocation and a recovery/food-first route that misses the ferry despite matching three total caches. Watch preserves its slower route's failure and a costly repair-to-diversion pivot. Neither episode claims a deep campaign or downstream value for every remaining resource. No arbitrary efficiency penalty or output bonus was added to force a preferred policy to win.

## Framework decisions

The [preregistered mechanism comparison](mechanism-comparison.md) finds the smaller pooled-stamina/linear-practice model within declared tolerance in **9/21 paired protocols** (7/12 development, 2/9 reserved). Balanced and long mixed work preserve useful simpler cases; separate food/rest bottlenecks create predictable disagreements. Linear versus saturating practice is a curve comparison, not evidence that the latter requires more state. All frozen results, sensitivities, accounting, source ranges and desktop timings are retained. No human authoring benefit or physiological calibration was measured.

The optional [observation-memory candidate](observation-memory-probe.md) stores only an owner's delivered reports with provenance, bounded retention, monotonic receipts and validated state. In fourteen fixed software cases, it answers 10 correctly, a simpler notebook 12, and no retention 8. A 10,000-report run demonstrates bounded storage, not a cognitive superiority claim. The candidate remains private and unexported; the earlier social record also remains experimental.

Human/runtime 0.1.0 and 0.1.1, clock 0.1.0, model dependencies, source-release locks, laboratory 0.3.0, original games and Solo Repair remain unchanged. Only app delivery advances to 0.6.0. There is no new general cognitive, physiological or religious evaluator in the public runtime.

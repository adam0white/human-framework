This retest is the second iteration after R2. By the playtest rule, the game is finished. The remaining asks go to the [Deferred list](voice.md#deferred) with dates.

Dispositions: 5 keep, 7 mixed, 4 quit.

### Blocking-grade asks, deferred by the two-iteration rule

1. **"Say it again" is shown when the option is not offered, and "stands until 15:45" is contradicted when the plan ends earlier.**
   - Pavel: "On the phone the window had closed, and the page still said "Say it again"."
   - Emre: "On the phone it said the plan stood until 15:45 and it ended at 13:15."
   - Leila: "The sheet also promises "say it again when he wakes", but on the phone at 15:00 no shift option was offered."
   - Proposed fix: offer "say it again" only when the option is in his list, and write the end time from the actual end.
2. **The clock crawls, Skip does nothing visible, and a stale or doubled "Continue" holds the clock.** Ten testers raised this, including Maya, Nia, Diego, Kwame, Samir, Jonas and Elena.
   - Samir: "Merge the two stacked "Continue" buttons into one, and stop the clock crawling while a "Continue" notice is on screen."
   - Kwame: "Make Skip visibly do something or say why it is off."
   - Proposed fix: show one Continue at a time, and let an unread notice stop holding the clock. Skip jumps to his next choice, or says why it cannot.
3. **Speak is missing at the moment he chooses.**
   - Kwame: "At 10:00 on my laptop it was replaced by "Let time pass" and he picked rest without me."
   - Mei: "Keep the Speak button available at every stop, and say why the clock stopped when I cannot act (laptop 12:45 and 14:19)."
   - Proposed fix: every decision stop offers Speak, or names why it does not.
4. **Contradictions in the text.**
   - Elena: ""I will not do that" at 05:08, then "He did what you suggested: pray at the mosque" at 05:14, when he was asleep."
   - Leila: "Desktop day 1 showed 3 went well and 1 badly, yet "listens less closely"."
   - Emre: "Money stayed at 62 after the long shift on the desktop, and I could not find the pay."
   - Proposed fix: credit a suggestion only when that act happened. Write the verdict from the same tally. Show shift pay on the day card.

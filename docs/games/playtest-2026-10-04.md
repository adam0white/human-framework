## Direction for Game 3 and later games

> I'm glad Islamic truth guides our framework, but games don't have to focus on that part and instead keep that gentle.

> It should probably be even longer than G2 and potentially infinite play (until the characters die of natural causes? I dunno). We could see longer time effects of our framework. Though it should have more UI than just text, and showcase some stuff happening, yet similarly deeply integrate the human framework like the existing games.

> Also, for playtesting purposes, do you think the end game should offer download json or something so you can check things better and even replicate my play if needed?

> I love that a player's read of the game state and character stats stay as approximates based on known information; the progressive/subjective UI should be part of that. There are many good decisions that's driving the already enjoyable demos we have. Maybe G3 is where I go with a public demo?

> Since taste is what matters on games and LLM output doesn't come with that by default, you should get reviews, adversarial, unbiased, wild carded reviews; and ultimately try to get more words of our play testers like me and cling onto them.

> I like realistic, game-driven approach to game elements in games; such as having tactical slowdowns instead of pauses like tactical RTS, game speed like grand strategy games instead of outright turn based games, actual capabilities that can potentially but barely change like AP per turn instead of arbitrary caps on numbers, UI showing what's happening instead of numbers like showing ammo left or making you feel it instead of numbers for that, hp, and whatnot, and even the save mechanics and the menu ui being built on top of these. You get my point, but these are ambitions, potentially stretch goals and more about the games not necessarily the framework.

> We can use mobile best practices, I'm no expert but I've seen stuff such as full screening a game and capturing input(?). We shouldn't spend most of our time on getting an action game ready UI, though.

> Research S26 viewport before assuming 360x740 everywhere.

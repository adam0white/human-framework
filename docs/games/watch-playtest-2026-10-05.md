## His exported runs (analysed 2026-10-05)

Adam exported the same village (seed 20261004) at the end of years 2, 3 and 4. Each log extends the one before, so the year-4 file (272 inputs) stands for all three; it is kept as the test fixture `apps/site/test/fixtures/watch-owner-2026-10-05.json`. It replays exactly on d48d8f6, the build he played.

- **What he did.** 69 lantern moves, 44 talks, 43 postings (25 asked, 18 urged, none insisted), 30 card answers, 21 bell pulls, 10 fair picks (seed, heir, bell and wall in year 1; outsiders, rebuild and wall in year 2; seed, outsiders and wall in year 3). On year 1's fifth night he took every watcher off their post; they stood anyway, and the two nights lost 0 and 3 sacks.
- **How it went.** Grain lost per winter 5, 8 and 13; harvests 28, 24 and 23; no hungry spring. The mill wall fell at the year-2 thaw and was rebuilt that autumn; Tamar was lamed in year 3. Nobody died or left: 20 people by year 4, with two births and nine arrivals. Trust in the Keeper rose for the people he urged and talked with (Mara 0.58 to 0.69, Joss 0.62 to 0.71) and stayed near 0.4 for newcomers. Going home to sleep emptied most posts (98 times over the run).
- **Did his choices matter?** Yes, measurably, though one run cannot show it. Replayed from his log with a policy changed: urge turned to ask lost 50 sacks and had a hungry spring with four people leaving; no bell lost 45; no talks 42; no input at all 64, with the west wall lost in year 1. His own play lost 28, better than every variant. Dropping a single input moved the loss anywhere from 28 to 51, because every later spawn shifts; that is why "the HF is wearing off of me and looks more like randomness" (deferred: separate RNG streams, watch.md §12).
- **Bugs his run showed, now fixed.** A lamed keeper kept her post; Volume II asked again who keeps the Gate after Tamar; people who went home said they "didn't blink till dawn"; newcomer lines outlived the first winter; up to nine people mended a nearly whole rope; epilogue lines repeated (docs/findings.md, 2026-10-05).

# The Night Watch lab-panel round, 2026-10-27

- **Build tested:** live https://human.adamwhite.work/watch/ at central `cab6c9e`, opened with `?labRunSource=agent`.
  - The analyst read `/release.json` after the round: `cab6c9e`, framework 3.2.0, `dirty:false`.
  - No session recorded the commit. Each `driver.out` and `wall-clock.jsonl` holds only the driver version (`0442587`). That is a Ruling 1 gap. Nothing in the reports suggests a second build.
- **Previous round:** the one-tester same-tester retest of 2026-10-27 (`docs/games/watch-playtest-2026-10-27-retest.md`). This is Night Watch's first lab-panel round, and it replaces that retest.
- **Testers:** 14 Claude Sonnet persona agents, all on a first visit to this game.
  - The 12 standing panel personas: Maya, Emre, Nia, Rosa, Jonas, Aisha, Diego, Mei, Samir, Tessa, Pavel and Leila. None had played Night Watch. Each read only its own diary from other games.
  - Two cold players: Anika Bose and Erik Lind. Each was cold only in the first session; the second session came after the first.
- **Method:** the harness lab driver. Each tester played two fresh sessions on the same evening, 360x690 phone and 1280x800 desktop, both assigned light mode. The floor was 0, the ceiling 30 minutes, and testers stopped freely. Recording stayed on, and runs are labelled as agents.
- **This is a fictional lab panel.** It is not evidence about recruited players or a population.
- **Caveats:**
  - Tap speed. The driver's own time per operation (`op_ms`) was 109 to 407 ms across all 28 sessions (median 154 ms). The time between one tap and the next was a median of 3.2 s; that is the persona's thinking time, not the driver. The round had one 30 s locator timeout (Emre, desktop, "Back to the village", 622 s).
  - Dark page. Maya, Tessa and Erik said the page looked dark although light mode was assigned. The screenshots `tessa/desktop/b-001.png` and `maya/phone/a-007.png` are dark. It is not verified whether Night Watch has a light theme at all.
  - Own history. Emre read 607 of 876 lines of his diary and Nia 320 of 887. The Read tool's line cap may have cut others too. This matters little, because no diary held this game.
  - Seven sessions end with a driver `ceiling` row at 1800 s. In each, the tester had already logged a voluntary stop. They are not forced stops.
  - Maya's phone stop is at 434.6 s in the jsonl. Her report estimates about 700 s.
  - Every report was saved by the coordinator from the agent's final reply, because the subagent's Write was refused.
  - Some stop reasons are mislabelled by the testers themselves (Nia FINISHED and BUDGET, Pavel BUDGET). The words below come from the full stop and return sentences.

## Verdict: needs a fix cycle

A **new blocking ask** has no earlier F-number and is tied to a quit or a conditional ("mixed") return sentence. "Tied to" means the ask is named in that tester's return condition or stop words.

- Dispositions: 0 keep, 11 mixed, 3 quit. No stop was caused by a driver limit.
- Ask 1 alone is enough for a fix cycle. It is confirmed in six sessions' jsonl and named in five mixed returns and one quit stop.

### New blocking asks

1. **F-1592. "On to dusk" does nothing while the refuge visit is open, and says nothing.**
   - Maya: "On to dusk silently did nothing three times while a refuge choice was still open; it worked only after I chose Stay. No message told me why."
   - Nia: "Yes, on my phone, another evening, if I can see what happens to Meryem and Samira, and only if the "On to dusk" button stops dying silently."
   - Aisha: "I lost trust when On to dusk silently did nothing after I chose the refuge visit".
   - Samir: "On to dusk does nothing now. The text says Save Kian or stay together but there is no Save control; I am stuck at dawn."
   - Tessa: "On to dusk does nothing and tells me nothing."
   - Rosa (quit) met it too: ""On to dusk" did nothing on the phone until I chose "Stay here together". No message said why."
   - **Confirmed in the jsonl (Ruling 8 checked).** In maya/phone, nia/desktop, samir/phone, tessa/desktop, aisha/phone and rosa/phone, every "On to dusk" tap has `error: null` and `requested_control_present_before: true`. The driver tapped a present, enabled control, and the game did not move on. In every case "Stay here together" released it, and the next "On to dusk" worked.
   - Confirmed: Rosa was blocked after only "Ask Kian and Ada about visiting the refuge together", before any choice under it. So the block starts when the agreement opens.
   - Confirmed: `maya/phone/a-007.png` was taken after Maya tapped "Spend daylight caring for Ada and prepare a quiet departure". It still shows both options and an orange "On to dusk" that looks enabled. The choice gave no visible answer.
   - **Proposed fix:** while the visit is open, disable "On to dusk" and say beside it what it waits for, or let "On to dusk" carry the visit forward.
2. **F-1593. The refuge text is not plain English, and its two buttons run together.**
   - Rosa (quit): "the morning pages hold too many ideas (refuge, ward, quiet action boundary) and I had four rereads."
   - Erik (quit), in his return sentence: "I'd also want the refuge text rewritten in plain words."
   - Mei: "I do not follow "Save Kian at a quiet action boundary". Does Save mean rescue, or save the game?"
   - Tessa: "It keeps telling me to Save Kian at a quiet action boundary, and a quiet departure snapshot is ready."
   - Leila: "the options Spend daylight caring for Ada and prepare a quiet departure vs Stay here together are glued into one string."
   - Samir, Aisha, Anika, Pavel and Nia said the same about "Save".
   - Confirmed: Pavel's driver log holds the copied name "Spend daylight caring for Ada and prepare a quiet departureStay here together". Erik saw the same string in the page text and in `erik/desktop/b-005.png`.
   - Assumed, not confirmed: "Save" means saving the game at a quiet moment. No tester found out.

3. **F-1594. Dusk warns only about the scout's wall, and raids hit the walls nobody watched.**

## Claim and observation boundary

For an actor in two legal runs with the same accessible local history, the complete detached actor view, known-boundary forecast and result/error of the same local command must be equal. Equality includes local IDs, own budget, capacity metadata, refusal text, outgoing records and contribution status; it is stronger than selecting a few notebook fields. Research setup/world summary/full save are intentionally privileged and are never decision inputs.

Histories may legitimately diverge upon local inspection, station arrival/contact, a delivered passive radio receipt or a locally visible physical result. These tests do not suppress such evidence. In particular, advancing to an actual receipt after paying elapsed time may stop at different minutes in different worlds. Returning a hidden incoming time before advancing is a leak. The keeper's common episode horizon remains30 even when the remote launch privately closes at15 rather than27.

Channel mode and its possible bounds are common information. Paired channel worlds use the same declared mode and different permitted private outcomes. Reliable known bounds support inference; this is a finite authored information-flow test, not an unreliable-channel impossibility result or a model of human knowledge.

## Predeclared checks

1. **Private launch twins.** Pair launch15/27 with identical keeper local commands. Verify full keeper views, boundaries and command outcomes before any report/contact, through both hidden departure times and the common horizon30. The receiver's differing local notice is expected and is not pooled into keeper input.
2. **Private peer work/body twins.** Pair inlet2/14, legal receiver body differences and independently paid receiver inspection/repair/rest histories. With no contact or message, keeper views and action/refusal results remain equal. Reverse the observer and vary upstream repair/body/work as a second direction.
3. **Private channel twins.** In common bounded/lossy modes, change permitted actual delay/loss for a receiver transmission. Compare keeper views and known-boundary forecasts before any possible receipt. Repeat with unrelated peer transmissions to check sender-local message/proposal/observation IDs and source-bound channel outcomes. Do not treat the first actual receipt as an isolation failure.
4. **Elapsed local stopping.** A local driver may stop on the first new observed inbox/notebook receipt after real paid time. Test a keeper's ongoing long repair: passive reception preserves its work and paid progress. Before receipt, the known boundary must expose only the own job endpoint/local notice/common horizon, never the hidden arrival.
5. **Claims and consent.** Local proposal authoring, own decision, paid response transmission, peer receipt and physical promised work are distinct. A local accept/withdraw does not send a response, modify the peer's known contribution, or execute work. Lost/late responses remain attributed claims, and a newer local revision cannot be overwritten by an old response.
6. **Source-time order and provenance.** Send an earlier observation slowly and a later observation quickly using legal inspection/repair/report actions. The late old arrival remains inspectable but cannot replace the higher source-time claim. Sending reports or confirmations for inaccessible observation/message IDs must refuse without changing owned charges or state. Delayed content retains its original source time.
7. **Paid ownership and exact replay.** Save/restore after draft, partial transmission, completed send, delayed receipt, acceptance and withdrawal. Interruption refunds only unconsumed reserved charge and retains actual paid exposure; no interrupted envelope is delivered. Compare local-stop, one-minute and world-target drivers by final physical/public state while separately checking each exact journal replay.
8. **Unilateral control at the cap.** Use legal repeated zero-time request/stop prefixes to exercise each actor's own budget and independent peer activity. Every admitted local command must retain the actor's own stop/withdraw controls and horizon continuation. Interleave paid minutes and actor controls. The peer's budget cannot leak into the observer's view or block its commands. Preserve cap counterexamples instead of asserting closure alone.

No test will obtain gameplay states by patching private bodies, work, notebook entries, queues or counters. Twin differences come only from valid constructor inputs and legal command histories. Malformed-save mutations, if tested, are explicitly validation attacks rather than gameplay evidence. Abstract equality expectations are registered here; exact fixture syntax follows the committed host API and will be recorded alongside executed tests.

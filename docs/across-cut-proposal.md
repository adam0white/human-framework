# Proposal: Across the Cut

**Status:** selected next-stage feasibility study after app 0.9, 2026-09-08; no implementation, outcomes, player feedback or promotion. Begin with the headless gate, not a public page. All timings below are proposed game rules awaiting actual paid execution. The user’s existing autonomous authorization covers this work; no additional approval pause is required.

## Decision and alternatives

Build one new, short game with an upstream valve keeper and a downstream receiver, separated by a bend and embankment. The player controls the keeper; the receiver runs an exposed, replaceable policy using only its own experience. The question is: **when does paying to learn that another person received a particular proposal change worthwhile work?**

Two feasible approaches:

1. **Dedicated fixed-station slice, recommended.** Direct actor-owned notebooks, a tiny delayed-message transport and explicit work contributions. This isolates information and receipt effects without inheriting an omniscient host.
2. **A separate two-actor Last Light descendant.** Give the remote keeper its own paid berth-clearing work and independent departure/holding choices. This offers familiar routes and radio UX, but route fallback, moving visibility and existing launch deadlines make attribution harder. Preserve original Last Light either way.

Do not retrofit ignorance into co-located Shared Promise. Its visible-pump and handover rivals already explain useful successes. Do not import the observation-memory candidate as the premise of this milestone.

## Playable situation and provisional budget

Supply two water drums to a departing market launch during a 30-minute episode. Pipe delivery fills both; the downstream handcart can fill one. At the dock, the receiver sees whether this launch leaves at 15 or 27; the upstream keeper cannot see that notice. Report these concrete services separately from water wasted, time, recovery and messages; do not collapse them into a tuned reward score.

The keeper owns two water units and the valve part. The receiver owns an inlet fitting and a one-unit fallback cart supply. Ownership never moves through a message. Pipe release consumes the keeper's two units; they arrive three minutes after a two-minute release attempt finishes. The receiver must have repaired the inlet and be actively attending its hatch when water arrives. An unattended arrival is lost. Attending is ordinary paid active work, not recovery.

Local inspection takes one minute. It reveals valve work of six or twelve minutes upstream, or inlet work of two or fourteen downstream. Both sites must finish their actual work; a slow inlet plus an early launch may make full service infeasible. Those local facts create legitimate refusal and fallback cases. Use carried Human bodies, declared effort and actual paid rest/food; a forecast does not grant capacity. A four-minute receiving stint competes with a ten-minute cart trip, during which the receiver is away. Permit longer/repeated attending, blind release, immediate cart and fixed schedules as real strategies. One delivery consumes the corresponding finite supply; there are no quotas or regenerated resources.

Each station has four radio charges. Every report, proposal, response or receipt acknowledgment costs its sender one active minute and one charge, consumed only when transmission completes. Partial work retains paid exposure without an unfinished message. Received transmissions are automatically heard without stopping work: this deliberately models a passive radio, **not attention**. Declare channel mode at start: reliable two-minute delivery; reliable delivery within two to six minutes; or delivery in two/six minutes or loss. Actors know the mode and shared clock, not the hidden per-message outcome. In reliable modes, a competent controller may infer receipt after the known bound. In lossy mode, no acknowledgment received does not distinguish an unreceived proposal from an unreceived response. A sent record never directly reveals delivery success.

Fixed station roles are an authored scenario constraint, not a claim that communication is the only conceivable solution. The initial preflight must also price a feasible six-minute walk each way as a direct messenger/rendezvous alternative; if physical contact removes the interesting decision, redesign or reject the slice before UI work.

## What each person can know

Common at start: objective, clock, travel/transmission rules, their pre-agreed ordinary fallback and possible job durations. Each person sees their own body, owned objects, local inspection/completion facts, jobs, sent messages and actually delivered inbox. Neither sees the other person's queue, condition, progress, pending responses, hidden scenario identifier or message scheduler. Remote changes cannot alter local choices, reasons, next-visible-event times or journal text before an accessible receipt. An absent reply means **no reply received**, not refusal, dishonesty or failure.

Keep two independent structures per person:

- **Experienced claims:** cue, value, source, observation time, received time and actor-local receipt. Newer observation time wins for the same source/cue; older arrivals remain inspectable. Conflicting sources remain distinct. These are reports, not certified world truth or calibrated confidence.
- **Own contributions and message status:** exact proposal ID/revision, terms, own accept/refuse/withdraw decision, what was sent, and acknowledgments actually received. Never overwrite a promise with a readiness fact.

Example proposal: “I will release at 13; will you attend from 15 to 19?” Acceptance names that exact revision and commits the receiver's chosen attending work; it does not certify that the valve is already repaired. Its transmission is an acknowledgment of receiving/accepting the proposal. An optional final “received your acceptance” lets the receiver stop paid retransmissions and use that minute for work/recovery; without it, the policy may retry, honor its promise or use a declared timeout. Match a confirmation-only envelope against an equal-cost alternative message: receipt effects must not borrow fresh readiness information. Confirmation never mechanically gates successful reception. Each actor may independently withdraw, paying to communicate that change; lost withdrawal leaves the other actor holding outdated information. Local stopping remains immediate and unilateral.

Ordinary UI shows the keeper's local view and attributed reports. A collapsed researcher view may compare both histories and world truth after the run, never feed policy choices. Show “accepted response received” separately from “receiver attending last reported at…”.

## Small, discriminating experiment

First freeze the information/cost contract, ordering, policies and rejection rules, then run a headless feasibility gate before any page. Independent draft review found that uniform repairs and a universal minute-30 deadline admitted no-radio delivery at 12 plus cart insurance by 24. The local departure/repair alternatives above are a proposed correction, not an executed solution. Price all scripts using actual bodies and preserve the easy no-radio case.

Compare:

1. **Competent reactive rival:** public fixed rendezvous/fallback, sensible recovery, current received messages and no retained remote reports. Include blind pipe and immediate-cart subpolicies.
2. **Serious host notebook:** timestamped reports plus a small per-proposal response table. It may retry, choose conservative deadlines, recover and revise; no artificial capacity/expiry limit.
3. **Acknowledgment ablation:** the same notebook policy with optional final receipt confirmation removed. Give it identical transport and physical possibilities, including timeout/retry; do not make acknowledgment compulsory for successful reception.

The existing private memory candidate is optional *later* engineering comparison only if a concrete notebook obligation remains. It cannot replace the contribution table, and its known delivery-order overwrite should remain unchanged and visible in any comparison.

Predeclare eight development families: timely exchange; long reply; lost acceptance; newer revision followed by old response; local readiness changes before an old report arrives; accepted work omitted/withdrawn; easy fixed-schedule success where messages cost time; direct physical contact. Reserve exact timing/boundary variants until committed source freeze. These are authored cases, not blind human samples.

Use two analyses: identical paid message/action scripts with alternative representations to isolate bookkeeping; separately, full legal policies where different communication and labor are acknowledged interventions. Record both actor-local decision inputs, all attempt payments, resources, sent/delivered envelopes, responses, actual services and complete save continuations. Pair worlds with identical local histories: that actor's decisions and ordinary UI must match until histories diverge. Save/reload between send, arrival, revision and withdrawal; test duplicates, closure ties, interrupted transmission and bounded-journal stop/advance room.

Reject or narrow the milestone if fixed schedule/cart/contact weakly dominates every useful condition; if confirmation never changes a consequential local choice; if its advantage requires forced waiting, telepathy, disabling a legal rival or replacing promised work with truth; or if the notebook suffices but an abstraction adds obligations. Keep useful counterexamples. No human explanation or enjoyment gate passes without actual supplied feedback.

## Root execution note

First make the actor-local information/cost and time-driver contract executable, then compare no-radio, fixed/contact and notebook approaches. Do not expose a hidden future incoming-message time through a next-event label: advancing may stop when a receipt actually becomes observable after paid elapsed time. Keep channel outcomes exogenous and source-bound, and distinguish identical-exposure representation tests from policies that send at different times. A second requested proposal-review process hit the thread limit; one independent draft review completed and exposed the retained no-radio/acknowledgment weaknesses. This is design review, not verified feasibility.

Selected after Camp 0.3.0 maintenance on 2026-09-08. This is the next bounded implementation, not a delivered capability. The framework is the product; the scene is a disposable consumer, with no required chapter order or old-save support.

A real player's interface and a receiver that can accommodate the player's changing timing are still absent. Test the boundary between accessible information, paused presentation and an ongoing paid task. Do not reopen the completed work-helper extraction or add a general memory, planning or negotiation mechanism.

Use a separate fresh host/save/policy version, released runtime 0.1.1 and direct host rules. Keep the frozen private study and its failed confirmation premise unchanged. Commit the selected implementation contract and exact comparison inputs before recording evaluation outcomes.

Select first-hop absolute facts and source/observation/send/receipt timestamps. Render only the keeper's detached actor view and local UI state. Give the receiver a small disclosed policy driven only by its own view. Advance internally in integer minutes; pause presentation on actually received evidence or legitimate local stops without silently canceling or finishing the active job. Retain explicit stop/continue, owned resources, paid recovery/meal/work and a compact mobile/desktop control surface. Start with one scene. A new route or a replacement for an earlier experiment is a delivery choice, not the success metric.

## Discriminating checks

1. Use the preserved minute-eight report during a paid water release: with valve six, inlet fourteen, launch fifteen and delay six, inspecting 0–1, repairing 1–7 and starting release at seven brings the receiver report at eight. Stopping retains two water units and one paid release minute; continuing loses the units. Reproduce both through real controls and through save/resume at receipt. First verify that the selected receiver and source-time rules actually admit this case; preserve a failed reproduction instead of retuning the frozen study.
2. Hidden-world twins must give equal views, DOM/labels, affordances, errors and user-visible stop behavior until accessible evidence or effects differ. A private early departure cannot end the keeper's episode early. Out-of-order delivery selects claims by source time; unsupported relays reject. Current physical state must remain correct after interrupted work/travel and repeated pauses.
3. Compare representative exact-world schedules using competent cart-first no-radio, fixed/conservative and paid-contact alternatives. Re-evaluate the new receiver; old study totals cannot certify it. Report delivered service, water lost/excess, owned inventory, paid work, attendance and radio costs separately. Include a condition where communication loses and keep that outcome.
4. Verify the real controls at phone/desktop sizes and the changed public dependency/privacy boundary. If delivered, follow the usual tested, reviewed, pushed, deployed and exact-live-verified workflow. Browser checks and authored synthetic cases are not human-usefulness evidence.

## Decision and stop rule

After one bounded implementation/evaluation, stop expansion if factual reports create no useful decision or outcome advantage over the competent simpler alternatives. Keep the simpler solution and the failed case; do not add memory, acknowledgments or negotiation to rescue the premise. If the new interface preserves information ownership and exposes a useful consequential choice, deliver that narrow result without promoting a portable cognition module. Human explanation, authoring benefit and empirical calibration remain separately open.

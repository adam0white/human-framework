# Player continuity and interface milestone

Active 2026-09-08 after private Across the Cut completion at `2d788d8`. The user's new direct play reports steer the continuing autonomous roadmap. Existing design, implementation, private commit/push and deployment authorization applies. The public app remains 0.9 until a verified release.

## Evidence and decision

The same player reports that recurring body and world rules make the simple games feel like layers over simulated people. They want to remain with a finished world, see the games as a progressive tutorial, and propose a broadly shared advancing story. They find consequential puzzle choices pleasantly stressful. They also report scroll-heavy interfaces, odd explicit rest commands, an apparent workbench/ongoing-garden restart advantage for Meryem, and misreading the exclusive minute-12 evening-launch deadline. These are distinct observations from one person, not five participants or proof of empirical human fidelity. Preserve exact words in the private feedback record; a repeated message is a duplicate observation.

Choose a common, compact interaction layout as the immediate public improvement. Keep each current game's world, controller and replay identity unchanged in this presentation release. Do not remove diagnostic restart from preserved experiments. Investigate automatic rest and the reported ongoing-work defect in separate versioned work; do not silently rewrite frozen hosts or invent a duration discount that refunds past exposure. Develop a coherent continuing-story design around actual carryover and consequences. Across the Cut's accepted factual-report need remains eligible as a later chapter rather than an obligatory eleventh standalone game.

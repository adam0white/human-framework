# Across the Cut: actor-local information feasibility

Started 2026-09-08 at private main `38deade`; deployed app0.9 remains `cf4270c`. The shared-plan milestone is complete. This stage implements a private headless feasibility study before adding another public game or promoting any cognition package.

## Decision and boundaries

Use `docs/across-cut-proposal.md` as a design candidate, not verified timings. Build a new private two-site host around frozen Human/runtime0.1.1 and clock0.1.0. The keeper and receiver have legitimately different local observations, finite owned supplies and radio charges, paid work/travel/transmission, and distinct own contributions versus received claims. The original no-radio counterexample remains a control. A new general memory/planning/attention faculty is not presumed.

Keep every existing game, public asset, runtime/model/clock source, package export/version and release lock unchanged. New implementation stays under `src/experiments/across-cut/`, not public `src/games/`. No page, gallery card, app-version bump or deployment belongs in this preflight. Private commits/pushes are authorized; preserve the distinct live app source during the final handoff.

## First exact contract

Core owner commits `docs/across-cut-contract.md` before evaluating new-host outcomes and sends the API/cost/ordering contract to policy and isolation lanes. Refine proposed timing/physics openly: record any first infeasible or trivially dominated case before a dated amendment, not after reserved evaluation.

- Two actors share only the common clock and declared possible task/channel rules. Own inspection/body/jobs/inventory and received or co-located observations are accessible; hidden peer facts, global queue/IDs and scenario labels are not.
- Private launch time may be15 or27; the public episode horizon is30. An unobserved remote departure must not end the other actor's episode or leak through their view before local evidence. Report world outcomes separately from actor-local knowledge.
- Specify repair partial-work/part ownership, release reservations/consumption, water arrival and excess/loss accounting, receiving-interval endpoint order, cart occupancy/return/interruption and paid six-minute contact travel. Do not silently disable physical contact or useful alternate work. Use actual carried Human bodies and costs.
- Radio completion costs one active minute and one owned charge. Interrupted transmission has no message/charge completion benefit. Message IDs and observation receipt counters are actor-local. Channel outcomes are exogenous/source-bound, not chosen to favor a controller or shifted accidentally by unrelated transmissions.
- Reports reference local observations/claims and their source times; transmitted claims are not current-world truth. Proposal IDs/revisions, a recipient's own accept/refuse/withdraw decision, sent response, received response and optional confirmation are distinct. A recorded acceptance does not perform work.
- Define when own consent takes effect relative to response transmission and what interruption/withdrawal preserves. Receiving a passive radio message need not interrupt work; explicitly do not call this attention.
- Reliable known-bounded modes permit competent inference after the bound. Lossy missing replies do not prove refusal or dishonesty. An acknowledgment may save paid retries or inform a revision; it cannot gate physical success by fiat.
- A local driver may advance until an actually observable receipt, but cannot reveal a hidden future incoming time before paying elapsed time. Equal actor-local histories must give equal views, affordances, reasons and public next-boundary forecasts, including IDs and capacity metadata.
- Actor decisions remain outside the world transition code or use explicitly replaceable actor-local policies. Journals/saves must preserve strict deterministic replay, simultaneous ordering and unilateral stopping room; reuse the lessons of the corrected shared-plan reserve without modifying frozen code.
